# Console product authority

Status: active product authority.

## Product boundary

Console builds one governed company object system in this order:

1. Ontology / Foundry-style object engine and deterministic policy.
2. Company, OrgUnit, JobPosition, Person, and Employment. People/recruiting directory create mints Person in the same transaction as the employees row (#966; `person_id = employee_id`). `hr.appoint` remains a distinct four-eyes assignment.
3. HR appointment, promotion, and transfer through one canonical assignment writer.
4. Payroll projected from existing payroll truth as PayRun. Draft calculate is admitted (`POST /api/v1/payroll/runs/{id}/calculate` already persists versioned won drafts; `payable` stays false). Korea compliance conclusions, wage-statement legal sign-off, and payment execution remain **HOLD**.
5. A Leptos SSR frontend. ADR-0030 substrate gates are green and `Layer::Ui` is accepted (ADR-0041); first full-depth vertical is payroll execution. GET `/` serves deny-by-omission SSR (`console-payroll-ui` merged by `console-app`; #952). Unauthenticated and MEMBER get the empty shell; `PayrollRunRead` gets OpenAPI `PayrollRunSummary` required fields as `data-run-*` attributes, no won (#959). Those authorized runs are a Leptos `#[island]` (`AuthorizedRuns`, #962) hydrated by committed release WASM at `/pkg` (#964). Shipping screens (organization, HR, payroll) are composed at `/organization`, `/hr`, and `/payroll` (#976); payroll drill-through hrefs and empty-vs-denied SSR landed (#982). Persona real-backend E2E on `/`, `/organization`, `/hr`, and `/payroll` landed (#978). That is the ADR-0025 §4 bar for calling those GET screens shipped. It is not evidence that the screens are reachable: measured 2026-09-08, no browser reaches an authorized screen, because the principal transport is `Authorization: Bearer` only and a navigation cannot carry it — every UI test sets the header as an HTTP client must. ADR-0042 (proposed) states the defect and its options and decides nothing. Live exposure remains **HOLD**.

**Tenant is always a Group.** Every Company has a real `groups` row (Palantir-uniform account shape). A standalone 법인 is a Group of one, minted 1:1 with that Company. A conglomerate is the same Group object with multiple member Companies. A Company belongs to at most one Group (`UNIQUE (org_id)` on `group_memberships`). Group is not a legal person and is not the employer.

**Company is the legal entity and the RLS cell.** Employment, OrgUnit, JobPosition, PayRun, and Company business receipts always carry one `org_id`. Group never arms `app.current_org`. Writes stay one Company. Consolidated reads remain N× `with_org_conn`; empty grant → empty view. Account identity/context and Group topology/capacity/operation records must not become mixed-Company payroll truth; scoped owners for those records are first-vertical constraints to enroll, not current substrate.

**Cross-entity work is policy, not tenancy.** Staff of Company A and Company B may share an office; that does not merge OrgUnits or employments. If policy allows, an actor legally employed at B may operate member-company actions (including another company's PayRun) as a principal authorized on that Company — still that Company's `org_id`, four-eyes, and ledger. It is not one mixed group payroll.

The overlay already exists (migration 0060 `groups` / `group_memberships`, grants `GROUP_ADMIN` / `GROUP_VIEWER` / `GROUP_FINANCE`, fail-closed `group_member_org_ids`, `console-platform-group::consolidated_read`). Every Company has a real Group row and `organizations.group_id` is NOT NULL (#971); remove from a multi-member Group remints a Group-of-one. Group still does not arm `app.current_org`. OrgUnit kinds Site, Department, and Team under one Company are enforced in revision attributes (#975); parent cycles longer than 1 are refused (#981). Operational `sites` / `regions` / `branches` stay the auth spine and are not OrgUnits. It does not add `ObjectKey::Group` in this record. The canonical `ObjectKey` roster stays six until a later reviewed design proves Group identity writes need a port.

Console targets the functional breadth of Foundry, Ontology, Actions and Fusion as one native governed object platform, including data, lineage, collaborative work and operational continuity. This is a product target, not a completed equivalence claim. The existing Postgres + object-type substrate is the first integration and comparison target; storage versioning, concurrency control, ordering and replication remain evidence-based design decisions. A versioned capability-by-capability inventory with acceptance evidence is required before claiming full-suite equivalence. Console does not use Palantir SaaS, does not import or export Palantir files, and does not call Palantir APIs.

**Palantir AIP is the target** for the intelligence layer. That layer is built in the separate Intelligence repository until Intelligence has a named, SHA-bound stable base; cloning it into Console is then authorized. Until that clone lane, AI judgment remains out of Console's tenant product. Console-owned **seams** (fail-closed application port, same `Principal` and ontology actions, no second writer, no Palantir client) are admitted so a later clone can connect without rewriting Console. Owner lock (2026-08-29): those seams include **bind-only** Intelligence HTTP, fail-closed to `127.0.0.1` only, implemented as `GET http://127.0.0.1:<listen-port>/internal/intelligence/bind` on the existing listener (#972). That route is not in published OpenAPI, not shipping UI, and not under `/api/v1`. Unset or non-loopback bind is fail-closed. No chat, no inference text in tenant UX, no HR/payroll write, no autonomous merge. Intelligence is not a tenant application and is not autonomous merge authority. Do not call Palantir APIs.

The first internal implementation vertical is native nonpayable payroll, with Account/current-context, policy, durable source entry, drafts, evidence, review, transfer, historical correction and selected-Company Group-operation dependencies. Account setup and this vertical are internal milestones, not the first client release. The release contract below controls the required scope; existing code and historical shipping claims do not establish integration acceptance or production qualification.

## Product invariants

- Commands are deterministic and revision-aware, with replay-safe receipts and auditable mutations.
- Tenant isolation, deny-by-omission, and nondisclosure apply at every read and write boundary. The tenant **account** is always a Group (standalone Company = Group of one with a real row). The RLS **cell** is always a Company (`org_id` / `app.current_org`). Group never arms that GUC.
- Effective-dated truth uses half-open intervals; history is closed and appended, never overwritten. Generalizing that substrate across core HR (`asOf` / `from` / `to`, slices, corrections, delta; #316) is **admitted direction**, not a claim that every HR object already has an as-of read surface.
- Projected objects have exactly one domain writer. Ontology and adapters do not create alternate write paths.
- Requester and approver are distinct natural persons for `company.*`, `hr.*`, and `payroll.*` approvals, even when their capacities differ. All other kinds — including `organization.*` and `people.*` — hold only the account-level `approver_id <> requested_by` bar. `requires_natural_person_four_eyes` (`backend/crates/governance/domain`) is prefix-scoped and a test asserts the exclusion. Extending the bar to the remaining kinds is **unscheduled, not blocked**: migration 0076 shows only that `users.employee_id` is nullable, and a NULL resolution can fail closed exactly as the enforced kinds already do. Whether unbound accounts actually exist is an open question no census has answered, so the compatibility risk is a hypothesis on HOLD rather than a reason to leave six of thirteen dispatch targets outside the invariant.
- Preflight uses the same authorization, policy, state, revision, and input validation as execute and performs no mutation.
- Legal sources are versioned evidence, not transferable compliance conclusions. Production exposure and compliance claims require separate authority.
- The repository integration and source-release branch is `dev`. Ordered environment mirrors are `dev` → `staging` → `canary` → `production`. Creating or fast-forwarding those names is not production exposure.

## Architecture

The existing Rust backend is reused as verified substrate rather than rewritten wholesale. The product exposes governed company objects and authorized actions:

`Company → OrgUnit(Site/Department/Team/…) → JobPosition → Person/Employment → HR action → PayRun`

The published HTTP contract for those six objects is generated from a typed semantic manifest (`backend/crates/contracts/src/semantic_manifest.json` → composed OpenAPI, generated Rust codecs/validators, generated API docs, and an in-repo TypeScript SDK; #989–#1018). Free-form `CreateObjectTypeDraft` children remain outside that contract. Execute/preflight params are typed onto the thirteen DispatchTarget Inputs (#997). ADR-0031's accepted generator target is landed for that six-object roster; the 2026-08-03 ADR observation that no emitter existed is historical. Palantir/SAP names remain comparative evidence; this record does not clone OData or OSDK. SDK/runtime support must be resolved in the release inventory; Python/Java support and distribution are not established by the existing TypeScript generator. The served OpenAPI document uses OpenAPI 3.1 JSON Schema null unions (#990), JSON error envelopes (#991), top-level bearer security and explicit `security: []` on public operations (#992). Bidirectional request/response conformance is admitted; the body-gate floor is 106 resolved of 291 census with `BODY_UNDECIDABLE_MAX` 175 leftover non-Head bodies (`scripts/check-request-body-contract.mjs`). `info.version` is stamped from the crate version (#1015); tying it to release-please remains unscheduled. Company, OrgUnit, Person, JobPosition, and Employment Head instance and collection GETs are published; OrgUnit→JobPosition reverse collection is published from the existing `list_for_org_unit` port (#1016); Head GET/list `permissions` are `employee_directory_read` from existing authz (#1017); Head FK links bind published GET `operationId`s (#1013). OpenAPI and generated clients remain one writer.

The tenant account is always a Group (standalone = Group of one with a real row). Group is not a node on this legal chain and not an `ObjectKey`. OrgUnit kinds live under one Company; a shared physical office is not a shared OrgUnit. Architecture is Clean Architecture (ADR-0001 as amended by ADR-0045): Entities (`Layer::Domain` + `Layer::Kernel`), Use Cases (`Layer::Application`), Interface Adapters (`Layer::Rest` / `Layer::Worker` controllers and `Layer::Adapter` gateways), Frameworks & Drivers (`Layer::App`, `Layer::Ui`, Axum, sqlx). Dependencies point inward. Rest/Worker must not depend on Domain or Adapter; a `Layer::Rest` crate must have a sibling `*-application` crate. Those two rules are enforced by the layer-boundary gate with a shrink-only ratchet for edges and missing application crates that still exist (including `console-payroll-rest`). Hexagonal “one core plus ports” is not the inner-core rule. Platform `*-rest` crates remain `Layer::Platform` because path classification precedes the `-rest` suffix. The frontend reads real contracts, omits unauthorized data server-side, and contains no client-side business authority. Cargo is the target build system; existing Buck paths remain repository reality until a dedicated, evidence-backed convergence change removes them without losing test coverage. `-ui` members were skipped by first-party Buck generation while Leptos was unvendored; #1079 put the Leptos crates in the third-party graph, so that exclusion is retired and `console-payroll-ui` is generated and built like any other member.

The first client release requires the full supported public Foundry capability inventory, complete Console business workflows, classical model development and operations, and a polished native Leptos experience. The deployment population is one operator-managed Group, six Companies and 2,000 people under South Korean employment/payroll requirements. AIP remains on the separate Intelligence roadmap. The one release inventory is `release_inventory` in [`../program/console-capability-registry.json`](../program/console-capability-registry.json). Freeze supported public Foundry capabilities against 2026-09-19 with retrievable source bytes, digests, capability leaves, connector/runtime/SDK matrices, journeys, dependencies and executable acceptance criteria. Payroll must calculate, obtain independent approval, issue statements, export approved payment instructions, and reconcile externally executed payments. Keep approved obligations and immutable instruction revisions separate from export artifacts, destinations, totals and delivery attempts, external acknowledgements, settlement evidence, reconciliation exceptions, returns and corrections. Export or download never marks wages paid. Future direct payment execution consumes these same owners and instruction contracts and adds delivery attempts; it does not introduce another payroll writer. Implementation of calculation, statement, export and reconciliation workflows is required; legal/compliance conclusions and production/payment execution still need separate qualified authority and evidence.

### Workspace and interaction acceptance

One workspace has labeled Work, People, Communications, Data and Administration navigation groups. Work contains assigned tasks, approvals, deadlines, pending outcomes and recoverable interruptions. People contains recruiting, people, employment, organization, attendance, labor and directly discoverable payroll. Communications retains independent persistent messenger, mail and calendar destinations. Data contains objects, datasets, pipelines, analyses, sheets, documents, applications and models. Administration contains policy, identity, integrations, schemas, resources, packages and operations. Navigation reflects policy; it cannot enforce authorization.

Internal users, applicants, former workers and external participants share an interaction grammar, with available information and actions determined by current policy. Each substantial workflow shows its subject, Company or Group scope, effective period, status and accountable owner; context-prefilled typed inputs and actual object references; evidence, annotations, discrepancies and consequences beside the decision; distinct draft, submitted, pending, committed, rejected, conflicting and uncertain outcomes; persistent URLs, reopening, history and accountable recovery. Sustained work uses full pages. Short actions may use dialogs. Communications preserve business inspection and working context. Narrow layouts preserve input and evidence through accessible navigation; only genuinely tabular surfaces may require horizontal scrolling.

Consolidate Korean-readable typography, spacing, semantic colors, tabular numbers, controls, focus, validation, status and responsive layout into one shared light theme. Use native Leptos components and authorized view models, readable `view!` compositions and demonstrated repeated patterns. Ordinary navigation, content and forms render through SSR; hydrate only browser-state interactions such as selection, collaborative editing and live conversations. Static collections must not ship unnecessary islands. Use normal product routes; do not restore `/_ui`. A universal screen generator or another frontend framework is not part of this design.

### Shared ownership and execution contracts

Every release-reachable entry follows `Leptos / HTTP / worker → application use case → domain rules and application-owned ports → persistence or integration adapter`. The application root composes dependencies; SQL, transport behavior and transitions stay in their responsible layers. Reuse integrated architecture work, close actual App/Platform bypasses, direct lifecycle mutations and source-inclusion workarounds, and verify behavior beyond manifest dependency checks. UI, APIs, imports, automation and operational tools invoke the same canonical writer.

Account identity persists independently of Employment. Group is tenancy; Company is the legal business boundary. Job title, rank, location and organizational hierarchy confer no authority. Cedar is the release authorization target for resources, fields, actions, grants, delegation, schema administration, sharing and operations; this target does not claim current Cedar enforcement is complete. Authentication and current authorization context are constructed server-side. Client identifiers select requested resources only. Navigation, search, previews, SSR, hydration, downloads, subscriptions and exports use authorized projections.

Group-scoped datasets, analyses, applications and model artifacts need explicit owners. They may combine authorized Company sources without becoming mixed-Company payroll truth or bypassing Company policy. Derived resources inherit source restrictions unless a separately authorized release rule permits broader disclosure. Group operations orchestrate Company-owned child commands with individual outcomes and recovery.

Extend existing contracts with stable object/property/relationship/item/resource identities; versioned schemas, effective dates, provenance and exact money/time; durable drafts, checkpoints, comparison, replay and append-only restoration; command identity and expected revisions; shared preflight/execution authorization and validation; durable confirmed/conflicting/known-failed/unknown-outcome receipts; and versioned events and resumable subscriptions that recheck disclosure authority. Preserve compatible API routes through these owners. Serialize generation and migrations. Never reinterpret historical command bytes or reseal historical records for a new codec.

PostgreSQL remains the initial authoritative business substrate. Compare existing revision/transaction mechanisms, narrow sequencing and collaboration algorithms on identical correctness histories and workloads before selecting dataset formats or collaborative protocols. Reject loss of acknowledged edits, policy/attribution failures, duplicate effects, starvation or an exceeded envelope. Select the simplest passing mechanism and record a measured replacement trigger. Stable cell/property identities permit independent edits only while shared invariants hold; conflicts require resolution. Structure edits and formula recalculation use versioned dependencies and deterministic recovery.

User code, notebooks, containers and imported models execute outside the trusted Console process, with scoped data access, controlled egress, isolated resources, cancellation and durable output publication. They never receive authentication or payroll database credentials. Dataset/application/model/package version identities precede the executions they describe.
