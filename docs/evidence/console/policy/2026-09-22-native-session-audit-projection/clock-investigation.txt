## Mechanism and limitation

The existing oracle compares DB-derived expiry with host time without a shared clock. Positive DB-versus-host skew can violate the upper bound when skew exceeds the second-floor remainder plus intervening response latency. The observed positive VM skew supports this mechanism, but the failed execution lacks the values needed to prove that this particular comparison branch caused the failure. A backwards host clock step or other cross-clock changes also cannot be retrospectively excluded.

Disposition: unresolved harness cross-clock sensitivity. Preserve the original failure and unchanged successful rerun. No claim that a flaky test was fixed, and no tolerance increase, quarantine, skipped check, or weakened assertion. Any future oracle/diagnostic change requires exact proposed tests and independent review; same-clock DB bracketing is preferable to arbitrary host-clock tolerance.
