The proposed eleven requirements and eight refinements are sound as a risk-based readiness framework. They are not a demonstrated industry consensus, a requirement to finish the entire Foundry-equivalent product before the first implementation, or evidence that the current Console repository has already passed them. Implementation should begin once the approved first vertical has sufficiently precise contracts, independently reviewed and approved exact test SHA and trustworthy owner-specific RED probes. Production exposure remains a separate decision.

Three corrections materially change the recommendation. Gates must attach to individual obligations and to the actual operation they protect. Existing approved contracts must be reconciled and tested instead of being repeatedly redesigned. Evidence must distinguish authored specifications, discovered tests, reached assertions, passing behavior, mathematical proof, and deployed operational guarantees.

Scope and evidence

The first vertical is native NONPAYABLE payroll with the Account/current-context, policy, draft/action, evidence-custody, work/transfer, review, historical own-publication/correction and selected-Company Group-operation dependencies approved for that vertical. Console remains a native governed object platform targeting Foundry, Ontology, Actions and Fusion breadth. The broader HR lifecycle, native communications, enterprise sheets and external collaboration remain visible later capabilities; their architectural dependencies matter now, while their complete implementation specifications belong before their own implementation.

The operating baseline is one pilot tenant/Group, six Companies and 2,000 people, operated by Console. Company is the legal entity and RLS cell; Group is the tenancy. Cross-tenant isolation is separate from policy-controlled access among Companies within a Group. Employment, office location, job title and organizational rank do not themselves confer authority. Persistent Account identity is independent of employment and must support future applicant, former-worker and partner access under current policy.

Undated live documentation and older foundational work are labeled separately. Vendor reports describe their own experience; they do not certify Console or constitute independent causal research. Recent agent-development evidence

The evidence supports concise, relevant instructions and local evaluation; it does not support instruction accumulation as a correctness strategy. OpenAI's 11 September guidance recommends precise skill triggers, progressive disclosure and task-relevant document routing. It warns that large instruction sets can conflict, overconstrain newer models and induce unnecessary work. It also recommends explicit completion boundaries and permission for known-safe disposable test workflows. These are model-provider recommendations, not controlled proof that a particular Console instruction change improves outcomes.1

Gloaguen and colleagues' paper was first submitted in February and revised on 23 June. It evaluates four agent/model configurations on established tasks and CTXbench, whose 138 tasks span twelve Python repositories. It reports no general improvement in task success with repository context files and more than 20% average inference-cost increase. Developer-written files outperform generated files in its comparison, which is not the same as outperforming the no-file baseline. Its authors explicitly retain a role for nonstandard conventions and recommend evaluation before adoption.2

The study's scope matters. Its models include Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5.2, GPT-5.1 mini and Qwen3-30B-Coder; these are not an experiment on the current Console model and Rust toolchain. CTXbench uses generated task descriptions and generated tests informed by the original patches, followed by RED/GREEN checks and manual correction of overfitting. Those checks strengthen the benchmark, but the benchmark is still a test-defined sample rather than a measure of long-term maintenance, security or production readiness. Its Python focus limits direct generalization to Rust, Cedar and Leptos.2

Khatri's 28 July preprint compares no context, always-present context and selectively retrieved wiki context over three Python repositories. It reports 291 completed runs, 288 evaluated runs, fifteen tasks for Claude and seventeen for Codex, with three repeats per strategy. Its main correctness result is a bounded null; its own conclusion calls the equivalence analysis descriptive rather than a powered equivalence claim. Larger wiki corpora in two repositories, different injection channels across agents, Codex-based task screening and a small informative task sample limit causal interpretation.3

Two claims in that paper deserve additional skepticism. More retrieved text is not necessarily a better treatment, so the assertion that a larger corpus can only strengthen a null is not established. Its characterization of the Gloaguen study as a Claude-family study does not match the four configurations described in the June revision. Neither issue invalidates every result, but both argue against treating the paper's reconciliation as settled consensus. The experiment is a reason to test instructions locally, not to remove safety contracts.2,3

There is also conflicting older efficiency evidence. Lulla and colleagues' March revision studies 124 pull requests across ten repositories using GPT-5.2-Codex. It reports a 28.64% lower median runtime and 16.58% lower median output-token use with AGENTS.md. However, comprehensive semantic correctness was outside its scope; the authors performed a manual sanity check of fifty tasks. Faster termination and fewer tokens are therefore not evidence of equivalent correctness. This older source helps interpret the recent papers but is not relabeled as June–September research.4

Bun's 8 July Rust rewrite account supplies a relevant engineering case. It describes separate implementer/reviewer contexts, explicit lifetime and translation guidance, compilation, existing runtime tests and subsequent hardening. It also states that, at one phase, every line had been reviewed by two adversarial reviewers and none of it worked yet. The publication date is in the requested interval; the reported rewrite work occurred in May. It is an affiliated practitioner's account, not a controlled comparison or a mandate for Console to run dozens of agents.5

Recommended Console instruction contract. Keep the non-negotiable repository invariants in the root instructions: authority entry points, one writer, exact source ownership, lane admission, preservation of tests/evidence and distinction between legal claims and engineering facts. Put task-specific design detail behind the authoritative entry points. Each implementation assignment should state the desired observable behavior, responsible owner, preserved invariants, exact base/test identity, probe, applicable validation and stop conditions. Avoid duplicating the entire product architecture into every assignment.1,6,7

This recommendation does not authorize silently simplifying the repository's prescribed review sequence. Four design rounds and explicit design/test approval are local governance requirements. No cited paper establishes that four rounds are universally optimal. Reconsidering that rule would require its own authorized policy change and evidence; a literature summary cannot waive it.22

Evaluate future instruction changes with representative Console tasks and fixed toolchain, data, commands and acceptance oracles. Compare equal task information where possible, preserve repeats and incomplete-run outcomes, and measure acceptance success, regressions, prohibited changes, unnecessary interventions, elapsed time and total useful-outcome cost. A prompt that shortens a run by abandoning a required assertion has failed. This is an evaluation method for future instruction changes, not a prerequisite to every first-slice code edit.

Gate model

Use three ordinary stages: I, before dependent implementation; A, before accepting the implemented slice; and P, before production exposure. Add X, an activation overlay: safeguards must precede actual real-data conversion, credential cutover, sharing, authority enrollment, irreversible disposal or other consequential effects, even when no public launch occurs. X prevents an internal migration from bypassing the controls supposedly reserved for launch.

Tests at I may expose genuinely absent owners or schema. Their fixtures, intended assertions and dependency boundaries must be explicit. Assertions that cannot yet run do not need invented implementations to make them appear executable, and they do not count as tested behavior. At A, every required assertion for the accepted capability must actually execute and pass. At P or X, actual deployment/data/provider evidence must meet the guarantee being activated.

Findings on the eight refinements

1 — Gate every obligation: accept with the activation overlay. One label per broad heading is too coarse. The recovery heading contains preimplementation semantics, implemented fault tests and deployment-specific operational evidence. Assign each to the earliest operation it protects. Contract selection should not wait for an already implemented system, and actual data use should not precede its required protection.

2 — Define executable TDD precisely: accept, with a repository-specific qualification. An Account catalog test failing at a missing table can admit that catalog work. It cannot admit unrelated policy behavior. The formatter tests provide an example of meaningful behavioral RED at an existing boundary: they call the actual response formatter and observe private diagnostic differences in public bodies. Four negative cases fail at body equality after status/header checks; their later assertions remain unreached.24

There is no universal principle that an absent-interface compiler failure can never be legitimate TDD. The current DELIVERY lane tool rejects green or nonexecutable commands; it does not categorically forbid every deterministic compilation RED. However, the approved design30 verification plan additionally requires invocations to exist and discover tests. For a genuinely absent owner interface, use an available responsible boundary or obtain a precise reviewed refinement of that requirement. A discovery-only outer wrapper or dummy owner is not a solution. Record zero downstream assertions until they execute.22,23

3 — Check dependencies, not only capability names: accept. The accompanying capability ledger records what the first slice must preserve and when later adoption would require migration. Stable object identities do not automatically provide stable field/item addresses, formula semantics or communication delivery identity. Future sheet collaboration requires an explicit logical address model and versioned operations; choosing OT, CRDT, MVCC or locks now solely because a grid will exist would be premature.9–12

4 — Govern policy administration: accept; much of the design already exists. Cedar's official guidance explicitly includes meta-permissions. The right to change a resource's owner or classification can itself grant authority, so those writes must be protected. Console's design13 already covers delegation ceilings, correlated grants and policy/schema/field administration. The remaining task is owner enforcement and adversarial tests, including stale authority and omitted applicable forbids, rather than introducing job-title roles or a second policy engine.13,23

Cedar's normal policy-evaluation behavior is skip-on-error. An erroneous policy can coexist with an Allow from another policy; applications may inspect diagnostics and choose stricter behavior. Console's fail-closed-on-diagnostic contract must therefore be enforced in the adapter. Schema validation also relies on conforming requests/entities and does not prove current data loading. Neither Cedar nor SSR automatically closes every serialization, alias or outbound path.14,15

5 — Expand lifecycle/migration coverage: accept; reuse the existing transition contract. Design30 already specifies M0–M6, bounded resumable mapping, quarantine, reconciliation, mixed-version refusal and permanent legacy-auth fencing. Tests should seed populated old data, interrupt backfills, try old readers/writers, preserve identity/receipt attribution and verify that rollback cannot revive revoked credentials. Persistent Account identity is compatible with separately managed authenticators, employment data, retention, deletion and legal holds; it does not mean every payload must exist forever.16,23

Software rollback, data restore and forward recovery after irreversible disposal are distinct. A restored old database can contain old permissions and omit newer acknowledged effects. Serving must remain fenced until its current authority and effect history satisfy the selected recovery contract. A successful schema migration on an empty database does not establish this behavior.17

6 — Separate invariants and budgets: accept. Tenant isolation, complete membership, attribution and duplicate-effect prevention are correctness obligations under contention and overload. Latency, throughput and availability have measured envelopes. Safety may require rejecting or preserving a blocked request instead of meeting a latency target. Fairness and eventual progress need stated scheduling/resource/failure assumptions; a finite schedule cannot establish an unconditional liveness guarantee.17,18

The employee counts 2,000, 50,000 and 1,000,000 are data dimensions, not concurrent writer counts. Vary arrival rate, hot-key fraction, fan-out, retained history, transaction work, pool capacity and fault schedule independently. Compare complete candidate mechanisms at the same semantics and workload. Change the architecture only when measured thresholds and a tested alternative justify its compatibility and operating costs. PostgreSQL's current ownership fit makes it a comparison target, not an unchallengeable commitment to MVCC.17,23

7 — Add observable usability: accept. Screen inventories cannot show whether people find the right action, notice crucial evidence or recover. Palantir's own ontology validation guidance says structural review cannot confirm usability and recommends task-based independent validation. W3C similarly treats participant evaluation and accessibility conformance as complementary. The accompanying protocol specifies tasks, necessary evidence and observations; no participant study is claimed to have occurred.19,20

For Console, a successful task is not merely a click on Submit. It includes selecting the correct scope and period, preserving required population, understanding NONPAYABLE status, identifying blocked/incomplete work, and knowing whether an interrupted command committed. Measure assistance and errors as well as time. Essential facts must be visible at the decision point; linked but undiscovered information does not meet the intended experience.

8 — Review the evidence machinery independently: strongly accept. The original Elle paper depends on observable operation/version histories and explicitly excludes predicate reasoning from its model. A per-row history checker therefore cannot prove complete payroll membership or a negative selection predicate by itself. FoundationDB's original simulator runs real database code with synthetic workloads, multiple oracles and fault coverage, but explicitly does not replace performance or external-dependency testing. Console should reuse real owners and independently challenge its history/oracle boundaries, not build a second payroll implementation in the tests.18,21

A concrete local counterexample was found in the Buck wrapper test: the generated fake executable ignored failed credential-file mode/content assertions and later returned success. An isolated harness mutation that changed the file from0600 to0644 was accepted by that checker. The corrected fixture distinguishes build from test, explicitly enforces the existing mode and four required key/URL-prefix checks, and rejects the same0644 mutation plus four independently malformed content keys. Every outer test byte is preserved. This establishes those fixture checks; it does not establish complete credential security or invalidate the independent PostgreSQL suites. The earlier shell exit0 was insufficient evidence for file permissions/content.

Challenge omitted requests, missing effects, duplicate effects, wrong attribution, orphan audits, invalid intervals, absent fault witnesses and unknown outcomes relabeled as no-effect. A checker that accepts an extension of an incomplete history may establish modeled safety, while the operational process still has unresolved work. Do not turn internal mutation assertions into extra discovered test counts or claim statistical independence merely because two agents reviewed a file.

Concrete architecture and test gaps

The evidence supports a focused refinement, not another blank design cycle. Existing CCF1/native18/source19/submission24 byte contracts and source owners should be preserved. The shared restricted JSON format belongs in the existing ontology application owner if the layer graph permits that direction; payroll domain must not acquire a dependency on an application layer merely for convenience. Raw duplicate-key validation must precede conversion into a JSON Value. DTO validation remains responsible for undeclared fields, nullable/required semantics and exact scalar/set rules.23

Three design30 gaps are concrete enough to block dependent codecs: the exact native28 stream domain tags and length framing; the relationship between semantic and custody EvidenceRefs and the described paired input stream; and the complete normalized OWNER_SUBMISSION envelope/component mapping. The existing CalculationOutcome18 already carries ResultRef, so a second serialized ResultRef would create another consistency obligation. These are byte-identity questions, not missing implementation functions.23

A single paired stream, two separately retained streams, or two references to one paired stream have different retention and reconstruction consequences. Mandatory aliases create redundant consistency checks; changing to one reference may conflict with the current distinction between semantic and custody evidence. The right refinement must state which bytes each reference retains and why, preserve existing 18/19/24 golden bytes, and get exact revision-bound review before implementation. No arbitrary choice is declared approved here.

The complete first-slice test package still needs owner-bound positive and hostile cases across current Account/context, policy administration/revocation, draft/seal/replay, native input/calculation completeness, partial-visibility review, corrections, work creation/transfer, Group operations, custody and populated migration/cutover. An inventory of these tests is not authored executable coverage. Tests whose owners are absent can remain prerequisite-blocked before implementation only with precise intended interfaces, fixtures, independent oracles and explicit downstream status.

Local primary/standby, holder/fencing and commit-transport tests must execute before the corresponding recovery behavior is accepted. Designing these fixtures is necessary before the dependent owner code; running a full pilot failover or certifying 50,000/1,000,000-person capacity is not a requirement before the first implementation. A custom database simulator, generic event engine, or second VCS is not justified solely by this checklist.

Verus should target exact implemented computations and relational information-flow obligations. Define low-equivalent inputs, permitted declassification, observations, termination/timing scope and trusted dependencies. The existing source-bound freshness predicate proves supplied-value comparisons; it does not prove genuine current policy loading or projection/event/egress noninterference. Verus documents assume, external bodies/specifications and some executable-spec translations as trusted components. A mock projector or an axiom asserting the desired nondisclosure result would not close the gap.26

The two open-loop workloads retain intended arrivals, actual invocation and completion, client admission rejection and independent complete effect/audit readback. A new environment finding changes how this evidence should be interpreted. The active Cargo/Buck disposable runners use fsync=off, synchronous_commit=off and full_page_writes=off; actual PostgreSQL18.6 readback confirms all three. These tests remain useful for transaction, policy and cancellation semantics. They cannot establish acknowledged-commit crash durability, replica loss tolerance or safe promotion. PostgreSQL documents that durability depends on the setting effective at commit and actual synchronous-standby configuration, not the name of the database or a single configuration flag.17,25

The official 13 August PostgreSQL18.6 advisory also fixes stale role-dependent cached RLS plans after role changes. Four active disposable runner/image constants were pinned to18.4. The bounded correction updates them to the independently verified official18.6 image index, preserves historical evidence and executes the existing relevant suites. Exact execution counts and final independent disposition are in postgres-update.json; the unchanged version probe is a dependency-prerequisite RED/GREEN, not a reproduction of the CVE or a Console exploit demonstration.25

The18.6 compatibility run executed49 tests:46 passed and3 failed, with aggregate runner exit1. SSR10/10, attendance8/8 and executed ownership23/23 passed. RLS5/8 passed; its three Account privilege/missing-role failures have identical outcomes and mechanisms in an immutable18.4comparison run. Thirteen Node-reported tooling tests also passed. The bounded update has no observed regression in these exercised cases; it does not make the suite wholly green or complete Account isolation.

Other operational templates and recovery scripts still reference18.4. Their lifecycle/data compatibility and deployment ownership are a separate transition; this four-file correction is not repository-wide remediation or an upgrade of any deployed database. Before those templates are used for the protected workload, their version and actual behavior need corresponding evidence. No old18.4 log is relabeled as18.6.

Readiness decision

The checklist assessment is complete: all eleven requirements and all eight refinements have a disposition, gate assignment and evidence boundary. The capability-preservation ledger and usability protocol supply concrete authored additions. The complete versioned Foundry leaf inventory remains open; twenty-three family rows are not leaf-level equivalence. The research does not justify expanding the first vertical or replacing the repository's local delivery authority with a generic best-practice framework.

Global implementation readiness is not established. The remaining entry obligations are the precise stream/reference/submission contract refinement; the complete independently reviewed and approved first-slice test source package with meaningful per-owner RED classification; and reconciliation of exact design/test/authority identities into the final handoff. Existing missing production owners are expected in TDD. Missing precise contracts and missing authored required tests are actual preparation gaps and must not be relabeled as implementation failures.

One known test correction is separately constrained by automatic approval review: allow only the validated public.auth_webauthn_ceremony_bindings(ceremony_id) to public.auth_webauthn_ceremonies(id) ephemeral cleanup edge to use DELETE CASCADE, require that edge to exist, and retain UPDATE NO ACTION/RESTRICT plus all other destructive-edge checks. The current new test incorrectly rejects that legitimate lifecycle. Independent review approved the precise correction; automatic review demanded explicit approval of this exact exception and rejected generic authorization. The original test remains unchanged. This is one specific approval constraint, not a reason to stop unrelated research or checks.24

The implementation-entry finish line is finite: settle those byte/identity definitions, approve the complete authored test candidate, reconcile authority, then admit each clean-base owner lane through its actual RED command. The same probe must turn GREEN and the required deeper assertions must execute before acceptance. Payment, legal/compliance, provider activation and production exposure retain their separate gates.
