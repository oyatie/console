본인 급여 검토·정정 요청 + 일상 업무 인계

본인 화면의 첫 금액 기능을 비지급 급여 계산 검토본으로 정했습니다. 내부 준비 상태, 본인에게 공개한 검토본, 법적 명세서 발행·지급은 서로 다른 상태입니다. 공개 당시 금액과 버전을 보존하고, 바뀐 결과는 정정 이력과 새 결과로 연결합니다.

정정 요청은 항목에서 바로 시작합니다. 회사·고용 관계·계산 버전·기존 값을 자동 연결하고, 필요한 질문과 설명을 입력합니다. 정확한 대체 금액이나 첨부파일이 없어도 문의할 수 있습니다. 담당자가 없으면 접수된 예외 업무로 남기며 요청을 잃지 않습니다.

담당자는 제출된 항목·설명·관련 근거를 한 흐름에서 보고 답합니다. 금액만 수정하거나 새 계산을 만들었다고 요청이 완료되지 않습니다. 정정된 승인 결과가 실제로 본인에게 공개되고 항목별 답변이 있어야 정정 완료입니다. 퇴직해도 같은 Account와 현재 허용된 과거 고용 권한으로 기록과 요청을 이어갑니다.

인계는 부재 전용 기능이 아닙니다. 바빠서 옮기는 업무, 본인의 인계 요청, 권한 있는 업무지시, 책임 범위 변경도 같은 HandoverPlan과 실제 업무 단위·권한·업무량·초안·미확정 실행 절차를 사용합니다. 사유와 임시/영구 이전은 별개이며, 직급이나 지시 메시지가 권한을 대신하지 않습니다.

이번 범위는 설계 후보입니다. 명세서 법적 발행·지급·외부 공유·production 권한과 실제 API/DB/실행 테스트는 별도 조건을 따릅니다.

PR1 — Publish an exact own payroll review result; preserve the question and its resolution
Proposed bounded B08/W03 design. Uses AM1/AI1/EC1, AC1/PC1, FD1, GO1 and WA1. This packet chooses the first monetary self-service scope, not production, payment or legal issuance authority.

PR1.1 Scope and inspected facts
Chosen positive journey: authorized operator explicitly releases an APPROVED nonpayable payroll calculation review snapshot -> its subject uses the same Account to read permitted own amounts -> selects the disputed item and submits a saved correction request -> eligible responsible worker reviews actual evidence -> no-change response OR corrected/reapproved successor and new own publication -> subject reads response and relevant difference. Employment can end at any stage without deleting Account or automatically removing historical own rights. Label the result '급여 계산 검토본 · 지급 전'; do not label it paid, legally issued, receipt-confirmed or payable. PayRun state stays its existing business state; review publication never transitions it to PAID/ISSUED or payable=true.
At source05200e43, payroll/rest list_my_lines resolves users.employee_id and returns MyPayrollLine readiness fields, no won amounts. payslip_draft GET requires payroll Manage and calculates from contract/attendance; it is not an own historical publication. issue_payslips loads release-gated PAID lines, emits InboxDocKind::Payslip rows separately and later records deliveries/status ISSUED; load_payslip_issuance selects latest per-line calculation and joins users.employee_id. The inspected path is not the proposed version-bound self-review release transaction. InboxDocKind has only Payslip and LegalNotice and receipt semantics specific to them. Do not publish review copies by abusing legal issuance, changing gate checks, or emitting review payloads as issued Payslip docs.
Reuse payroll's existing domain/adapter writer for new canonical PayrollReviewPublication and PayrollReviewRequest facts, exposed through common ontology projections/Actions. Workflow owns assigned work/queues, common draft owner owns incomplete inputs and revision history, identity owns subject/Account binding, policy owns permissions, notifications own transport. There is no existing payroll application crate established by this inspection: choose the shared responsible use-case seam in W04; REST and common Action must call the same owner transaction, never separate writers. Actual statutory statements, payment execution, PDF signing and external delivery remain their own reviewed capability/HOLD. The new own-review scope needs explicit current-authority alignment before implementation; evidence does not enable it.

PR1.2 Publication and recipient objects
PayrollReviewPublication is a Company-owned canonical object with stable random publication_id; immutable origin Company/legal issuer, Employment/Person subject references, source PayRun/submission/approved decision and exact input/calculation/line versions, public projection schema revision, currency KRW, period/pay_date semantics from the frozen source, subject-specific line-item snapshot, publication command/batch reference, released_at, recipient entitlement/ownership proof at release, status/control generation and optional predecessor-publication relation. Its content is an immutable derived disclosure snapshot, not another editable wage truth. Use the existing common canonical-object projection/revision mechanism; no parallel draft VCS. Snapshot retention is explicit even if original source changes or is disposed under its own policy.
Frozen visible content: authorized issuer/subject labels, covered Employment/period, earning/deduction item IDs and typed labels, quantities/rates/bases if included by the approved public projection, integer won amounts, gross/total-deduction/net amounts, calculation basis/version labels approved for subject disclosure and product-kind 'NONPAYABLE_REVIEW'. Exact original text/labels used for the released view are captured; later renamed salary items cannot rewrite what the subject was shown. Raw tax evidence documents, colleague lines, bank identifiers, resident IDs, internal approver comments and Company totals are not default publication content.
Subject is the actual Company Employment/Person calculation unit, not a name/email match or an arbitrary selected Account. The identity owner resolves current verified own-record entitlement under EC1 against this historical subject, binding release recipient evidence. Rehire creates a distinct Employment while preserving Account; it neither overwrites old publication subjects nor restores old work grants. Concurrent Employments/Companies have separate publications; no accidental consolidation across employers. Multiple Account aliases require current verified ownership of the same subject, not duplicate publication rows per alias. Notifications go only to explicitly admitted Account delivery bindings; a later alias is not automatically notified merely because it has the same name or email.
No verified recipient/entitlement means no release for that target and a governed delivery-setup exception; do not silently drop that row. A caller may explicitly choose a feasible subset after preview, with that exact scope recorded. An unresolved subject binding may be repaired through identity-owner procedure, not payroll editing recipient_user_id. Historical ownership remains a current verifiable relation, not permanent bearer access embedded in the object URL.

PR1.3 Release input, disclosure grant and transaction
Entry: approved payroll result -> '본인 검토본 공개'. System selects exact eligible reviewed calculation units and creates a typed release proposal; user may select a policy-allowed subset. Proposal binds Company, run/submission/decision/revision, each calculation-unit/subject/projection revision, publication purpose and explicit target manifest. All selected targets must be ready; zero is no operation. First slice maximum2,000 subject publications per Company command; larger sets require explicit bounded separate commands, never silently truncated 'all'. No scheduled/background autonomous release in this first slice: explicit current human release intent, with only durable notification delivery afterward.
Publish authority is a distinct policy permission to disclose that exact source projection to those subjects; PayrollRunManage/read alone is insufficient. Conversely, releasing an already reviewed snapshot is not a fresh human wage decision: a policy may authorize a release controller without giving them all salary fields, relying on exact complete PC1 approval coverage. They must see the mandatory release evidence: authorized recipient manifest/scope, product-kind, exact reviewed-version identity, release eligibility and known relevant warnings. Hidden monetary values are not a new decision they sign. If the controller cannot discover the selected recipients/scope or mandatory release metadata, they cannot release that selection. A narrowly authorized payroll materialization workload may read only the source projection needed to produce the subject snapshot; it cannot use the controller's absent salary read as a general bypass.
Before release, independently verify policy permits disclosure of each field to its actual subject. Identity/recipient proof, source/roster/calculation, PC1 exact reviewed coverage, approved current non-superseded run, payable=false, no paid/issued/disbursement effect, publication schema, required evidence and optional exact governance gate must all be valid. Policy versions/digests alone are not locks. Use AI1/WA1 applicable current guard prefix -> draft/attempt guards if this release intent uses FD1 -> domain period/run/source/governance order -> publication identity/current-pointer slots sorted by subject key. Concurrent correction/withdrawal and all publication paths share the owner run fence. Draft autosave never releases content.
Single target-Company transaction materializes immutable publication content, records scoped subject disclosure entitlement through the policy owner's participating port if this policy needs one, stores publication indexes/current links, consumes an exact gate when configured, appends release receipt/audit and notification outbox; FD1 attempt success/consumption joins when applicable. No file generation/email network call occurs under these locks. Any failure rolls all back. Group is never a Company context; six-Company release is six explicit Company commands in this slice, not silently admitted GO1 calculation dispatch for a new Action family.
Uniqueness: at most one publication for (Company,source calculation-unit version,subject,public projection revision,purpose); same exact command returns original authorized receipt, same key changed payload conflicts, different command overlapping already published targets returns existing identical publications and adds only missing identical bindings. A correction of projection/content/recipient requires explicit successor or security suspension, never overwrite. The release receipt records exact target set and created/existing result refs, so replay cannot claim a different selection. All source versions must still validate for NEW effects; historical successful receipt replay follows current disclosure and exact command binding before new-effect guards.

PR1.4 Current publication, correction and security suspension
Separate immutable content, currentness/correction state and independent disclosure state (ALLOWED/ACCESS_SUSPENDED). Security suspension must not overwrite a successor/correction relation; restoring disclosure does not restore old currentness. Release creates CURRENT review publication for that source unit. A linked correction workflow marks affected prior publications CORRECTION_PENDING at the same owner boundary that supersedes the old run or proves the source invalid for future use. It preserves old content as historical with the stated caveat and does not publish unapproved replacement amounts. After new source preparation/calculation/independent approval, explicit release of the successor atomically creates the new publication and sets predecessor SUPERSEDED_BY with current pointer. Successor rejection or failed release never reactivates old correctness or marks a case resolved.
For monetary/source corrections, the predecessor edge is derived from the exact payroll correction chain and same Company/subject calculation unit, not an arbitrary client link. One current successor edge per predecessor; rejected attempts to publish competing successors conflict under source/run and publication guards. Multiple genuinely distinct same-period Employments or separate run units are shown separately unless the domain has an explicit corrected-unit mapping; publication uniqueness does not solve the independent duplicate payroll-run problem. Correction affects only the appropriate published subject units and preserves unaffected proof where PC1 permits it.
Publication-only replacement uses a distinct payroll-owned replace_review_projection action when source facts and the approved calculation are unchanged but the disclosure projection/label is wrong. Input binds predecessor publication_id, expected current-slot generation, SAME Company/subject/Employment/approved source calculation version, new governed projection revision, reason/evidence and command ID. Require current exact release/projection-change authority, valid approved source and subject disclosure rights; preserve all source monetary values. A projection cannot introduce replacement wage amounts or change recipient/Employment. Changed source amounts still require AC1 preparation/calculation/approval; recipient misbinding uses security suspension plus identity repair and a separately authorized correct-subject publication, never a successor that silently retargets ownership. Acquire the same source/run and publication-slot guards; atomically materialize new immutable content, replace the one current publication pointer, append predecessor edge(kind PROJECTION_CORRECTION), receipt/audit and outbox. No new wage fact, payroll approval or payment occurs. For an initial release, a stable publication slot keyed by Company/subject/calculation-unit correction lineage/purpose must be empty; ordinary release cannot create a second CURRENT row merely by choosing a different projection revision. For either source or projection successors, CAS the same current slot and predecessor with a unique outgoing edge, using owner-verified source lineage or exact unchanged-source binding respectively. Two competitors have one winner; historical replay returns the original permitted result without reactivating it. Inadvertent disclosure may first suspend old access under PR1.4, and replacement never silently unsuspends the predecessor; its historical content remains subject to current security policy. Publication-only replacement may satisfy a case about projection/label disclosure when the corrected result and response are actually available, without inventing a payroll recalculation.
Source invalidation must be visible without relying only on eventual notifications: read projection evaluates the current owning source/run supersession/integrity status. A source owner unable to provide current validity yields '현재 기준 확인 불가' and blocks new release/claiming the old view current. Historic amounts remain viewable only if current disclosure permits; a source revocation is not itself a revocation of historical access. Old raw data and current status are clearly distinguished.
Security suspension is separate from payroll correction: identity/recipient misbinding, accidental confidential projection or actual access revocation can set ACCESS_SUSPENDED through a narrowly authorized security/policy owner action, immediately preventing future disclosure via all surfaces while preserving forensic content under restricted retention. It is not a general employer 'hide complaint' capability or an offboarding action. Readable status/reason depends on permission; peer/misbinding targets receive uniform unavailable. Already displayed/downloaded data cannot be recalled. Disclosure restoration or recipient correction requires the governed identity/policy procedure; never retarget an existing publication in place or use an employer credential reset.

PR1.7 Case state and human response
Payroll owns request states OPEN -> IN_REVIEW -> WAITING_REQUESTER / CORRECTION_PENDING -> RESOLVED (resolution_kind NO_CHANGE or CORRECTED); requester withdrawal yields WITHDRAWN through its own action. WA1 assignment state is separate. Claim/read/notification does not move to RESOLVED. Current handler must be authorized for this case, subject, required disputed source fields and current task; natural-person separation applies to any underlying payroll approval. If the requester is also payroll staff, they cannot unilaterally adjudicate their own request: resolution requires a different currently verified human, with the underlying payroll S/D/gate rules still applied independently. Titles play no role.
Handler view surfaces each submitted issue item, narrative, allowed evidence, actual matching payroll/attendance/source refs, previous responses and required next action. Response action binds current request/content revision and exact item coverage; new requester evidence after preview causes a stale-response conflict and preserved input. No generic 'read' checkbox proves understanding. Require a concrete typed disposition for every issue item and a subject-readable explanation addressing it. Human policy may require additional approval for a disposition; routing or old wage approval does not provide it.
Dispositions: EXPLANATION_NO_CHANGE with explanation and permissible supporting basis; NEEDS_INFORMATION with specific unanswered typed question and preserved case; CORRECTION_REQUIRED with exact domain correction refs and expected affected units; OUTSIDE_SUPPORTED_PROCESS with reason and accountable manual follow-up (not successful resolution); explicit requester WITHDRAWAL. A refusal to change a disputed amount is an accountable case response, not a legal conclusion that the wage is correct or a waiver of the person's rights. Do not require acknowledgement/acceptance as a condition to view money or maintain statutory rights. Nonresponse and an internal due date do not auto-dismiss the case.
RESOLVED with NO_CHANGE requires complete issue response coverage, authorized resolver and a recorded subject-visible response revision; source support cannot be entirely hidden with an empty boilerplate conclusion. Use a lawful/permitted derived explanation or escalate to someone able to provide one. RESOLVED with CORRECTED requires exact accepted correction linkage AND the corrected approved result actually published to this subject, with current visible response/delta or permitted comparison. A draft created, amount edited, calculation completed or payroll approval alone is not corrected delivery. Workflow task closes atomically with case resolution if its completion criterion is met; capacity release follows WA1. Notification delivery is not required to commit the case outcome, but publication/readback must exist.
Requester may append evidence through versioned own-response action while open/waiting, without editing original submissions. They may withdraw an open request, preserving history; withdrawal does not cancel a mandatory Company correction or undo a business effect. After RESOLVED/WITHDRAWN, disagreement creates a linked follow-up request referencing the response; do not mutate the closed decision or enforce a silent appeal deadline. Internal service targets/reminders are typed operational settings, not statutory limitation periods. No deletion of a complaint on Employment end.

PR1.8 Connecting the correction to payroll ownership
Case handler workload/self-request/instruction/responsibility-driven transfers use WA2 even without absence; reassignment never resolves the case. Case handlers do not directly write wages/attendance/tax facts from the case form. CORRECTION_REQUIRED creates or links the owning domain correction task with subject/unit/source refs; qualified owners perform their typed actions. For an APPROVED nonpayable run, use AC1 supersede_approved_draft and fresh preparation/calculation/review, preserving original decision and source history. Other business states use their admitted owner path; paid/issued/scheduled payroll remains outside this nonpayable correction capability and must enter an explicit specialized/manual accountable process rather than reset state.
Several cases may share one corrective PayRun successor, but each subject request retains its own evidence/outcome coverage. A run correction may affect subjects who filed no case: existing publication status is updated for the precise affected units and successor publication is separately authorized. Case resolution never grants all-subject read or bulk publication. If handler cannot publish the corrected result, it remains CORRECTION_PENDING with a linked publication task assigned under WA1; the requester sees the permissible pending state without internal names/amounts for others.
Resolution and withdrawal race under case/version/task guards: whichever commits first records its state; a later request conflicts with authorized readback, never erases already completed domain correction. Publication and resolution may be separate Company transactions: a durable publication exists first; resolution locks and validates its exact current recipient/source/status before linking it. If publication commits and resolution response is lost, retry resolution via its immutable receipt; no new publication. If publication later becomes stale/superseded, the historical resolution stays historical and a current concern/follow-up is surfaced from owner truth, never falsely displayed as unqualified current correctness.

WA2 — Handover is an ordinary work action, not an absence-only feature
User steering (verbatim): 부재시만 인계가 아니라 바빠서 인걔, 업무지시로 인계도 있을거야. 그런 부분도 고려해줘
This addendum extends frozen candidate09 WA1; it preserves its original evidence. It applies to general enrolled work and PR1 request-handler work. No implementation or authority activation.

WA2.1 Independent dimensions and triggering objects
Use the same workflow-owned HandoverPlan and exact WorkItems for availability changes, workload redistribution, incumbent's transfer request, authorized work instruction, responsibility/scope change and scheduled coverage. These are trigger sources, not alternative task assignment engines. Extend WA1's versioned RoutingRule object with typed trigger bindings; each binds the source kind, permitted initiator/automation action, affected work predicate, recipient selection rule, validity and applicable approval requirement. The underlying source is a typed event, governed work instruction or explicit request object/reference; a reason note alone is not authority.
Trigger/reason, temporary versus permanent responsibility intent, exact task selection, recipient and effective interval are separate fields. 'Busy' does not mean temporarily absent, ill or globally unavailable; record the actual workload/transfer request and leave availability/Employment unchanged. A work instruction does not automatically imply permanent transfer. Sharing part of someone's workload means selecting real tasks, or an admitted exact-coverage split; never the ambiguous PARTIAL_SHARING assignment mode.

WA2.2 Who requests and who can execute
Incumbent entry from '내 업무' or task detail: '업무 인계 요청' -> select exact permitted tasks -> choose typed reason/linked instruction or workload context -> optional suggested person/period -> preview -> submit. Having a task does not automatically grant transfer authority. If current policy permits direct transfer of these tasks, the same confirmation executes WA1; otherwise persist the exact HandoverPlan as a request routed to someone with scoped transfer authority, preserving selected work and context. Recipients are candidates, not permission grantees.
Instruction entry from an authorized task/workspace/plan: '담당 변경 지시' -> exact tasks -> recipient or approved routing rule -> effective interval/responsibility duration -> preview/confirm. Issuer must have the exact resource/action/delegation permission. A chief executive, manager title, org-chart parent or message saying 'I instructed this' supplies no grant. A separately configured approval gate binds this exact plan only when policy requires it; approval records permission to execute the transfer, not payroll/HR business approval. Recheck current authority before the effect and preserve the instruction issuer, requester and executing principal independently.
If no extra approval is configured and the issuer has transfer authority, do not impose a ceremonial second approval or recipient acknowledgement gate. Any recipient acceptance requirement must be explicit policy/state; until accepted, show the current actual worker and pending transfer, not two assignees. First pilot default is authorized assignment without recipient acceptance, with durable notification and ordinary objection/transfer-request path. The original worker remains current until the transfer commits; no implicit release upon clicking request.

WA2.3 Workload automation and conflicting instructions
WA1's 'do not reshuffle solely because someone else's load is lower' remains the default anti-churn rule. Explicit workload redistribution and a configured workload-condition trigger may create a bounded HandoverPlan. Automatic redistribution requires an already authorized versioned rule identifying the overload predicate, task eligibility, recipient constraints, maximum moved units/tasks, cooldown and which states may transfer. Pilot rule activation must bind concrete positive limits (initial validation defaults: maximum20 tasks and100 moved units per plan, cooldown10 minutes per task); missing bounds disable automatic redistribution. Manual instructions use the same safety checks but may supersede a cooldown through explicit scoped rule policy, never silently. No LLM decides who is busy from prose; actual budget use, typed workload override/request or governed task priority/deadline facts are inputs. If the workload condition has no configured authority/rule, surface a repair/request path instead of silent transfer.
Execute selected tasks through the SAME WA1 current policy/capacity/epoch/draft/attempt/Group fences. Work instruction cannot force over-capacity admission, skip mandatory recipient evidence visibility, retarget a sealed actor or repeat unknown business effects. Busy workers may request moving selected tasks without changing their total CapacityProfile; changes to capacity itself remain separately authorized. Automated rebalancing selects only definition-supported editable/no-effect work; started/sealed/Group-bound/unknown work follows the existing explicit recovery barriers and cannot be forced through a load target. Rules cap moves and use event/routing generations; newer opposing instructions race by expected plan/task/responsibility versions, with no last-writer-wins overwrite or automatic ping-pong.
Plan control is Company-owned workflow metadata in this pilot: (company_id,plan_id), immutable selection of exact task IDs/expected epochs when activated, content revision, monotonic control_epoch and state DRAFT/REQUESTED/AUTHORIZED/ACTIVE/STOPPED/COMPLETE. A cross-Company UI selection creates explicitly linked per-Company plans through idempotent creation commands/correlation, with each actual plan persisted in its Company's owner. Cancel-all calls each authorized Company cancel action and reports per-Company stopped/unknown/denied outcomes; no atomic all-Company cancellation or new Group mutator capability is claimed. A missing creation/cancel ACK is reconciled by that Company's exact receipt before another plan is created. Original selection is not silently broadened or dropped in the aggregate view.
For each plan-bound transfer, extend WA1 lock order after identity/policy and RoutingAccountControl locks: Company coverage/definition guards -> Company plan SHARE guard -> WorkItem task guards -> applicable GO1 parent SHARE -> draft/attempt and remaining owner guards. Hold the plan SHARE lock through charge/custody/assignment/receipt commit; require state ACTIVE and exact content revision/control_epoch. This fence is mandatory for automatic and manual plan effects. Initial/direct one-task handover uses a one-task plan. Normal initial task assignment remains WA1's owner path and cannot masquerade as continuation of a stopped transfer plan.
Cancel action takes current caller/scoped policy guards then plan EXCLUSIVE, holding no capacity/task/child/domain locks while waiting. It sets STOPPED, advances control_epoch and appends receipt atomically. Already admitted transfers holding SHARE finish first and retain actual receipts; after STOPPED commits no remaining transfer can pass that plan guard. No synchronous task walk or Group-stop call while holding this lock. Cancel replay preserves terminal stop. DRAFT/REQUESTED content amendment uses the same EXCLUSIVE fence/CAS and invalidates old preview/gate versions; these states permit no task effects. ACTIVE content is immutable: stop remaining work and create an explicit successor plan for changes, rather than editing task selection in flight. Authorization gates bind exact plan/content/scope and current executor; an AUTHORIZED plan activates through a current authorized explicit action, while direct authorized confirmation without an extra gate may create ACTIVE immediately. COMPLETE is set by an exclusive owner transition only after exact selected task receipts prove completion; it cannot overwrite STOPPED. Progress comes from per-task receipts and does not upgrade the transfer's plan SHARE lock to EXCLUSIVE.
The original worker remains current until each transfer commits. After partial success, cancellation stops remaining transfers and preserves per-task receipts; it does not silently reverse completed transfers. Returning a task uses a new authorized transfer/epoch. The fixed selection cannot absorb new tasks without an explicit successor plan or separately configured future responsibility relation.

WA2.4 PR1 integration and review oracles
Payroll review-request handlers use these entry points for overload, authorized instruction or responsibility changes even when everyone is present. Requester-visible case status remains truthful; workload reassignment does not resolve the case or alter submitted content. Transfer of work-bound response drafts preserves permitted input and original author; private drafts and decisions remain protected. Manual and automatic routes use one owner contract, audit/receipts and current policy. A new handler sees needed case narrative/evidence at the action point rather than needing to find it in old chat.
Required positive cases: present but overloaded handler transfers selected cases; self-request waits for scoped authority; authorized instruction directly assigns when policy allows; bounded configured workload rule assigns without absence. Required negative cases: title-only order, forged instruction ref, source trigger without workload mandate, overloaded nominee, partial-task ambiguity, two instructions on one epoch, cancel after partial plan success, and reassignment misreported as case resolution. These are included in this packet's non-executable acceptance plan.
