HT1 — Historical employment, wage and complete attendance basis

HT1.1 Employment interval query
Current PgEmploymentPort::get/list excludes closed heads and chooses latest effective revision. Payroll cannot use that as historical eligibility. Add read_employment_coverage_in_conn(org,employee,earning_window) on the canonical Employment owner. Resolve retained employee->Employment binding history, fetch overlapping heads including closed ones, and partition each head by revisions ordered by effective valid_from (not append version), ending at the next effective revision or head.valid_to. Clip to earning window. Return exact interval/revision refs and explicit gaps/ambiguities; never pick the first or silently fall back to employees.employment_status.
New employment_binding_revisions retain immutable Company/employee/Employment association intervals and append ended/replaced revisions through the same canonical owner. Existing employment_source_bindings remains its current projection. Retarget/deletion cannot erase historical association. Backfill current source rows with evidence-backed head bounds only; deleted earlier associations are UNKNOWN_BINDING, not invented history. Rehire can add a new Employment while preserving the earlier one. Competing overlaps are represented and blocked, not hidden by the old one-current-binding primary key.
NP1 requires one Employment identity covering the entire earning month with eligible state on every intersecting segment. A promotion or transfer may produce several revisions of the SAME Employment; store all segment refs, do not falsely require one unchanged attributes row. Unknown state/association or a gap is blocked. Site/department scope uses explicit historical membership selection over the month; any overlap with the selected obligation scope includes the employee as a required slot, while unsupported intra-month scope allocation blocks full-profile assessment rather than dropping them. Company scope includes all relevant employment candidates, including hidden and currently exited people. Same-Company source binding and Person identity must be unambiguous. Employment need not remain open on pay_date.
Existing head terminal bounds and EXITED effective segments must agree. A backdated EXITED revision inside an otherwise open head cannot be counted as ACTIVE merely because a later revision exists. Interpret each segment by its declared state; unsupported reopen/contradictory terminal history blocks assessment. Source history corrections remain explicit owning commands and period-lock rules, not payroll rewrites.

HT1.2 Wage interval query and correction
Keep employee_contract_wages append-only. Add employee_contract_wage_corrections and correction_heads in payroll adapter, keyed Company/base_wage/revision with predecessor CAS. Base identity/employee/effective_from immutable; correction records typed amount/kind/hours, source refs, reason and original actor. Revision0 means actual base, not a synthetic correction row. Existing direct wage and insurance-draft readers use the same corrected resolver before correction enrollment; original rows/hashes stay unchanged.
Resolve greatest base effective_from <= earning start, then enumerate every effective boundary through max(next_month_start,pay_date+1day). NP1 requires the same effective base identity across that full span and its current correction overlay; equal amount on a new base still changes basis and fails this narrow profile. MONTHLY, positive i64 won and declared1..400hours are mandatory; HOURLY may be stored but this calculator blocks it. New effective inserts, correction heads and absence predicates are fenced by WAGE; selected-row version alone is insufficient. Full-month employment ending at next_month_start can still be paid later if wage basis remains applicable under this profile.

HT1.3 A governed work calendar
Add attendance-owned work_calendar_revisions and work_calendar_assignment_revisions; reuse WorkCalendarPort for leave and a borrowed-connection resolve_work_obligations method for payroll. Calendar is a typed versioned work object with zone, finite covered dates, day kinds and exact duty/break intervals, paid-duration/basis rules and source refs. It is not weekday counting, the employee's current branch label or a messenger meeting calendar. Publish from deterministic recurring templates plus explicit dated overrides; users resolve exceptional dates, not manually enter every person's month.
Resolve one effective calendar assignment per Employment/date with no gap/overlap. The calendar must explicitly cover EVERY earning date, including non-working and paid-rest days. Day kind WORK_DUTY,PAID_REST,PAID_HOLIDAY,NONWORKING has declared rule source; unknown is UNKNOWN_CALENDAR, never weekend=free or holiday=paid by assumption. Duty/break times are integer minutes/UTC microsecond boundaries, not floating hours. A source rule defines expected paid/working durations and premium classification. Exact leave charge and paid absence decisions come from leave owner; pending/unresolved leave cannot count as covered. Scope changes, calendar edits and leave decisions participate in their source families.
This defines the minimal real work-calendar source needed by both existing leave and payroll seams. Full scheduling optimization, shift bidding and general calendar UX are not implemented by this packet. The manual missing-calendar provider remains fail-closed on unenrolled sources.

HT1.4 Actual event coverage and bounded boundaries
Reuse attendance/domain::strict_pair_seconds for pairing semantics, but keep all completeness and paid-time rules with attendance owner. CLOCK_IN/OUT count equality is insufficient (IN,IN,OUT,OUT is invalid). OUT_FOR_WORK/BUSINESS_TRIP/RETURNED remain timeline facts; they require a classified paid-duty disposition, not automatic working seconds. No event or empty exception list proves no overtime.
Query under ATTENDANCE/CALENDAR/LEAVE source guards using indexed (Company,employee,occurred_at,id) ordering. For this profile, fetch earning window plus24hours before/after, max4096events per employee with a limit+1 refusal sentinel; additionally fetch at most one prior clock boundary anchor by indexed keyset lookup. No unbounded whole-history scan. Establish a consistent opening state from an owner-verified boundary/event sequence; missing/contradictory anchor, open pair beyond the bounded window or any unsupported >24hour shift blocks that employee. Do not discard the next month's CLOCK_OUT for a pair that crosses month end. Construct strict complete pairs, keep overlapping pairs, clip to [earning_start,earning_end), then classify schedule/break/leave/premium coverage. The current strict clipping helper can report negative duration for wholly disjoint complete pairs: filter those pairs before invoking clipping or extend the responsible pure helper to return zero for non-overlap, with a meaningful later test. Do not feed unrelated boundary pairs as work in the month.
The query includes every scheduled duty plus unauthorized/off-schedule events; exceeding bounds or conflicting equal-time events is an explicit unsupported/integrity blocker, not truncated completeness. For NP1 actual events must be precision-compatible with the declared schedule rule; unclassified sub-minute/duration rounding is blocked, not silently floored into paid minutes. Half-open coverage and break intervals must partition each duty without double counting. The resolver produces per-date CoverageDisposition with exact event/leave/exception/source refs, expected vs covered duration and unresolved reasons.
WORK_DUTY is covered only by complete classified work intervals plus independently resolved paid absence allowed by the profile. PAID_REST/HOLIDAY also need their rule/applicability basis. Any unpaid reduction, overtime/night/holiday premium, unresolved late/no-show/early-leave, uncertain paid-break/absence consequence, retro obligation or unsupported shift becomes a typed profile blocker. Resolving an exception with CONFIRM or a free-text note never creates money or proves zero consequence. A no-adjustment conclusion requires complete declared source coverage and current rule evidence; automation flags deviations and routes the precise unresolved duty/source for repair.

HT1.5 Coverage and close identity
attendance_coverage_revisions are immutable per Company/employee/earning interval/revision, referencing calendar assignment, event-selection digest and exact rows, leave/exception disposition revisions, boundary proof, rule versions and blockers. A data row inserted later triggers re-resolution through family generations. Coverage stores source semantics, not a user boolean that all is fine.
Existing attendance_month_closes and amendments are reused as operational close identity, but their current readiness does not prove coverage: CloseChecks::ready ignores pending leave and lacks schedule/event completeness. Add a versioned native close-basis receipt binding the selected scope/membership and complete coverage revisions, amendment revision and exact period lock. Whole-Company vs branch close semantics remain distinct; branch-only attestation without Company period lock cannot impersonate whole-Company lock. Enrolled close requires resolved required slots and explicit current permitted attestation, after automatic checks; attest=true alone is insufficient. New amendments are versioned effects; changing basis invalidates current close/calculation use and follows NP1 reopen/review/successor paths. Never silently unlock the payroll period or edit a prior close.
Attendance input collection, no-adjustment assessment, operational close and payroll run close are separate stages: coverage can be prepared before operational close; payroll eligibility references that coverage; run close subsequently binds exact unchanged coverage/assessment and operational close. Avoid a cycle where an assessment needs a run close that itself needs the assessment. Close metadata changing does not self-invalidate unchanged underlying coverage.
