GO1 — One Group operation, independently committed Company Actions
Proposed B03 design. Source base 05200e43af13b527ce031b04369e05852862f1d6. Builds on candidate03 Account/current-policy AI1, candidate04 exact review coverage, candidate05 entity drafts/attempts and candidate06 recovery. No implementation or authority amendment.

GO1.1 Source facts, ownership and selected first proof
platform/group/src/lib.rs resolves identity/member data through narrow functions, then consolidated_read opens one with_org_conn per member. consolidated_read_via uses ? on each member result, so a member error aborts that aggregate; it supplies no durable mutation coordinator. Migration0060 gives runtime no raw access to membership/grant tables and uses identity-only SECURITY DEFINER resolvers. Its legacy 'Group is NOT a tenant' comment is superseded by current PRODUCT: tenant is Group, Company is the legal/RLS cell. GROUP_ADMIN/VIEWER/FINANCE in existing code are compatibility facts, not the proposed hardcoded authorization vocabulary.
workflow's Company-bound payroll outbox drain demonstrates stage-through-owner before acknowledgement and idempotent recovery after lost ACK. It is not a Group owner or a proven distributed lease. Existing payroll calculation is at POST /api/v1/payroll/runs/{id}/calculate and calculate_run_in_tx; current payable remains false. PreparedCommand and Company receipts supply reusable preparation/CAS/replay patterns, not automatic coverage for every projected use case.
Extend platform/group with durable operation-control ownership and bounded orchestration; keep payload, input draft, attempt, calculation, gate and domain receipt in their Company owners. Reuse the same ontology Action preparation/owner dispatch seam after its required fixes. The first positive proof is one existing payroll draft calculation per selected Company, up to the six-Company pilot, exact registered ActionDefinition reference/version bound to the payroll owner. Do not invent that a proposed action key is already seeded. Each target PayRun must already exist and be ready for its owning calculation; creating runs, closing attendance or approving pay is not silently bundled into calculate.
This is a common operation pattern, not a separate HR tenant/product. More Actions enter through an explicit owner capability contract proving the same guards. No multi-Company decision/approval, payment, arbitrary cross-Company transaction, automatic post-gate continuation or general actor takeover is enabled by this first proof.

GO1.2 Protected Group metadata and Company data
GroupOperation is a governed control entity owned by platform/group, with stable ID, Group ID, original Account, immutable selection manifest/version, control epoch, creation/activation/cancellation references and minimal target-slot linkage. It need not expand the canonical ObjectKey roster merely to exist. Group selection/operation UI uses this owner; it does not write payroll through generic instance CRUD.
Choose owner-only Group control tables and narrow typed owner functions, following membership-control isolation rather than pretending this is a seventh Company. Explicitly classify them as protected Group control data; no raw runtime/PUBLIC SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE and no reuse of groups' publicly readable identity-column grant. Helpers have fixed search_path, minimal privileges, immutable Group binding, authenticated server ActorContext and current Group-operation policy checks. No user-selecting principal parameter comes from a public request body. The application remains responsible for current Cedar evaluation and per-field projection; a supplied allowed=true, operation UUID, customer bundle name or worker identity grants nothing. Database ACL/helper confinement is an additional boundary, not a claim that a SQL function automatically evaluates Cedar.
This new storage/control path requires explicit authority/classification and SQL/ACL proof before activation. Group is never passed to app.current_org; business reads/writes use one Company with_org_conn. A narrow group-control guard may be read/locked inside a Company owner transaction without reading another Company's business tables. The coordinator stores no payroll amounts, raw input, unrestricted errors, source documents, mandatory-review evidence or human session bearer tokens. Child outcome mirrors contain minimal IDs/state/checkpoint only, still confidential under Group/target metadata policy.
Every Company child has a unique (org_id, operation_id, slot_id) binding, exact input revision/attempt reference, original actor/action/target binding, domain command ID, dispatch generation and receipt reference. Input uses the common entity revision store of candidate05; control bindings do not create another input history. A domain owner is the only writer of business facts and the authoritative effect receipt. Group observation is a rebuildable projection, never authority to repeat a succeeded command.

GO1.3 Input path and frozen selection
Legal ownership, organizational containment, physical workplace, service responsibility and authorization are independent relations (GO1.12). No selected Company must employ its own HR preparer, no Site must contain a local manager, and no chief-executive title is a prerequisite or universal permission ceiling. Readiness checks actual authorized actor/evidence/owner state, not local staffing or job-title templates.
In the Group workspace, choose an admitted Action and existing period/target objects. The server resolves candidate Companies through current membership and discovery policy, then reads each candidate in its own authorized owner context. Present permitted per-Company target, readiness, relevant changes/evidence and next correction Action in place. A failed Company read yields an authorized unavailable row for that selected target; it must not erase successful reads from other Companies. No hidden Company names/counts in the error panel.
The user explicitly selects the intended targets and confirms the permitted review summary. The manifest freezes one tuple per target: Company, membership incarnation, target object, Action definition/version, exact input draft/revision/schema, expected business/source versions, actor and required reviewed-evidence refs kept by the Company owner. Per-child domain inputs can differ. Selection is based on typed refs, not a narrative interpreted by an LLM. Preparing/saving input does not execute a calculation.
At initial launch all selected targets must have a known complete prepared input and positive readiness; otherwise keep the selection editable with specific permitted fixes. An explicit 'run ready selected targets' option creates a manifest for exactly that displayed subset and labels its scope as that subset. It never silently skips blocked/hidden targets and then claims whole-Group completion. Zero targets means no operation; a GET never creates one. Maximum six targets and one Action definition family in this first pilot operation. Larger/mixed-Action workflows require a later reviewed capability; this bound is not an organizational hierarchy limit.
The roster is a snapshot, not a subscription. Companies added later do not join old operations. Removal/suspension or a new membership incarnation blocks not-yet-executed children even if the same Company is re-added; current Group-operation scope cannot be inferred from an old membership UUID alone. Unrelated membership additions do not invalidate other unchanged selected memberships. Already committed results remain historical. 'Whole Group' means the explicitly authorized full selected membership snapshot at its stated time, never a claim about all future members.

GO1.4 Durable preparation and activation
create_operation uses an Account-bound stable creation command ID and exact manifest digest; duplicate same command returns the same operation, changed contents conflict. Independently chosen new IDs are still subject to the domain's duplicate-work/current-run invariants; idempotency is not a global duplicate-intent policy. The Group owner commits a PREPARING header with immutable slot identities before child dispatch. No calculation can run in PREPARING.
Create/seal each Company input intent through its owner in a separate Company transaction using deterministic stored slot/command bindings. The parent is SHARE-locked through the narrow group-control guard and must still be PREPARING with the expected manifest/control epoch. The child and Company preparation receipt commit together; acknowledgement to the Group owner occurs afterward. Lost child ACK is recovered by exact slot lookup, not another child ID. Never hold one Company's transaction open while opening another Company or writing a Group observation through a second connection.
The Group owner records a slot's preparation only from a verified Company-owner receipt that binds the exact immutable slot, attempt and inputs. Once Company preparation commits, its bound READY attempt, input revision and immutable slot binding cannot change while the parent is PREPARING, including before its ACK reaches the Group owner. Standalone edit/unseal/cancel must reject under the parent guard; they cannot merely observe PREPARING and continue. Changing a prepared input requires parent STOPPED followed by owner-confirmed no-effect cleanup and explicit successor preparation using permitted saved inputs. Source/policy staleness changes current readiness, not the immutable preparation artifact. Bound children also cannot execute in PREPARING or bypass parent state through generic routes. Group activation takes the parent exclusively, confirms that every fixed slot has its preparation receipt and the caller explicitly confirmed this exact manifest, then records ACTIVE and a new dispatch epoch. Because prepared bindings are immutable throughout PREPARING and all parent transitions serialize through its fence, no previously acknowledged READY attempt can have been independently cancelled/unsealed before activation. The EXCLUSIVE lock also excludes concurrent changes; a SHARE lock alone would not prove that earlier ACKs stayed valid. Preparations are durable facts, not a promise that runtime authorization/source data will still be current when each effect starts.
Partial setup stays PREPARING: show saved input and permitted prepared/unavailable targets; no child runs early. Session failure or a crashed browser preserves it for fresh authorized resume. Cancellation fences the parent and terminally stops bound child intents; retries that lost their preparation ACK cannot recreate them later. Cleanup can finish per Company after the parent fence commits. No all-Company distributed transaction, 2PC or compensating payroll write is required to create a bundle.

GO1.5 Explicit human intent, session and worker authority
Activation records the authenticated original Account, current session identity/security generation, exact manifest, dispatch context generation and bounded validity. This first worker is transport for an explicitly launched calculation request, not an independently authorized human decider. Store server-side session reference/epoch, never a reusable human bearer token. Each new Company effect requires the identity owner's CURRENT enabled Account/session and current Group/Company/action/property/resource/relationship permission in AI1 order. Candidate03's live-session requirement is preserved; closing a tab alone need not cancel a still-valid session, but session expiry/logout/revocation blocks not-yet-authorized effects.
The workload principal only has permission to claim/reconcile these bound intents and invoke the named owner with a verified dispatch context. It cannot choose a different human, target, Company, payload, Action or stronger role. Both workload admission and original actor's current authority are required. Attribution records the real initiating Account and separate technical executor identity. Identity/CompanyActor/receipt FK changes from candidate03 must be complete; no dummy Company user or elevated service actor fills a foreign key.
If context expires before a new effect, mark the dispatch waiting for renewed authorization while retaining exact input. The same original Account may explicitly resume under a fresh valid session, review current permitted basis and advance dispatch context/epoch under the parent/child guards. This may reuse the unchanged unexecuted child command, because no terminal business rejection or changed payload is being rewritten. It is not an automatic session substitution. No cross-generation rebase or old worker resume: dispatch tokens bind parent epoch, child generation, actor and session generation. A different Account cannot inherit this intent as if they authored it; B04 handles governed successor work. Already committed effects remain historical and can be read by other currently authorized readers through the owning receipt projection.
Policy/gate/source changes are not repaired by granting the worker more privilege. If a later permitted Action requires a human decision or extra execution gate, pending gate approval alone does not trigger it; candidate03's explicit decider action remains. Adding independently authorized scheduled/workload execution requires a separate actor/intent contract; this bundle does not smuggle it in.

GO1.6 Per-Company execution and current guards
For each newly attempted effect, lock in order: AI1 Group identity/topology guard -> sorted Account/session guards -> target Company membership/attribution/policy guards -> relevant work-assignment guard -> Group operation SHARE fence -> Company child/attempt guard -> the existing domain period/run/source/governance lock order. Reuse candidate05's input epoch/attempt barriers. Current revocation, Group membership changes, parent control transitions and child cancellation writers must participate in the corresponding locks before activation. Acquisition timeout or deadlock rolls back that attempt and returns a retryable no-effect outcome only when rollback is established.
Under those locks verify ACTIVE parent and the exact immutable slot/manifest, current dispatch generation/claim fencing token, READY Company attempt, complete input and current domain expected versions/evidence/SoD/gates. The Company owner atomically commits business effect, domain receipt/audit, attempt SUCCEEDED/draft CONSUMED and child terminal success reference. The Group coordinator never preconsumes a governance gate or writes payroll rows. The parent stays a read/SHARE guard in this transaction, so no cross-Company business mutation occurs.
Historical receipt lookup precedes new-effect checks after current disclosure and exact request binding, as AI1 requires. A stale source/session context cannot rewrite a succeeded child into 'never ran'; disclosure can still be denied. A new effect must pass the current guards; its old preflight or prepared snapshot is not authority. If a domain failure is terminal REJECTED_NO_EFFECT under candidate05, the same attempt cannot later execute. The parent observation is updated after the Company transaction commits, via an idempotent monotonic receipt observation. An ACK lost in that gap yields stale progress only; reconciliation recovers success without another effect.
All direct legacy business routes accepting the bound domain command ID must consult its Company attempt plus parent provenance/fence at the shared owner. Cancellation, stale dispatch or a completed child cannot be bypassed by sending the same command to the single-Company API. A new direct command is a genuinely new separately authorized intent, still subject to business duplicate/current-run rules, not a way to reinterpret the old operation as completed.

GO1.7 Claims, retry and unknown results
Queue/transport progress is distinct from business outcome. Persist a claim generation, holder identity and lease deadline in Company child control; claim CAS is atomic with that control. Initial validation assumptions: two concurrent Company executions per Group, one per Company, claim lease 60 seconds with heartbeat at 20 seconds; no more than five automatic retries with delays 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 seconds plus bounded jitter. Actual calculation/statement deadline is capped below the lease (initial 30 seconds), and runtime proof must establish whether this fits the 2,000-person pilot. Failure to fit changes the reviewed bound; it never permits stale worker execution. No database transaction waits on a human or another Company.
Lease expiry is not proof that the previous transaction failed. A successor claim must lock the same child guard; it cannot take over while an old transaction holds it. Once acquired, read the exact domain/attempt receipt: success => observe it; terminal no-effect/cancel => do not execute; verified rolled-back READY => same-command retry under current actor/parent guards; unresolved outcome => remain unknown. A late worker holding an old fencing generation must fail before any effect. The lease is scheduling coordination, while Company transaction/receipt/parent fencing supplies effect safety.
Only transport/DB failures with established no-effect rollback retry automatically. Current policy/session blocks wait for explicit reauthorization; stale input/schema/business version requires a newly reviewed intent. Exhausted retry budget creates an actionable paused item, not final business rejection. Manual retry uses the same original binding after receipt reconciliation and current authorization; it cannot force success or mint a new ID for an unknown outcome. Reconcile queries are bounded and subject to backoff too; outages cannot become an infinite polling loop.

GO1.8 Stop/cancel and partial results
Group cancel_pending is a scoped control Action: stopping future dispatch is separate from reversing business effects. It requires current permission on the operation's fixed control scope; it grants no salary read, Company business correction or gate authority. Cancellation acquires AI1 current caller/control-policy guards then the parent EXCLUSIVE lock (never first taking a child/domain lock). All potentially executing children hold parent SHARE before their effect. Thus already-admitted Company transactions finish first; after STOPPED/control-epoch advance commits, no later child may newly execute through any path.
Keep the immutable target set; do not delete failed/unknown targets to make progress look complete. Cancel then reconciles each fixed Company child through its owner, eventually marking proven unstarted/rolled-back READY attempts CANCELLED_BEFORE_EFFECT. A successful child remains succeeded. Unknown/temporarily inaccessible Company state remains reconciliation pending; absence of a mirrored receipt is not cancellation evidence. Because the parent fence already stops new effects, a failed cancellation ACK does not permit delayed child execution. Cancellation retry uses the original control command and current disclosure; stale ACTIVE caches cannot override the locked parent.
No distributed rollback is offered. If three calculations commit before cancellation and three never start, display those actual results under current rights. Correcting a successful calculation is another payroll-owned Action with its own evidence and review, not a coordinator compensation. Parent STOPPED means dispatch stopped, not 'all six business effects undone'.

GO1.9 Repair, continuation and finality
One Company can succeed while another blocks/fails. Reopening the operation restores its saved selection and each permitted owner result; the user does not retype successful inputs. For transient no-effect failures, resume/retry the same unchanged slot as GO1.5/7 allows. For terminal rejection, changed input or source basis, use the owning correction form to create new common-engine draft revisions, then an explicitly confirmed successor operation containing only the affected targets. Record predecessor operation/slot links and why the scope changed; successful original slots are not dispatched again.
Do not mutate an activated manifest, replace its actor or silently overwrite a failed slot with its successor's success. Original outcomes stay visible as history; a permitted 'continued in another operation' link leads to repair results. A combined completion claim needs an explicit exact coverage projection of original slots to accepted successor results and current permissions; this first proof displays the linked operations separately rather than inventing that projection. A successor cannot run while the previous slot has an unknown possible effect: reconcile or terminally fence that exact old attempt first. Domain duplicate-work rules apply even if a new operation ID is chosen.
Header control state is PREPARING, ACTIVE or STOPPED; progress/outcome is derived from the frozen slots and verified receipts, not conflated into one success flag. All-selected-success can be shown only when every target has an authoritative success receipt and the caller may know every required status. A succeeded calculation does not mean payroll approved, paid, legally compliant or currently ready after later source changes. Show historical calculation result and separately authorized current readiness. A later Company departure does not erase its earlier receipt or add a new target.

GO1.10 Least-privilege readback and ergonomic screens
Read scope is current Cedar over Group operation metadata, each Company/target, status and individual fields. Group operation access, Company discovery, calculation-status visibility and amount visibility are separate. A permission bundle allowing payroll in A and directory in B cannot become payroll in B. Use Company-owner authorized DTOs for detail; the coordinator never joins raw wage tables or collects unrestricted snapshots to mask in the browser. SSR/hydration, direct URLs, search, chat links, notifications, export and historical views all use the same projection. A linked operation grants nothing.
For a caller lacking complete manifest/status visibility, list only authorized selected slots and always label the view as the caller's permitted scope. Omit total target count, hidden status count, overall completion percentage, all-done badge, hidden-dependent pagination/notification and confidential child error details. Do not render a restricted-company placeholder whose presence reveals hidden selection. A caller with explicit manifest-existence and status-summary rights may see the corresponding whole-operation progress; those rights still do not grant amounts. Compare worlds with identical authorized slots but extra hidden slots: visible entries, totals/continuation, errors and notifications must match. Strict timing noninterference is not claimed.
The first six-target list is bounded without pagination. Authorized Company errors remain local rows; a Company query failure is distinct from denied/not-found and may be described only when target discovery is allowed. Full views identify per-row last-confirmed state/time so stale coordinator mirrors are not mistaken for current owner state. Amount totals require separate aggregate disclosure over exactly the authorized basis and labels; no hidden-compensation summation by default.
Proposed input path: Group workspace -> payroll draft calculations -> select Companies/existing runs -> inspect per-Company readiness and linked evidence -> confirm selected scope -> durable preparation -> activated per-Company progress. In-row actions open the same owning correction form, not a second Company-specific UI system. Retain keyboard selection/focus, announce status updates without stealing focus, and expose safe retry/cancel actions in place. The user sees business readiness/result labels; internal command IDs, leases and lock state belong to authorized diagnostics, not required form fields.

GO1.11 Recovery, lifecycle and adoption proof
The parent manifest and Company child bindings/receipts must be retained through all pending/unknown/repair states and the required result history. Apply candidate06's recovery admission to restore: old parent ACTIVE or lost success mirrors cannot restart business effects. Rebuild progress from Company receipts, preserve cancelled parent epochs and current actor/session/assignment state, and fence old worker generations before dispatch. Missing protected receipt/control evidence blocks affected admission; backup restore never means 'start every child again'. No blanket expiry of running/unknown operations.
Implementation admission requires source-owned metadata/helper ACLs; current Account/Group attribution and policy guards; complete child preparation/owner transaction participation; direct-route provenance closure; cancellation/membership writers sharing fences; bounded claim/retry and per-target projection; then the positive six-Company synthetic scenario plus failure/response-loss/revocation cases. Existing roles/functions/outbox code are reusable patterns, not these proofs already passing. Do not weaken historical tests or classify mutation routes as safe metadata to bypass the gates.
Rollback disables new create/activate/dispatch while retaining parent fences, Company receipt reconciliation, current-authorized views and compatible Account attribution. Never remove Group control data and leave Company-bound command routes capable of executing forgotten cancelled intent. This packet closes the bounded B03 design contract subject to independent review; B04 general responsibility transfer/autonomous workload identity, remaining B07/B08 and whole design/test SHA approval remain separate work. No code, executable tests, migration, deployment or payment is performed.

GO1.12 Central services, remote responsibility and nonhierarchical permissions
User-confirmed requirements: some legal Companies have no local HR and depend on a Group-wide HR function; an ordinary employee may legitimately have broader system permissions than a chief executive; some Sites have no on-site manager and are served by the same Company's headquarters (itself a Site) or another Company's Site/Department. These are first-class cases, not exceptions requiring fake staff, a special tenant shape or rank elevation.
Keep separate typed relationships: (1) a business object's owning legal Company/RLS cell, (2) Company-local OrgUnit containment, (3) actual workplace/location and any headquarters designation, (4) responsibility for specific work/resource scopes, and (5) current policy assignments authorizing exact Actions/fields. None implies the next automatically. A headquarters label does not grant access to every Site; working at Site B does not prevent responsibility for Site A; a Company's lack of local staff does not make its legal records Group-owned.
Use a governed work-coverage relationship owned by the target Company to reference provider Account or an existing provider unit using a composite (provider Company, OrgUnit) identity, together with target Site/Department/resource selector, task-kind references, explicit descendant rules, validity and authority/provenance refs. A Group-wide service arrangement is a common source referenced by separately authorized target-Company coverages, not a mixed-Company HR table. Provider-unit existence and the issuer's ability to designate it must be verified through a narrow identity/relationship owner read; never broaden target-Company RLS to inspect provider staff profiles. Consent/acceptance or approval requirements come from the actual coverage-owner policy. This paragraph defines the required relationship contract; the complete matching/assignment lifecycle belongs to B04, not an already-built source feature.
Coverage supplies candidates/routing context; it is not an automatic data grant. Related permission assignments retain correlated (Action, target scope, field set, validity, delegation limits) tuples. If approved coverage publication provisions a grant, it must do so through the policy owner with an explicit bounded grant receipt and cannot exceed the issuer's authority. A provider unit's whole membership does not automatically get every covered resource. Each atomic WorkItem retains exactly one accountable assigned Account; team coverage is not ambiguous shared accountability. No available qualified/authorized assignee yields a visible blocked work item with an authorized manual assignment path; it does not pick the chief executive, closest manager or hierarchy ancestor by default.
For this B03 proof the actual initiating actor and its current target grants/assignment are resolved before launch; a provider in Company B can calculate Company A's draft with no A Employment, local HR user, local manager or copied target-company roles. Identity owner's CompanyActor gives A attribution only. The one human may act across all six Companies where individually permitted; operation and receipt retain real original Account. Direct authorized work in A does not require Group-operation permission; the Group workspace's additional capability is for the grouped control surface. Losing one coverage/grant blocks only the pending effects that rely on it. A dependent unit-membership or coverage incarnation change advances relevant guards; remove/re-add cannot revive an old dispatch context by matching the same labels.
System permissions are sets of scoped capabilities, not a universal rank number. An employee may administer configurations that a chief executive cannot access; that does not automatically grant the employee salaries, legal signature capacity or all business approvals. A chief executive may have a different narrower read scope. Required authorization, natural-person independence and applicable legal acting capacity remain separate checks; neither title nor breadth of unrelated permissions can satisfy them. Company policy can assign different rights to people with identical titles and multiple precise scopes to one person.
Representative coverage cases required in this candidate's oracles: six Companies served by a Group HR provider; Company A Site served from A headquarters Site; A Site served by B Site/Department; one provider serving A and C with different field permissions; headquarters actor denied a Site outside their scope; low-rank-labelled employee allowed a technical Action while CEO-labelled Account denied; titles changed without changing permissions; missing local managers with explicit remote qualified Accounts satisfying independent review. Subsequent B04 must prove provider change, workload balancing, qualification, no-candidate/manual fallback and atomic-task handover against these cases. This does not claim all possible organizational arrangements have been exhaustively tested.
