RP16.5 One authoritative submission linkage
The source/evidence content of payroll_review_bases is immutable. Its separately classified submission linkage governance_request_ref permits exactly one owner CAS from NULL to the newly created immutable governance request; no content column is mutable in this operation. Both creation and link binding commit in the same payroll submission transaction. Governance binding must refer back to this exact registered owner/basis, Company, digest and REVIEW_DECISION purpose, with one request per basis. A later withdrawn/rejected/stale proposal requires a new basis/proposal identity, never clearing or overwriting the old link. Ordinary submission does not append a new ReviewCycle just to copy this link. Cycle membership retains only basis_id. Completion resolves each member's sole authoritative basis.governance_request_ref (NULL means not submitted), verifies its exact current eligible governance decision, and freezes the actual chosen request/decision refs in its immutable completion manifest. It never reads a stale nullable link copied into immutable cycle membership. Source/cycle correction still follows the explicit versioned owner protocol.
