Access model AM1 — one account, object relationships, policy-selected access
Current user-confirmed design. This file supersedes any earlier draft wording that partitions people into applicant/vendor/former-worker account types, requires an employer to create a login, or prohibits an otherwise authorized action merely because a person is called external. AI1/EC1 implementation notes are reconciled to this model; old reviewed Git SHA remains historical evidence, not approval of AM1.

AM1.1 Uniform identity and access
Account is the durable authentication identity. It exists before any Company relationship and survives employment/engagement endings. Applicant, employee, former worker, vendor contact and partner are contextual relationships to Company/business objects, NOT mutually exclusive account types or permission tiers. A person may hold several at once. Internal/external is relative to a selected relationship and confers no authority by itself.
Every request resolves the same authenticated Account -> server-derived target resource/scope -> current Cedar policy decision -> permitted fields and owning Action. Relationship facts, qualifications, purpose, time, issuer Company, delegation and workload may be policy inputs where relevant. They are typed object references/facts, not free-text role inference. No request is authorized merely by 'external', 'employee', account existence, a CompanyActor row, Group membership or a token role string.
One object component/owning action contract is reused across audiences. An employee may edit a permitted contract field, an applicant may submit requested information, and an external reviewer may approve a specifically authorized record under the same owner guard. Server projections omit unauthorized fields/links/aggregates; hiding a button is not enforcement. Relevant natural-person separation still applies to high-risk actions for ANY actor whom policy allows, regardless of employment status.
Different entry links, home views and navigation are permission-filtered compositions of the same system, not separate external databases or simplified copies of business truth. A limited external engagement should show its task, relevant material, communication, deadline and next action without internal menus. If later granted broader scope, the same account gains the corresponding views; no account conversion or duplicate registration.

AM1.2 Authentication and request context
Choose ONE durable Account authentication model and a common resource-scoped authorization pipeline. 'Personal area' describes the default view when a person has only own-record or engagement rights; it is not a special user class. Do not maintain mutually exclusive personal/work/applicant/vendor JWT personas or reject a currently authorized domain Action solely because authentication came from an external entry.
Account authentication verifies account-security state and credentials independent of employment, Company roles and branch memberships. A verified account session alone grants no Company data. The server derives target Company/object references from the route/action/resource, resolves current permitted scope and constructs the same bounded effective request principal used by the domain owner. Company RLS remains per-Company. Group/whole-company management requires its own current grants; own application/certificate scope never expands to an org-wide principal.
Use explicit protocol version/audience boundaries while migrating existing role-bearing tenant tokens so old handlers cannot mistake a new account-only token for an authorized Company context. That is a deployment/security compatibility fence, not a permanent separate permission system. New handlers uniformly authorize every resource/action; legacy handlers may remain unavailable to new sessions until adapted. Arbitrary client org/role claims never select the armed Company without authorization. Narrow time-bound share/invite proofs are additional scoped witnesses and never universal login or Company-role grants.
Keep account-security generation separate from Company/relationship authorization generations. Ending A Employment invalidates only grants dependent on that relationship and their business leases; it does not delete personal passkeys, stop roleless login or invalidate unrelated B access. Actual account-security suspension/recovery remains separate and applies to all sessions when required. Employer HR/UserManage/SubordinateUserCreate cannot take over the personal account via reset/OTP before or after exit.

AM1.3 Account-owned storage decision
The earlier 'home Company as permanent login anchor' framing is insufficient for a person with no Company relationship. Select a Company-independent accounts root owned by identity. Preserve existing user UUIDs by backfilling accounts.id from users.id; a new applicant/external person can create accounts without inventing an Employee, Employment, Company membership, dummy Company or platform-admin sentinel.
Existing users rows and their origin org_id are legacy profile/provenance references during compatibility, not account ownership. CompanyActor remains an attribution anchor (org_id,account_id) referencing accounts(id), with preserved history and NO implied grant. A narrow own-record/external Action can use an attribution-only anchor after verified entitlement if its owner requires one; it need not create ACTIVE workforce eligibility. Work/engagement permissions are distinct policy-owned relationships. Registration alone does not add the account to every Company's actor roster.
Reuse existing credential/session machinery via a reviewed ownership migration, preserving passkey credential IDs/public keys/counters, account IDs and valid session custody. Credentials and account-security state ultimately reference Account and enforce account-scoped identity-owner access, not employer org RLS or employer UserManage. Existing org_id can remain nullable legacy provenance during expansion; it cannot remain required for a new unrelated account or be an alternative Company-admin credential access path. Use account-scoped access helpers for credential data; do not OR a permissive Company policy into the credential boundary. Account lookup/recovery stays narrow, enumeration-resistant and independently authorized.
Migrate every reached actor FK/readback to the correct Account/CompanyActor owner before enabling that new path, including currently GLOBAL users(id) audit/lifecycle actor FKs (not only composite Company FKs). Preserve original actor UUID and historical receipt bytes. No dummy users row is created just to satisfy a stale FK. Credential/session FKs are retargeted to Account, not CompanyActor; business relationship/Employment FKs remain Company-owned. This is an explicit coherent identity transition, not a claim that adding a field fixes the existing stack.
Sequence: additive Account root and verified ID mapping -> Account-aware credential/session readers and ownership guards -> validated backfill/FK closure -> uniform authenticated-request policy resolution -> migrate employer admin/reset/delete callers -> enable new account creation with zero Company/role/Employment rows -> enable each actor/own-record/engagement Action only when its entire receipt/audit/readback closure is ready. No rollout while legacy employer reset or org-purge paths can delete the migrated credentials. Legacy Company hard-delete remains blocked while its retained evidence/profile/storage dependencies exist. Later Company archive/removal cannot delete Account merely by origin.
After new Account-only records exist, rollback may disable their new effects but must retain Account-capable sign-in/recovery and data custody; it cannot downgrade to mandatory Company users or delete new accounts. Validate key/session continuity and no Company-admin access widening; schema/crypto/auth migration tests are required only after approved design/test gates.

AM1.4 Applicant journey — current HR scope
Published job posting may be viewed without login if explicitly public. Protected application uses the same Account, with zero Company work roles/Employment required. Entry: posting/invitation -> authenticate or create Account with user-controlled credentials -> application draft -> typed required answers/attachments -> submit -> own status/doc requests/interview calendar -> permitted offer response/withdrawal. Unverified account ownership is resolved by the authentication flow; payroll-grade different-human verification is not a prerequisite for merely applying.
Reuse existing recruiting owner and hire orchestration. Current recruiting REST create/get routes require RecruitingManage/Read and return manager-shaped data; these are not proof of applicant self-service. Add applicant-scoped use cases/DTO projections over the same recruiting records and writer, mapping authenticated Account to application ownership. Do not grant RecruitingRead/Manage to applicants or expose assessments, interviewer notes, other applicants, internal score/ranking or unpublished rejection rationale. Subject-owned submission does not imply permission to edit employer decisions. Corrected/subsequent submissions are versioned; response loss uses stable command/draft identity.
Application ownership is bound when the account submits/accepts an authenticated invitation; matching a name/email in an imported record is not enough to claim it. Claiming an existing unbound application requires a reviewed ownership-verification workflow. One account can apply to several postings/Companies; applications and Company disclosures remain separate. A closed/rejected/withdrawn application removes only actions no longer eligible; it does not kill Account or erase records contrary to retention policy.
Hiring uses the existing authorized recruiting->HR owning path, binding the existing account to the correctly verified Person/Employment and assigning approved work permissions when effective. Do not create a new login or Employee merely at registration/application. Existing recruiting_hire.rs writes the employee through HR owner; extend its account/application linkage and replay contract coherently. Offer acceptance alone is not permission to self-create an Employment or managerial role. External role/thread/calendar access comes from the corresponding case/object policies, not a blanket messenger/mail/calendar entitlement.

AM1.5 Former-worker journey — current HR scope
Employment ending preserves Account and verified historical subject links. The same permission-filtered home can show own records and certificate request/issuance under EC1. There is no former-worker-only auth realm. Access rests on authenticated ownership of the relevant past relationship and current issuer/object policy, not ACTIVE CompanyActor or present Employment. Missing proof triggers identity-owned recovery without creating a fake current job. Rehire attaches new relationship/permissions to the same account and never restores old grants implicitly.

AM1.6 External Company participants — future modules, common foundation now
Vendor, supplier, customer and partner modules remain future implementation. Current design preserves the common mechanism: an issuer-owned engagement/case links the same Account (and verified external organization membership when needed) to a precise task/resource set, allowed actions/fields, purpose, validity interval, delegation boundary and audit trail. No global external-person role grants access to all Company records, and being employed by a partner does not confer access without the relevant policy.
Invitation is recipient-bound, purpose-bound and time-bound; accepting it verifies the account/recipient and materializes only the authorized relationship through the owner action. An invite URL is not a bearer permission to browse internal objects, and possession of an email does not automatically prove authority to represent an external organization. Delegated company representation is verified at the required assurance level; accepting a document request must not grant signing or financial approval power. Require step-up only where action policy requires it.
Entry is the task deep link or 'my work' showing permitted engagements. Show required inputs, relevant object evidence/contact thread, due date, saved-progress/receipt state, correction path and current responsible role. Known facts prefill from authorized objects. External participants should complete the task without learning the internal org chart, adopting internal HR roles or re-entering Company metadata. Reuse the same forms/object components/owning actions with allowed fields and choices, including manual fallback when automatic matching/validation cannot resolve facts.
Expiration/revocation ends that engagement's prospective actions and data access as policy specifies; it preserves Account and immutable action receipts, with continued historical receipt access governed separately. It does not grant permanent access to an entire thread/mailbox/storage folder or another Company's engagement. Link sharing remains narrow/time-bound with approval where required; allowed export/download cannot be magically revoked after a file leaves the system.
A good external task experience is a product objective in itself. Optional discovery of other Console capabilities may be offered separately, but no sales signup, tenant purchase, broad data consent or internal onboarding is a condition of finishing the invited task. No vendor/partner screens or business logic are implemented by this design revision.

AM1.7 Mandatory proofs and remaining design boundary
Positive: new Account with no users/Company/Employee/Employment/work-role rows authenticates and submits one authorized application; applicant can inspect only own visible state. Same Account gains a verified Employment and explicit work grant later, then survives its termination and obtains its own eligible certificate. Also plan future limited engagement entry/expiry with same object/action semantics; do not call the future vendor module built.
Negative: internal/external label toggles grant nothing; applicant token/account cannot read assessor notes or appoint itself; revoked work access does not erase own historical rights; switching target Company never widens scope; Company admins cannot reset personal credentials; invite recipient/scope/expiry tampering fails; same human cannot bypass high-risk SoD through multiple contexts. Tests must exercise authenticated endpoint -> owner -> receipt/readback, not merely navigation visibility.
This user-directed model supersedes earlier scoped acceptance wherever account storage/principal partition assumptions changed. It requires renewed scoped independent review. Whole-design consensus, authority amendment and exact design/test SHA approvals remain pending; no implementation or product tests are authorized by this evidence alone.

AM1.8 Internal and external least privilege: the same pipeline
User clarification: internal HR staff, executives and site managers also need different screens; least privilege should expose only what their work needs, using Cedar just as for external participants. There is no privileged 'internal' default. HR/title/executive/site-manager names are interview examples only. Do not predefine these job titles, ranks, permission roles, persona enums or title-selected screen templates. Company operating models are customer-defined; no title/rank automatically selects policy, permission or navigation.
The server composes an AuthorizedWorkspaceManifest for current Account, selected Company/Group/task scope, valid assignments and policy/version context: permitted workspace/view IDs, permitted entry actions and the minimum authorized navigation metadata. It is a UI projection, never an authorization token. Each view query, object field, Action preflight/execute, search, aggregate, notification, attachment, export and direct URL re-evaluates the relevant current resource policy. Clients receive only authorized DTO fields; CSS hiding, disabled navigation and a missing sidebar entry do not protect data.
Reuse common object headers, evidence/decision components, forms and owner Actions. Different current work relationships and entitlements produce different allowed compositions and task priorities. Do not introduce HR-vs-executive-vs-vendor business logic, title-to-sidebar maps or forks of object truth. One account may hold several scoped jobs; switch/select the relevant work context rather than merging all available Company powers into one unrestricted session. Pinning, search shortcuts or personalization may reorder authorized tools but never grant hidden access.
Example as policy illustration, not an unconditional role rule: a site manager handling an absence can receive availability/coverage information without medical evidence; an HR handler can receive the specifically permitted review fields; an executive can receive an authorized aggregate without individual payroll or medical details. Actual permission depends on Company/site/department/resource and assigned purpose, not merely those display titles. An aggregate itself requires permission and must not leak restricted individuals through counts/differences.
Task reassignment, temporary delegation, promotion, transfer, leave, exit, external engagement expiry and policy revocation all refresh the workspace projection and server authority. Preserve still-authorized draft/navigation state; clear revoked sensitive props/cache/selection and reconcile unknown committed results through current receipt permission. Manual exception/recovery paths invoke the same owner Actions and policies; they do not expose an admin backdoor.
Navigation groups workflows into a few stable workspaces; the server's permitted views determine the actual menu. Necessary evidence/details remain available at the decision point within authorized scope. 'Simple' must not remove essential controls or make users search for critical linked facts. This foundation supports current HR/attendance/payroll/common work; future vendor modules add their own views to the same model rather than a second access system.
Existing repository role-based paths, Cedar shadow/coexistence and org-wide gates are source reality, not evidence that this full policy model is already enforced. Activation requires an explicit route/operation/field surface inventory and negative direct-entry/cache/notification/export tests; no prior passing UI test proves universal authorization parity.

AM1.9 Customer-defined operating models; no seeded job-title authorization
User clarifications: Cedar is not attached to HR/executive/site-manager titles or ranks; those are examples and need not be hardcoded or created in advance. Company operating systems can differ. Neither authorization nor default screen selection may branch on those names, presumed seniority, internal/external status, or a mandatory catalogue of occupational roles. A title rename or promotion label alone changes no effective access. A company with no ranks/titles must still operate all authorized workflows.
Keep stable technical resource/action types and security invariants distinct from organization vocabulary. Companies define their actual work units, responsibilities, scoped assignments, delegation and policy relationships as governed typed objects. Optional customer-defined groups or reusable permission bundles are allowed where useful; they are not mandatory built-in jobs and their labels are not authority. Reference stable object IDs and versions. Do not require each company to duplicate every technical action to describe its own organization.
Authorization resolves authenticated Account, server-verified resource/action and relevant current typed relationships, scope, validity and evidence against the effective Cedar policies. Policy may explicitly refer to a company-defined relationship object; Console must not silently infer access from its display name or free text. The same title can have different permissions; different titles can have equal permissions; one person can have several independently scoped assignments. Rank has no built-in permission inheritance. Reassignment changes an explicit assignment through its owner and policy, not the original actor or every permission of the predecessor.
Cedar evaluates permission; it does not itself discover work, allocate replacements, authenticate accounts or design screens. Owning workflow services derive eligible tasks and assignment proposals from verified facts, apply configured suitability/load rules, and enforce policy before assignment or execution. The UI composes supported views from authorized use cases and current tasks, with optional user organization inside that boundary. Permitted but presently unused tools may be discoverable without crowding the task view; hiding them for focus is distinct from security denial. Automatic assignment details remain B04.
Policy/assignment authoring must offer typed selectors for existing objects, scope, actions, validity and permitted conditions rather than requiring routine users to write Cedar or infer relationships from narrative. Show the exact authorized impact preview, validate against the schema and authority ceiling, then version/publish through the responsible policy/assignment owner with review when required. Preview cannot itself grant access. Editing a job title, organization object or assignment must not be an indirect self-grant or allow policy administration beyond the actor's delegated boundary. Tenant-authored policy cannot override tenant isolation, field nondisclosure or mandatory separation of duties. Detailed authoring screens and migration of existing fixed-role gates still need the later complete state/surface inventory.
Acceptance: run equivalent permitted workflows with no built-in job titles and with two Companies using different structures/names. Renaming titles alone leaves decisions/views unchanged; changing a scoped assignment/policy changes only its authorized effects. Equal titles do not force equal access; equal effective permissions do not require equal titles. Direct entry, fields, search, notification and export remain under the same current policy. These are proposed behavioral oracles, not implemented proof.

AM1.10 Scoped permission bundles and confidential subsets
Recommendation: composable customer-defined permission bundles as an administration aid, with typed resource relationships/attributes and Cedar permit/forbid evaluation as the enforcement model. One Account can hold several assignments. A bundle is not an occupation, rank or separate principal. Keep the full assignment tuple together: grantee/account or governed subject group, bundle/action references, target Company and resource selector, allowed field group, validity, purpose where applicable, issuer/approval and delegation ceiling, version and provenance. Do not flatten action sets and resource scopes across assignments: an A-Company payroll grant plus a B-Company directory grant must not become B payroll access.
An absence of a grant is default denial, not an explicit prohibition. Multiple independently valid assignments may supply alternative permits to the exact same request. Explicit matching confidentiality forbids still defeat those permits; a person cannot bypass them by choosing a different bundle/workspace/context. If policy supports a narrow exception (specific authorized account/clearance, purpose, time and approval), encode the exception in the guarded policy condition and validate its required evidence. Another permit or an 'administrator' bundle does not override a matching forbid. No platform/Company administrator title or rank automatically reads payroll. Policy administrators must not be able to remove higher-authority confidentiality controls outside their delegated boundary.
Example A is illustrative only: a person assigned to prepare payroll may view/edit authorized amounts for a scoped set while a protected individual's compensation fields remain unavailable. Confidentiality attaches to explicitly governed resource/field policy or classification objects, never automatically to an 'executive' title. Employee identity, employment facts, attendance, individual compensation and medical evidence are separately permissioned field groups. Permission to see the person does not imply permission to see their pay; permission to read pay does not imply edit, approve, export or delegate. Keep governed reusable field groups to reduce administration, with explicit schema-checked per-property exceptions only where needed; do not mint a role per employee.
Example B is illustrative only: directory/attendance actions can target current people/Employment relationships in an explicitly chosen Company/site/department scope. 'My department/site' resolves to a specific authorized assignment relation and effective time, not a client string or unbounded graph traversal. Decide descendant inclusion explicitly in the selector; a shared physical site never merges legal Companies. Transfer or end of an assignment changes prospective access at its effective instant. Historical case access needs its own valid relationship/policy; old membership alone is not an eternal directory grant.
Business completion under partial visibility is an explicit boundary. A payroll preparer who cannot inspect protected amounts can complete only the permitted preparation unit; the remaining unit is routed to a currently authorized actor or shown as blocked without disclosing its subject/value. The owner's overall readiness must not become approved merely because the visible subset is complete. Calculation may use protected inputs only under a separately authorized deterministic owner-operation contract; compute permission is not read/disclose/approve permission. No blind human approval of hidden amounts, silent exclusion of protected people from payroll, or automatic expansion of the preparer's access. Full partitioned preparation/review, final decision coverage and nonleaking readiness composition require a dedicated B02/B07 contract before whole-run decision UI can activate for partially authorized users. This amendment reopens that completion portion of B02; the S/D/Q/G distinction remains specified, not whole B02 closure.
Confidentiality follows derived representations: totals, count/difference queries, min/max, sorting, filter suggestions, search snippets, email/thread previews, notification badges, exports, attachments, sheet cells/formulas and cached results. Do not send hidden raw fields to the browser and mask them. Distinguish authorized-subset totals from full payroll totals and label them only if the fact of partial coverage is itself permitted. Suppress a derived result when its inputs or combination of released results reveals protected data unless a separately reviewed safe-release rule authorizes that specific aggregate. There is no general promise that thresholds prevent reconstruction. Native object references retain their resource policy in every view; scalar copies and sheet derivations need owner-controlled provenance/disclosure handling, not automatic declassification by copy/paste. Full sheet enforcement remains its own later design; no Fusion implementation claim here.
Current result projection and workflow assignment use the same policy owner, but policy bundles do not assign business responsibility by themselves. An eligible pool can contain several people; the workflow records one accountable assignee per atomic task and separately eligible reviewers/helpers. Divide work by explicit task units, not a 'partial sharing' enum leaving ownership ambiguous. Automatic selection/load balancing remains B04. Manual reassignment is a current-policy owner Action with bounded scope/expiry and audit, not copying all permissions from an absent colleague.

AM1.11 Permission authoring and user experience
Use Discord/MMO patterns as interaction inspiration: composable named capability bundles, scoped object/team spaces, visible effective permissions, clear ownership and short guided setup. No claim is made that MMO games implement enterprise field security. Do not copy Discord's role-collision precedence or Administrator bypass into confidential business records. Company-defined reusable bundles avoid manually configuring every person/action pair; an assignment supplies the scope and validity.
Authorized configuration flow: select person or governed subject group -> choose/create a reusable capability bundle using existing action selectors -> select existing Company/site/department/task/resource scope and explicit descendant rule -> choose permitted field groups and any separately authorized restrictions -> set validity/delegation bounds -> inspect effective allowed/denied impact and affected ongoing tasks -> submit/publish through the owner under current change policy. Existing object facts prefill; no routine Cedar coding or narrative-to-policy inference. A custom restriction cannot silently exceed the policy editor's authority.
An effective-access inspector answers 'what can this person do, on which objects/fields, for how long, and from which assignment/policy?' An authorized administrator sees the relevant grant/prohibition path and proposed-change diff. A user sees only a sanitized explanation and an eligible request/correction route; denial explanations must not reveal a protected individual's existence or the secret policy membership. Preview evaluates policy against authorized synthetic/projection data; it is not impersonation or a credential handoff, and never exposes the target person's hidden business data to the editor.
Actual task screens remain common native object views. Show the task, usable inputs, authorized linked evidence, save/result state and next action, with scope visible in plain language. Hide inaccessible sections server-side; show 'restricted' only when existence is itself authorized. Keep permission debugging in the authorized configuration surface, not in routine task completion. No 40-icon sidebar or blanket job persona switch is required.

AM1.12 Cedar semantics, errors and bounded enforcement
Official Cedar authorization semantics at the retrieved source: default deny; matching forbid overrides permit; policies with evaluation errors are skipped and reported in diagnostics. Therefore 'using Cedar' alone is not a proof of fail-closed confidentiality: an erroneous forbid could be skipped while another permit succeeds. Console must validate published policy/schema/entity inputs, resolve required current typed facts server-side, and reject the affected request when required policy/fact resolution is incomplete or the evaluated policy set reports an error. Record policy IDs/versions and bounded diagnostics without leaking sensitive facts to the user. A decision with such errors is not presented as authorized success.
Scope the evaluated policy set deterministically using schema/action/resource boundaries and all applicable security constraints; never omit a relevant forbid to improve latency or avoid an error. A policy-load/cache miss is not an empty unrestricted set. Contain errors to the affected request/Company/policy surface where correct completeness can be proven; do not claim a global deny-on-error cannot affect availability. Versioned change preview/validation and rollback to a previously valid policy revision reduce that risk without bypassing current revocations. Shared Company isolation, current resource guards and mandatory SoD remain independent enforcement boundaries.
Cedar evaluates individual authorization requests and does not automatically filter SQL rows or sanitize DTOs. Reuse current authorization owner and Company RLS, with source-checked query/projection guards. Batch/cache only against exact account/resource scope, policy/entity/assignment versions and valid time; never reuse a Company-wide answer for protected field exceptions. Future acceptance must prove list/detail/Action/derived-result parity and bounded work for the 6-Company, 2000-person pilot. Choose concrete query/filter plans from inspected owners in the implementation design; no new role engine, LLM authorizer or speculative service is required here.

AM1.13 Entity visibility and property-level access
User-confirmed distinction: authorization may cover the entire entity or restrict selected information within a visible entity, e.g. an executive's personnel card is visible but some sensitive properties are not. Separate authorized entity discovery/read from property or field-group disclosure and from each mutation/decision Action. Entity permission does not imply every property; a property grant does not by itself reveal an otherwise undiscoverable entity. Company RLS alone supplies neither distinction. Any exceptional blind/opaque-reference use must be an explicitly reviewed owner Action, not an inferred shortcut.
Reuse the SAME canonical entity ID, owner and UI component structure. The server returns only currently authorized properties and permitted actions. When the property schema/existence is itself disclosable, distinguish unavailable-by-policy from unknown, empty and temporarily failed data; show a neutral restricted marker and the eligible request route without exposing the raw value, length, digest or change timing. When existence/schema is not disclosable, omit the field/section entirely; don't publish a list of hidden keys in a redaction manifest. Client hydration payloads, initial SSR HTML, error messages and caches must not contain the hidden values.
Relationships are disclosure surfaces too. Showing a person does not automatically permit traversing their contract, salary, medical attachment, related message thread or another Company's Employment. Check the link/edge and target as applicable; omit unauthorized relationship identifiers, labels, counts and preview snippets. Historical revisions, diffs and audit readback must enforce current property disclosure as well, so hiding today's value does not expose yesterday's through history. Immutable owner evidence remains retained under its own policy, not rewritten to match a viewer.
Editing a permitted property uses a typed owner command containing only intended editable fields and expected versions. Omitted/restricted fields mean unchanged, never null/clear; reject supplied unauthorized field edits instead of silently stripping them and reporting success. Do not round-trip a redacted entity as a full replacement record. Server validation/calculation may depend on hidden authoritative facts only under the owner use case's explicit contract, with nonleaking errors; missing required human-review evidence prevents that decision, as AC1.9 specifies. Read, create/edit, decide, export and delegate remain separate permissions, including per-property write restrictions.

AM1.14 Linked objects in communications; reference is not a grant
User clarification: consider confidential document entity links in chat and state-changing actions reached from read-only views. Permission to read/send in a thread does not imply permission to discover the linked entity, view its properties or execute its Actions. Sending, forwarding, quoting, pinning, editing or exporting a message must not mint a resource grant. Native links carry opaque reference identity, not reusable object-read/share bearer credentials or embedded signed storage URLs. Storing a link in a readable message cannot serialize the author's richer object snapshot into every recipient's message body.
Resolve object cards and their allowed properties per current recipient/resource policy at delivery/open/readback; the sender may see a rich card while another recipient sees an authorized minimal card or neutral unavailable reference. If object existence/title is not disclosable, do not show title, path, type, owner, thumbnail, size, confidential label or a distinguishable denial reason. A cached preview generated for the sender/channel cannot be reused as the recipient's projection. Current policy also covers quotes, forwards, pinned views, thread summaries, mention suggestions, chat search, mobile/push/email notifications, history/export and attachment download. The comms owner may store stable object refs; the target owner remains the truth/disclosure authority.
External email recipients or anonymous link scanners receive no private object preview or data simply because a valid internal URL was present. Email templates and transport previews must not bake a private entity title/thumbnail/token into the externally readable body. Authorized external sharing is a SEPARATE owning Action with its own share/delegate permission, exact recipient/resources/fields/actions, purpose, validity and required approval. Approved sharing creates only that narrow time-bound grant/proof with audit; thread-send permission or object-read permission is insufficient. A share record cannot exceed issuer delegation authority, bypass confidentiality forbid or serve as unbounded onward-delegation authority. Redemption and each subsequent access validate current scope/expiry/revocation and recipient/account binding under the common pipeline.
Revocation/expiry stops future authorized server disclosure and clears managed client projections/caches on revalidation; already viewed, copied, downloaded, printed or emailed plaintext cannot be remotely unlearned. Native links preserve future enforcement better than pasted snapshots but cannot guarantee prevention of intentional manual leaks. Readable clipboard screenshots are not magically controlled by an export permission bit. Controlled export/share paths require their own policy and receipt; do not claim absolute exfiltration prevention or invent an LLM DLP system.

AM1.15 Read-only views and mutation entry points
View read, property read, Action prepare/preflight, execute, bulk execute, share/delegate and export are separate server-evaluated permissions. A screen described as read-only supplies no write capability. A person may independently have a narrow Action permission in a generally read-focused workspace; show that Action only if its exact target/current state and required evidence are authorized. The current read-only case with no such grant has no mutation path. Client buttons, frontend routes, role strings, prepared responses and AuthorizedWorkspaceManifest are not execution authority.
Every state-changing request resolves the authenticated actor and exact target at the owning use case, checks current resource/action/property permissions and business invariants, and uses the same command/version/receipt/idempotency contract regardless of REST, server function, object card quick action, keyboard shortcut, bulk toolbar, mobile client, sheet write-back, job/automation or replay entry. Session-authenticated mutation endpoints require the existing request-integrity/CSRF protections; no state change on GET, prefetch, link scanner or object-preview fetch. Audit/access telemetry and a separately scoped personal read marker are not approval or business-state mutation and confer no document write permission.
Prepare/preflight is nonmutating and does not expose hidden data; an earlier successful preflight does not authorize execute after policy revocation, resource revision change or session expiry. Revalidate under the owner guard at execution. Any policy-issued prepared witness is exact-command bound and still subject to current guards. Denied/stale commands produce no business effect, gate consumption, task completion or success notification. Exceptions only record a bounded security diagnostic according to logging policy. Response loss after commit is an unknown-result recovery case, not license to retry under a new identity/command or claim denial undid the committed result.
Bulk and automation cannot elevate the initiator via a more privileged worker. Each target/effect needs the approved human-intent/workload-principal contract, current exact scope and required disclosure/decision evidence. Background recomputation and scheduled operations must use their explicitly authorized owner contract; they cannot treat a viewer's open screen or saved sheet as a mutation grant. Cross-Company bulk remains separate Company effects with per-target outcomes and no implicit group RLS cell. Complete partial-success/unknown-attempt handling remains B03/B04.
Full surface inventory is required before activation. access-path-matrix.csv records concrete positive, deny, revoke and no-effect oracles for current and future entry types. This is a design checklist with explicit owner and scope, not a claim that all paths are implemented or exhaustively verified. Comms/sharing/sheet code and whole-suite activation remain subject to their own admitted design/test slices.

AM1.16 SSR as the disclosure boundary
User observation: because Console is SSR, the server can strictly control what information is supplied. Adopt permission-aware server composition as the primary delivery boundary: resolve current Account/resource/property rights, obtain the authorized owner projection, then render only that permitted view model. Keep one reusable entity component and schema, but never serialize its unauthorized properties. A field absent from the visible DOM must also be absent from page source, data attributes, hydration/island payloads, inline state, script/JSON blocks, error/debug output, accessibility labels, link metadata and streaming chunks. Do not send a full entity and rely on CSS, a client-side flag or JavaScript to redact it.
The first byte containing protected information must follow the authorization decision; streaming cannot emit an optimistic private component and retract it later. SSR caches and CDN/intermediary behavior must not reuse one user's richer projection for another. Authenticated sensitive responses use private/no-store until a specifically reviewed policy-aware cache strategy proves isolation and freshness. Cache keys/invalidation, where enabled, include the relevant Account, Company/resource/property scope and policy/assignment/entity versions; revocation and expiry constrain reuse. Do not cache an authorized snapshot forever merely because the object revision is unchanged.
Server ownership and API access remain necessary after initial render. Hydration fetches, server functions, object-card previews, search, downloads and future subscription updates obtain current authorized DTOs from the same pipeline. Client caches purge revoked managed data on revalidation; bytes already legitimately received cannot be recalled by SSR. SSR controls supplied data; it does not itself authorize mutations, protect an unguarded backend endpoint, or prevent a recipient from copying information already disclosed. Treat those as separate concrete boundaries in AM1.13-AM1.15, not reasons to abandon server composition.
Use schema-derived property groups and current owner projections to share enforcement between list, detail and SSR. Avoid an unbounded per-cell graph walk or an LLM judgment per render. Exact projection/query plans, streaming behavior and cache configuration must be demonstrated in the admitted Leptos vertical, including raw HTTP/HTML/payload evidence, not only a screenshot or DOM visibility assertion.
