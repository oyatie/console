User-confirmed invariant EC1 — account continuity and post-employment access
Current architecture is AM1 in access-model.txt: one Account and one Cedar resource/action pipeline for internal and external relations. Personal area is navigation, not a separate account type or permission system.
User instruction (verbatim): 로그인 계정은 퇴사를 하더라도 유지가 되어야해. 증명서 발급을 퇴사 이후에도 여기서 가능하도록 할거야.
This controls over any earlier assumption that offboarding deactivates the login account. It expands the current design revision to the required former-worker path; it does not authorize production or a legal certificate claim.

EC1.1 Separate lifecycles and ownership
Durable Account: authenticated personal identity/credentials/security status, independent of current employer or Employment. CompanyActor: Company attribution anchor/history, not a grant; scoped business entitlements are evaluated separately. Employment: employer-specific effective-dated relationship. SelfServiceEntitlement: server-verifiable right to access specified own historical records/actions. Ending Employment affects the relevant work authorizations; it does not delete/disable Account or all personal credentials, erase historical Person/Employment binding, or require another employee to recreate access.
Existing user UUIDs remain stable through the Account-root migration selected in AM1.3. Account exists without an employer; Company origin and old users rows are legacy profile/provenance, not login ownership. Employment/CompanyActor/historical ownership are scoped relationships and never distinct login types.

Company admin may end Company work eligibility and company-sponsored credential/session access according to policy. A tenant UserManage grant alone must not disable durable personal login, remove user-owned passkeys, reset the global account, deny another Company's rights or erase post-exit access. Actual account compromise may trigger an identity-security account suspension and session/credential revocation; this retains account identity/history and offers independent recovery. That security operation is not the employment-offboarding action.

EC1.2 Correct handling of existing deactivation and credential routes
Source evidence: identity/rest/src/lib.rs:3205 routes Company UserManage to deactivate_user; identity/adapter-postgres/src/lib.rs:475 documents credential sweep and implements users.is_active=false plus passkey/session revocation. This behavior is NOT safe to reuse for Employment departure under the confirmed requirement.
Introduce explicit identity.revoke_company_work_access (attribution/grant owner) and employment.end (existing Employment owner semantics) with linked receipts and a durable departure intent. The Employee/Employment end is the canonical effective-dated fact; do not create a second HR writer. Payroll/approvals/assignment owners consume current facts for their own eligibility. Do not assume editing an Employment object already disables every session.
For the updated lifecycle, tenant routes labeled '퇴사 처리' or '업무 접근 종료' must call scoped actions and never the global credential sweep. Retain the existing sweep as a real account-security action restricted to account owner/explicit identity-security authority with step-up and recovery evidence; ordinary Company UserManage is insufficient. Legacy deactivate/activate routes require explicit compatibility handling: retire their employment use and return a documented replacement action; do not silently relabel global credential deletion as business revocation. Existing security regressions must remain for the account-security operation; semantic test changes go through independent test review, not weakened expectations to pass rollout.
Likewise review credential reset/recovery/enrollment, users.is_active/is_active filters, account delete and employee unlink paths. The existing admin OTP issuance (auth-rest:1259) and admin_credential_reset (:1337) can confer account takeover; separate these from Company SubordinateUserCreate before departure as well as afterward. Leaving an employer-controlled reset route active while preserving passkeys would defeat personal ownership. Employer access to a person's home-company storage adapter is not personal-account takeover authority. Company's federation/managed device may cease functioning after departure; personal recovery/passkey enrollment is independent of a corporate email inbox or current Company admin approval. If user has only corporate SSO, surface enrollment of a user-controlled authenticator while employed and provide identity-owned re-verification recovery after exit. Failure to enroll beforehand cannot permanently erase the account or require an active Employment to recover.

EC1.3 Effective departure and surviving rights
Prepare departure intent with account/Company/Employment refs, exact effective instant, reason/evidence, affected grants/role assignments and tasks. System derives work grants tied to that Employment; unrelated independent roles (e.g. a separately valid consulting capacity) must be evaluated by policy and not accidentally erased. After effective end, no new work effect may rely on the ended Employment; current evaluation includes validity even if an asynchronous cleanup job is late. Maintain separate version dimensions: account_security_generation belongs to the durable Account, while Company-scoped relationship/work_generation and target assignment/policy versions fence Company work. Bump only work_generation/affected work grants on employment end; never reuse the home Company subject_authz_versions.session_generation as both personal-security and work-revocation counter. Existing mixed readers/writers must be migrated before activation. Personal refresh/session families bind account-security generation; Company token exchange and work token validation additionally bind live target work generation. A Company work revocation cannot sweep personal families merely because home Company equals the departed employer. Actual account compromise may increment account-security generation and revoke all personal sessions under its separate authority.
Account login remains active. Target Company's unrelated personal self-service scope is resolved anew through historical ownership, never by leaving PayrollRunManage or GroupAdmin active. CompanyActor remains an attribution record; relevant work relationships/grants may be REVOKED without making that record a universal eligibility flag. Neither verified human identity nor historical employee/Person ownership binding is revoked merely because Employment ended; actual identity conflict/security revocation remains separate. Explicit work grants based on another valid relationship must be independently authorized; employment end is not a universal read/write permit or universal account death.
At effective end, linked draft/approval tasks must be handed to eligible replacements or surfaced blocked without moving historical actor identity. Pending decisions from departed work capacity are cancelled/stale and current owner guards refuse them; existing committed receipts remain. Full automated assignment/unknown-attempt orchestration is still B04 and cannot be claimed implemented by account preservation.
Rehire creates/reactivates an appropriate NEW Employment/capacity under current policy while reusing the same account and verified person. Old Company grants, pending gates and tokens do not reactivate automatically. Multiple Companies/Employments and partial departures are explicit cases.

EC1.4 Common Account pipeline and historical discovery
Use the same authenticated Account and resource-scoped Cedar pipeline as any other audience (AM1.2/AM1.8). Roleless authentication succeeds; current verified ownership of the selected historical Employment grants only the permitted own-record Actions/fields. No ACTIVE business CompanyActor, branch membership, GroupAdmin or current Employment is required. Personal workspace is a projection of those rights, not a permanent separate principal/audience that forbids otherwise authorized work. Protocol-version/audience fencing during migration prevents legacy handlers from confusing authentication with Company authority; new endpoints resolve current scopes uniformly.

Proposed entry '/_ui/me/employment-records' lists only the caller's authorized current/former Employment records, labelled by issuing Company and actual period/status. The server resolves Account -> verified subject -> allowed historical Company/Employment refs; client UUIDs are selection hints and rechecked, never authority. For multiple tenants, each issuer evaluates only its own relationship/evidence; no global HR directory or cross-tenant person join is exposed. Group identity binding revocation/conflict may require re-verification of own-record ownership, not silently grant access by name/email. It does not erase the account or original relationship.
Data remains in issuer Company ownership/RLS cells. Read each permitted target through its owner's same-Company connection and resource/field policy; don't use a Group GUC or broaden generic employee APIs. Return the minimum DTO for certificate choices and status: historical Employment reference, issuer display label when authorized, employment dates/status, available certificate type refs, request/result refs and eligible action/reason. Do not include co-workers, internal notes, unrelated salary data, organization directory, managerial inbox or Company settings. Unauthorized targets return a nondisclosing result; an authorized own record with temporarily missing source shows data unavailable and a correction/support route.
Former workers may see '나의 기록' as their landing workspace after login; existing employees retain their authorized workspaces. End of work access clears revoked work props/caches and re-resolves self-service while preserving the personal session. Do not strand them on a permanently forbidden Company dashboard.

EC1.5 Certificate object and input flow
CertificateType is a versioned issuer-owned typed object, not unrestricted user text: supported facts, permitted purpose/fields, template/version, signer/issuer policy, required source revisions, allowed self-service action, auto-issue eligibility and validity/retention policy. Start with employment-history certificate semantics appropriate to CURRENT versus ENDED Employment; a former worker must not receive a misleading currently-employed certificate. The list uses available type objects; adding a type is governed configuration rather than a hardcoded universal employment enum. Qualified legal/template authority is separate from this UX/design contract.
Proposed self-service actions: request_certificate, issue_certificate, request_certificate_correction, retrieve_issued_certificate. Business owner is the existing identity application/adapter surface that owns employee profile orchestration, extended with a certificate use case; canonical Employment/Person remain the owners of the referenced facts. Certificate issuance writes new certificate records only, never updates Employment or wages. A future extracted HR certificate owner must be an explicit reviewed ownership move, not an alternate writer. The targeted source search found no demonstrated existing employment-certificate path; these endpoints/records are proposals.
User path: login -> 나의 기록 -> select former Company/Employment -> 증명서 발급 -> select eligible CertificateType -> choose only policy-supported purpose/field options -> inspect exact facts/issuer/template and request. Existing identity/dates/Company/position facts auto-fill. For complete eligible source facts, policy may deterministically issue immediately; no new human approval is invented for every request. If facts are missing/conflicted or issuer policy needs review, preserve the request and show currently authorized responsible assignment, state and next action; user need not search for a departed HR officer or repeat their employment history.
Request input: command_id, employment_ref, certificate_type_ref/version, supported typed purpose/field choices, expected preview/source digest; no free-form replacement dates or employer name that become certified facts. Correction request input includes wrong field selector, proposed value/evidence reference and reason, creates an owning review request without editing authoritative facts. Result is an immutable CertificateIssue with issuer Company, subject Employment/verified account ownership, exact source versions/digest, template/policy/signing identity, request/command receipt, issued_at, artifact hash and authorized retrieval reference. Repeated same-command request replays its result; a changed certificate type/fields or later reissue is a new command, linked to earlier issue where relevant.

EC1.6 Issuance, queue and fallback states
Separate CertificateRequest state (REQUESTED, NEEDS_INFORMATION, IN_REVIEW, READY_TO_ISSUE, ISSUED, CANCELLED, FAILED_RETRYABLE) from immutable CertificateIssue and its revocation/correction history. Auto-issue uses the same owning action and policy as reviewed issuance; the eligibility rule is deterministic, not LLM judgement. 'ISSUED' requires durable artifact/signature/result evidence, not a successful queue enqueue or a browser-generated PDF.
For artifact rendering/signing outside the DB, use a persisted issuance intent and stable issue_id before dispatch, idempotent artifact key, immutable rendered hash and guarded finalize. Losing a response does not create another certificate; reconcile the intent/result. Revocation of request authorization before finalize blocks new issuance; already issued artifacts remain historical results with current retrieve rights checked. Detailed worker scheduling and signer integration require their own reviewed delivery contract; do not label them implemented here.
Missing facts route to the issuer's current policy-eligible certificate task queue, not a hardcoded original employee. A currently authorized replacement receives the request and its permitted context through current scope. If no authorized issuer exists, show '발급 담당 연결 필요' with durable request and operator-assisted routing; Console support cannot fabricate Company facts or sign as that Company. A user may cancel an unissued request or supply requested evidence. Denial/withholding legality and deadlines require qualified rules; workflow error is not a legal refusal.
Any declined gate or factual conflict must preserve which fact is disputed and the correction path. Once the fact owner approves correction, re-resolve source versions and create a newly reviewed issue. Previously issued artifact is not overwritten; new issue and invalidated/superseded reference remain linked. Certificate errors cannot be fixed by hand-editing PDF bytes behind the recorded hash.

EC1.7 Retrieval, sharing and account security
Former worker retrieves only their own permitted issued artifact through current identity verification and issuer resource policy. Normal download produces a document the user can save; do not imply a downloaded PDF can be remotely unread. Internal object links remain access-controlled, not public storage paths. External online verification/sharing, if offered, requires the existing governed narrow/time-bound sharing contract and audit; possession of an internal ID/email link alone reveals no personal fields. Exact recipient/public verification policy is a separate reviewed capability, not automatically enabled by certificate issuance.
Work-credential revocation after employment end must not invalidate personal passkeys or solely user-controlled recovery. Account-security suspension does block self-service pending recovery and preserves history; former employer cannot lift or impose that global suspension through UserManage alone. Recovery re-establishes the same Account/subject via the identity owner, never a new fake active Employment. Record recovery evidence with narrow visibility and current assurance requirements for document retrieval.

EC1.8 Account-owned storage and issuer records
AM1.3 selects the Company-independent Account root and an explicit credential/session/actor-FK transition. Preserve IDs and personal authentication continuity; never re-home through destructive DELETE/INSERT or a platform-admin sentinel. Company cleanup must not delete accounts/credentials merely by legacy origin. Until all dependent record/custody paths migrate, hard-delete is blocked rather than risking surviving accounts/history. Account continuity does not imply every issuer document is retained indefinitely or that Console can sign for a dissolved Company; issuer custody/retention and successor authority remain separately governed.

EC1.9 Required positive and negative acceptance
Positive: end A Employment -> same personal account/passkey still logs in -> A work actions and old work tokens denied -> own historical A Employment appears -> eligible certificate request auto-issues under authorized synthetic issuer policy -> exact artifact/receipt retrievable. Repeat after all Employments ended. B work entitlement remains if independently valid. Rehire reuses account without reviving A old grants. Account compromised -> self-service suspended -> identity recovery restores same account without employer account recreation.
Negative: Company admin tries global deactivate/reset/delete; former worker guesses peer Employment/certificate IDs; corporate-email-only recovery cannot complete without alternative verified recovery; stale source/template between preview and issue; issuer removed during queued request; response lost after issue; duplicate command, source correction, actor departure and signer failure. Keep issued vs queued truth explicit. Compare personal credential count/key identity before/after departure and prove no user.is_active=false/global session sweep was caused by employment end. Existing credential-sweep security tests remain on the separately authorized account-security path.
This is now a user-confirmed design invariant and a concrete former-worker journey. Full schema/auth-admin migration, certificate rendering/signing/external-sharing details and qualified template authority still require their own reviewed tests/implementation; no user-facing claim of live legal issuance is made.
