Proposed contract AI1 — one account, Company acting capacity, verified Group identity
Addresses B01 and B09. Source-backed design proposal, not an implemented security claim or production enrollment authorization.

AI1.1 Chosen ownership and rejected alternatives
Account lifecycle, account-owned storage and one common policy pipeline are selected in access-model.txt AM1. Existing users UUIDs map unchanged to Account IDs; Company origin rows are compatibility/provenance only. No employer-owned login, persona-token partition, fake subsidiary user or platform sentinel is the selected design. CompanyActor references the Account root and grants nothing by itself. AM1 replaces earlier home-Company storage/principal assumptions.

Extend existing identity domain/application/adapter as the sole writer of Company attribution anchors and verified identity-evidence bindings. Existing canonical Person owner retains persons/person_revisions/employee_person_bindings; identity's new registry is a verified equivalence index over their composite references and login accounts, not another writer of HR profile or Employment facts. No new generic ontology engine or ObjectKey::Group is implied. Admission of this Group-scoped identity control data must be explicit in the later authority/schema candidate.
Choose company_actors as an explicit attribution/acting-capacity anchor keyed (org_id,account_id), with FK account_id->accounts(id), FK org_id->organizations(id), immutable original admission reference and created_at. Scoped authorization eligibility/status and monotonic versions belong to the policy-owned relationship/grant, not a universal active-workforce flag on this attribution row. Historical anchors are never deleted because a grant expires or a user leaves. The row is NOT an Employment, target-company login, credential store or feature grant. One real account may have anchors in multiple same-tenant Companies.
Creating an attribution anchor uses the identity owner. An explicit administrative admission requires current Company identity-admission permission and verified scoped eligibility. Alternatively, an owning applicant/own-record/engagement Action with a currently verified entitlement may invoke a narrowly scoped identity-owner ensure-attribution helper as part of its effect; the helper may create only that authenticated actor's anchor in the exact target Company after owner-verified entitlement, not arbitrary users/Companies. It grants no identity-administration or workforce permission to the actor. Registration alone or an arbitrary UUID/visit is insufficient. Record the authorizing owner action, Company, entitlement/evidence version and immutable receipt; changed payload conflicts. Group-wide admission is one separately authorized option, not a prerequisite for every participant. Relationship revocation/suspension blocks only effects relying on that relationship; actual Account or identity-proof suspension blocks the actions that require that security/assurance proof. Preserve attribution history independently.


AI1.2 Persistence and migration boundary
Retarget ONLY actor/subject-attribution composite FKs needed by the approved cross-Company slice to company_actors(org_id,account_id), preserving account ID values, Company isolation, RESTRICT/history semantics and source-owner write boundaries. A row's Company still binds its business objects; every org_id in payroll data, run, source and receipt remains one legal Company. Anchor FK existence is necessary attribution integrity, never sufficient authorization.
Minimum inspected surfaces: ont_action_command_receipts.actor_id (0177); employee_contract_wages.created_by (0210); gov_approval_requests.requested_by (0158); gov_approvals.requested_by/approver_id and used governance actor/config writes (0153); gov_approval_consumptions.consumed_by (0164); user_role_assignments.user_id and policy_assignment_preview_receipts.user_id (0065); subject_authz_versions.user_id (0096). Existing audit_events.actor and payroll lifecycle submitter/decider/resolver FKs are global users(id), not composite. They are not the old foreign-home-user blocker, but must ALSO migrate to accounts(id) before a new Account-only actor can write those surfaces. Preserve UUIDs and add relevant scoped attribution proofs; no dummy legacy users row.
Workflow initiated_by/claimed_by/completed_by FKs require corresponding review before B03/B04 Group orchestration is enabled; they are not silently fixed by payroll changes. Canonical Person actor FKs similarly remain explicit dependencies if a cross-Company HR write is added. Capture actual catalog FK inventory for the admitted write/readback closure at migration-test time; not all user-referencing tables are actor tables.
Authentication credentials, refresh tokens and bootstrap/passkeys migrate to Account-owned security custody under AM1.3; do NOT retarget them to company_actors. Legacy physical origin fields are not an employer-admin access path. Target employee ownership FKs remain employees/Employment/Person Company FKs. Company identity admission and payroll membership are independent.
Backfill one origin-Company attribution anchor per existing users row while preserving IDs. Preserve inactive work eligibility in the corresponding scoped relationship/grant history; do not convert legacy inactivity into global Account suspension or make the attribution anchor a workforce grant. Backfill existing local grants/freshness references against those anchors before constraint swap. This is attribution backfill only: it must NOT automatically mark accounts as different verified humans. Validate FK coverage with exact old/new catalog counts and negative cross-tenant attempts under runtime RLS. No unmapped row is dropped or assigned to a service identity to make validation green.

AI1.3 Live Principal and consented acting capacity
During migration only, validate existing signed actor_home_org provenance against the legacy mapping before accepting legacy delegated tokens; a missing required legacy claim requires token refresh. New Account sessions neither need nor invent a home Company. Resolve account security at the Account owner, never from a caller-selected Company or title.
The trusted server resolves ActorContext = authenticated Account + authorized target Company/resource + applicable tenant Group + attribution reference when the owner requires it + Account security generation + target policy/relationship/grant versions + applicable access scope + Group identity proof for actions requiring natural-person assurance. Existing signed actor_home_org is legacy token provenance only; new Account-only sessions need no home Company. The uniform AM1 pipeline governs all audiences. No field is a client assertion of authority. Live account enabled/session valid is checked through the identity-owned Account adapter through a narrow identity-only reader; for each new effect require current authorization for the exact resource/action, applicable property permissions and the specific relationship/evidence on which that authorization relies. Active workforce capacity, branch membership or Group delegation is required only if the selected action/authorization depends on it. Account-only applicant, historical own-record and explicitly authorized external actions require neither workforce eligibility nor Group delegation merely to be business effects. A required CompanyActor supplies attribution only. Former-worker own-record access uses the same Account/resource pipeline with historical ownership described in continuity-and-certificates.txt and must not require ACTIVE business capacity or current Employment. A missing target freshness row is not assumed a valid zero baseline after this capability is enabled; create/version it through the owning admission/grant transaction and fail closed if unexpectedly absent.
A Company or Group capability alone does not authorize any payroll amount read/write. Reuse domain PayrollRunManage/Read and current Cedar/PBAC action/evidence decisions. Keep original actor; do not derive target Company's built-in users.roles or team by pretending a target users row exists. Existing target-user inner joins in role-management preview/assignment, natural-person resolver and actor display are replaced with narrow anchor/identity projections where this slice needs them; no fallback to home roles granting target payroll power.
Credential/self-recovery endpoints remain Account-owner operations with independent policy and assurance checks. A Company work grant cannot reset personal credentials or transfer account ownership. Identity-security account suspension/logout/session revocation, target relationship revocation, assignment change and Group removal deny future effects whose authorization relies on that relationship; employment end is not identity-security account suspension; each is independently tested. Loss of one Company grant does not silently revoke unrelated grants, nor reveal their existence.

AI1.4 Group natural-person identity registry
Identity owns group_human_subjects keyed (group_id,human_id), with opaque human IDs and append-only versioned verification/status history. Keep it tenant-scoped, not a global public directory. group_identity_bindings bind authenticated account_id and/or canonical (company_id,person_id) to exactly one current VERIFIED human subject in that Group. A person can have multiple accounts/Employment records; every alias resolves to the same human. Historical bindings persist when superseded. Account/Person binding uniqueness plus expected-current-version CAS prevents concurrent divergent assignments. Conflict sets all affected subjects/bindings SUSPENDED for new high-risk actions until explicitly resolved.
Verification evidence contract: approved identity-verification-policy version; trusted verifier/issuer identity; issuer's opaque unique natural-person subject key (not a login/IdP account key); affected account-ownership proof; canonical Person refs if present; evidence case reference/content hash; method and decision; verifier actor context; validity interval; retention-policy reference; verified_at. Unique(group_id,issuer_id,issuer_human_key) prevents issuing two active Group humans for the same trusted subject. A different issuer/key is not automatically proof of a different human: enrollment requires the verifier to certify uniqueness under the SAME selected pilot issuer registry, or a reviewed explicit equivalence record. The pilot uses one designated Console-supervised verification registry per Group. Do not mix issuers until equivalence policy is separately reviewed.
This registry is a trust root backed by supervised identity verification, not mathematical proof from UUIDs. Names, email, phone, document OCR, embeddings and different IdP subjects cannot certify independent humans. Evidence hashes prove custody, not the truth of a human claim. Keep raw identity evidence in an access-controlled evidence case under its owning storage/retention policy; runtime receives opaque subject IDs, assurance status and versions, not resident registration numbers or identity documents. No new LLM identity matching is required.

AI1.5 Enrollment, verification, bootstrap and correction
For pilot, name the operational responsibility of proposing bindings (registrar) and verifying bindings (verifier). These describe distinct action responsibilities, not seeded job titles, ranks or mandatory permission-role records. A currently authorized proposer proposes typed account/Person bindings against the single approved registry, and a separately authorized verifier confirms source evidence and account ownership. Their accounts must resolve to two already VERIFIED different human subjects, and neither may approve a binding for themselves or use the candidate's unverified identity to satisfy independence. Capabilities are separate Cedar actions: propose binding, verify binding, suspend binding, correct binding; no blanket HR/Group admin implied permission. UI: choose existing person/account; choose existing evidence case; system preloads Company and known refs; verifier sees exact proposed links/differences and conflict reasons. Free-text reason supplements typed facts; it never creates the link by inference.
First two operators cannot recursively certify themselves. Before tenant-side verification is enabled, Console-operated provisioning creates an immutable TenantIdentityRootReceipt using an out-of-band supervised identity review: named registry/policy, two specific account IDs, two verified issuer-human keys, accountable external provisioning reviewer(s), evidence/retention refs and creation timestamp. Receipt is scoped to one Group and single-use initialization, may enroll only those named initial subjects, cannot grant payroll authority, and has no general tenant upload/import or magic verified=true endpoint. Subsequent users use normal registrar/verifier Actions. Initial provisioning identities and evidence are operational inputs still requiring actual launch authority; this DESIGN chooses the mechanism, not the actual humans or legal qualification. Synthetic test provisioning supplies explicit fixture roots and exercises subsequent enrollment actions; no test may silently set an arbitrary payroll source/account to verified through SQL to bypass this workflow.
Account continuity, subject uniqueness, and different-human verification are distinct evidence fields. If the registry's operational procedure cannot warrant its unique human keys, high-risk approval remains blocked rather than labelling two local UUIDs 'verified'. This limitation must be visible during setup and counted as an unmet readiness prerequisite.
Correct/merge/split are controlled identity-owned revisions with expected versions, exact affected aliases, evidence and registrar/verifier independence. No automatic merge by string similarity; no deletion of history or silent retarget. Merging aliases uses one surviving human identity and marks affected prior approvals for prospective integrity checks. Splitting requires re-verification of each resulting binding under the registry; a suspended source cannot verify itself. Emergency suspend is allowed to a currently authorized identity responder without waiting for the normal verifier because it removes prospective privilege; restoration/correction always requires independent verification. If fewer than two eligible verifiers remain, enter a recorded recovery case through the original supervised provisioning authority, with new out-of-band evidence; never temporarily disable SoD, grant SUPER_ADMIN or use a new self-certified identity. Recovery does not rewrite prior receipts or restore revoked permissions automatically.
Retention: enrollment requires a valid typed evidence-retention policy reference; enforce its retention_until/disposal rules and legal-hold state at the evidence owner. Do not invent a statutory retention period in this design. Remove raw documents according to that policy while retaining only permitted minimal attribution/proof history; if required proof cannot still be established for a NEW pending action, require re-verification. Historical receipt access is separately authorized; personal-data deletion and recovery cannot silently rewrite business effects.

AI1.6 Current proof, locking and revocation
Each NEW high-risk command resolves BOTH stored submitter/requester and current decider/verifier through current identity bindings. Store account, CompanyActor and human-subject IDs plus binding/proof versions in its immutable snapshot. Compare current verified human equality; a later merge of two subjects must invalidate pending 'different human' approvals even if the frozen UUIDs differ. Any relied-on identity version change marks pending command/gate stale and requires a fresh review; cancel/reprepare remains available to an independently authorized current manager. Completed effects remain historical facts; detected post-completion SoD defects mark downstream eligibility INTEGRITY_HOLD without pretending to reverse an already completed payment.
Authoritative read-modify-write boundary is the existing PostgreSQL instance. Do not hold a transaction in one Company while asking another service to mutate it. Within the payroll owner transaction, a narrow identity-owner helper resolves and SHARE-locks the exact Account/session guard, Group membership/identity guard and target CompanyActor/subject-policy guard rows; it returns bounded opaque proof/version results, no HR profile. Cross-scope helper is explicitly privileged for identity-only reads/locks with fixed search_path, parameter validation and revoked PUBLIC execution. Caller supplies server-verified ActorContext; no arbitrary user-selecting public endpoint. It never arms app.current_org with Group and never writes business data outside target Company.
Lock order for every participating new-effect/revocation path: Group identity guard -> account/session guards in sorted Account ID order -> target Company membership/CompanyActor/subject-policy guards in sorted IDs -> payroll period lock if required -> run heads in sorted run IDs -> source guard rows -> governance approval binding/consumption. Take shared locks for decisions, exclusive guard locks for changes; all grant/revoke/identity/session writers must join the protocol before activation. Existing helper lock order must be audited and adapted coherently; holding run lock and then taking an earlier guard is forbidden. Revocation and execution serialize: whichever acquires the relevant guard first defines the ordered outcome; after revocation commits, no not-yet-authorized command may commit on the old proof. Wall-clock expiry has a documented command authorization instant under these guards; no claim that a long transaction magically rechecks time at commit. Abort and restart commands that exceed their bounded owner execution deadline, never silently continue on an expired authorization lease.
No global Group lock for all payroll data writes is needed beyond the shared identity guard; six independent Company payroll transactions may hold shared proof guards concurrently. Identity mutation may briefly block them predictably. Include lock timeout/deadlock/serialization behavior in later runtime tests; retry same command only when no effect committed. A cached JWT/version, event-driven stale flag or postcommit notification alone cannot enforce this boundary.
Historical same-command replay first enforces current Account/resource disclosure boundary and exact immutable actor/action/payload binding, then returns historical receipt with separately authorized current status; it creates no new effect. Revocation may forbid disclosure, but it never converts an already committed command into 'never ran'. Successful current authentication alone cannot reveal old payroll amounts without current data permission.

AI1.7 Actor readback and continuation
Company-visible attribution reads from authorized company_actors plus minimal identity projection, not target users INNER JOIN. Display actual actor identity and, where permitted, acting capacity; show any relationship label only if field policy allows; legacy login-origin Company is not evidence of a current employer. Past actors remain readable as historical attributed IDs/approved display labels according to policy after account deactivation; no profile resurrection, credential lookup or foreign-HR browse.
Task handover changes responsibility for future work, not original command identity. A newly assigned manager can inspect permitted historical results and create their own successor commands under current authorization. They cannot replay another person's command as if they were its original author. Full durable unknown-attempt/takeover orchestration remains B04 and must be designed separately; this contract supplies real actor identity and current proof, not permission for a worker to impersonate a human.

AI1.8 Rollout, rollback, positive proof
Sequence: Account-root/security-custody transition from AM1.3 -> additive anchor/identity schema with owner/RLS/PD classification -> backfill local attribution -> create validated target subject/grant support -> migrate selected FK/readback closures -> ship uniform Account/resource context, legacy-token compatibility and current-liveness/proof guards -> enable supervised Group identity enrollment -> enable explicit bounded cross-Company actions only after one capability receipt proves all endpoints/readers/guards/migrations present. No silent fallback to account-only SoD for the new payroll contract.
Exact rollback: disable new cross-Company effects, retain anchor/proof/history and compatible readback. After first foreign-home actor receipt/wage write, restoring old (actor,org)->users(id,org) FKs is impossible without data loss and is forbidden. This is a forward-compatible expansion, not a promised reversible down migration. Account and credential custody follows AM1.3: after Account-only registration, retain Account-capable authentication/recovery even if new business effects are disabled. No Company-mandatory downgrade.
Mandatory positive scenario: Account with a B relationship with a legitimate A CompanyActor and A policy grant creates wage/run, submits and an independently verified human decides; target A owner writes and receipt commit under real original account ID, correct Company, correct proof. No fake A user, no B payroll changes and no hidden extra grants. Negative cases independently cover same-human aliases, missing anchor, active anchor/no grant, valid grant/revoked Account session, changed target freshness, removal of a relied-on Group grant, forged legacy home claim, unknown identity, concurrent bind forks, later identity merge, and old receipt after revoke. Existing legal/production authority and other B03/B04/B06/B07/B08 design gaps are not cleared.

AI1.9 Organization vocabulary
Registrar, verifier, submitter, decider, manager and sponsor describe responsibilities in the discussed action, not predefined job titles or rank hierarchies. AM1.9 governs authorization and navigation: company-defined scoped relationships and current policy determine who can perform them. No fixed HR/executive/site-manager role or title-to-permission map is introduced.
