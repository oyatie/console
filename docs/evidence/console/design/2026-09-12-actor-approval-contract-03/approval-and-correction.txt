Proposed contract AC1 — payroll approval roles and approved-draft correction
Scope: specifies decision/gate separation and approved-draft correction for further independent design review. B02 partial-visibility decision coverage remains open under AM1.10. Not implemented; no approved design/test SHA.

AC1.1 Distinct decisions and actors
S = natural person who submitted the exact payroll submission. D = natural person who intentionally decides that submission. Q = requester of an OPTIONAL policy-required permission to execute D's exact command. G = approver of that execution permission. Account IDs remain recorded separately from natural-person identity and target-Company acting capacity.
Default payroll decision requires verified S != D. It does not invent a second generic approval or a third human. A configured execution gate is additional: Q = D for a payroll decide command; verified Q != G. G may equal S unless that policy version explicitly requires otherwise. Always evaluate configured scoped relationship/field constraints (optional company-defined permission bundles are not fixed job titles); G cannot choose or replace D. Service/unknown identity never substitutes for S,D,Q,G. Same human through different accounts fails the relevant inequality.
A governance execution-gate approval does NOT move payroll SUBMITTED->APPROVED/REJECTED. Only payroll.decide_submission, executed by the payroll owner with D's current authorization and exact signed-off command, changes the business state. Governance owns its gate request/decision/consumption. Payroll owns submission and payroll decision. The common approval surface composes these distinct records and links; it does not merge them into an ambiguous APPROVED flag.
There is no gate by default merely because payroll is sensitive: existing required payroll S/D separation is unconditional; an additional execution gate comes only from the currently effective configured policy. This distinction must also be represented in seeds/runtime published action configurations, not assumed from dispatch registration.

AC1.2 Input and binding
Immutable PayrollSubmission identity: Company, draft_run_id, submission_id, submission_sequence, input_revision/digest, calculation_version/digest, exact roster/source manifest digest, rule identity, submitter account/CompanyActor/verified group-natural-person proof and proof version, policy version, submitted_at.
Prepare decision command: command_id, submission_id, expected_run_revision, expected_submission_digest, decision APPROVE|REJECT, reason (nonblank for REJECT), D's actor context derived by server. Payload digest covers ALL effectful fields including reason and exact target. No client-supplied identity/policy authority. The action instance_id may not silently fall back to object_type_id: payroll derives target submission_id/run from typed params and requires consistent route/instance/Company references or rejects.
When a generic execution gate is configured, its request binds Company, action dispatch, specific submission_id, requested command_id+payload digest, D/Q identity, input/calculation/submission/policy versions and allowed expiry. The optional gate witness is a reference to that exact immutable request/approval, not a free approved boolean. Existing target-only or object-type-only approvals cannot authorize this extended command. Changed effectful content creates a NEW intent/gate; no editing in place.

AC1.3 Input route and screen behavior
V07 calculated result -> '검토 요청' submits the immutable snapshot. V08 shows '급여안 검토' with actual submitter, proposed period/targets, exact amount differences, source versions and blocking issues; authorized reviewer sees '급여안 승인' and '수정 요청(급여안 반려)' with reason input for rejection. User-entered corrections never become approved amounts as a side effect of reviewing.
If no additional gate is required, D's decision executes now. If a gate is required, the same intent screen explains '이 결정을 실행하려면 추가 허가가 필요합니다', shows the currently eligible approval assignment and exact intended effect, then offers '실행허가 요청'. This stores an immutable intention and pending governance request; payroll remains SUBMITTED. The G screen is labeled '급여 결정 실행허가', shows D's requested decision and exact submission, and offers permit/refuse of THAT execution. It is never labeled as if G has already decided the payroll.
For this first contract, after gate approval D receives a ready-to-execute item and explicitly executes the exact decision. No background worker impersonation or automatic decision is required here; later automatic continuation must use the separately reviewed durable actor/intent contract B04. Preserve the pending intent and all references so D does not retype amounts/reasons. The gate can also expire/be refused/revoked; these events leave payroll SUBMITTED and display the next eligible human action. Normal no-extra-gate flow remains one decision action.
Authorized receipt replay precedes new-effect guards as D11 in prior candidate; a revoked source can invalidate current readiness but cannot hide an already committed decision from an otherwise currently authorized reader. If current disclosure permission is gone, omit details and give no hidden target/source names.

AC1.4 Transaction and authority
On new execution, use one target-Company payroll owner transaction: acquire actor/identity/policy guards in AI1.6 order, then lock payroll run; confirm active non-superseded exact submission; confirm current identity proof versions and live account/session and the specific relationship/domain authority required for this payroll effect; validate current source/roster/calculation and policy; verify required gates/checklists/reason; consume exact bound governance gate if required; append payroll decision and immutable owner receipt/audit; update run head. All commit or all roll back. The canonical wrapper must not preconsume that gate for this owner-atomic command.
An identity/policy mutation affecting authority must serialize with these checks by shared current-authorization/version guards in the same database; cached token versions or a hash alone do not lock current authorization. The concrete guard protocol is specified with actor contract; expired time windows checked against authoritative transaction execution time before commit. A replay of a committed receipt is not a new decision and cannot consume again. An unknown in-flight command is reconciled using same-command serialization; never mint another ID because response was lost.
Refused source/identity/policy/stale snapshot -> no decision/effect/consumption. User may open current authorized evidence, update their intent through a new explicit command, or ask an authorized submitter/replacement manager to cancel and reprepare. A source-invalid submission cannot be APPROVED or REJECTED through a stale decision; cancellation remains available with exact run/submission CAS and current Manage permission, preserving why review was stopped.
Assignment/policy changes cannot silently rewrite who submitted, requested or decided. A replacement D creates a new linked decision intent after the old intent is conclusively cancelled/expired and any execution is reconciled. This is not reuse of another person's command ID or gate. Full automatic actor takeover remains B04.

AC1.5 Approved-draft supersession action
Choose new explicit status SUPERSEDED, not overloading VOID or rewriting an earlier approval. Logical owning action payroll.supersede_approved_draft; owner existing payroll adapter/application boundary. It replaces only an APPROVED, still nonpayable draft with a newly created draft for the SAME Company/period/pay_date. It never replaces paid, issued, scheduled or otherwise externally committed payroll.
Input: command_id, predecessor_draft_run_id, expected_run_revision, expected_submission_id/digest, reason (required), readable immutable correction_evidence_refs, successor_correlation_run_id generated once per intention. UI entry V07/V08 on approved draft: '오류 정정 시작'. Preview names previous approval, current amounts and affected employees, states that old approval remains history and a new calculation/review is required; execute shows linked replacement draft. No manual typing of old targets/period/amounts is required.
Required current authority: PayrollRunManage AND policy action payroll.supersede_approved_draft, target evidence visibility, live actor context. Any extra execution gate comes from policy and binds this exact correction command; original approval is never permission for an unrelated correction. Supersession stops use of an old draft and creates an unapproved replacement, so it is not itself a new payroll amount approval. New successor submission will again require verified S != D.
Guard under predecessor run lock: status APPROVED; no outgoing supersession; exact reviewed predecessor identity; no payroll_disbursements row (including failed/scheduled attempts); no issued payslip/delivery; every predecessor calculation payable=false; evidence visible; current period/source policy permits a correction draft. A closed attendance/payroll period does not implicitly unlock: new draft may reference still-valid close evidence only after fresh prepare/close checks. Policy may block new preparation, but not silently lift a lock.
Store immutable PayrollRunSupersession relation: (org_id,predecessor_id) UNIQUE, (org_id,successor_id) UNIQUE, command_id, reason/evidence digest, actor proof, original_submission_id, occurred_at. Both composite FKs reference the SAME Company; predecessor != successor. The action only links a run it newly creates in THIS transaction, never an existing arbitrary run, so cycles and merged histories cannot be introduced. No generic graph writer.
In the same owner transaction, create a new staged successor through a payroll-owned header-allocation primitive separated from automatic roster materialization; do not call existing stage_draft_run_inner unchanged because it always materializes; assign its new correlation/source label once so the existing (org_id,period_start,period_end,source_label) natural key cannot return the predecessor. If creation reports an existing unrelated natural-key run, fail conflict and make no relation/state change. New successor starts STAGED, input_revision=0, no copied approval, no copied calculated/payable results. Carry over employee/reference selection as editable proposed defaults, not verified immutable inputs; fresh prepare re-resolves facts and excludes invalid memberships.
Append supersession edge+audit+command receipt containing both actual draft_run_ids. Set predecessor status SUPERSEDED, calculation_enabled=false, preserve all prior submission/decision/calculation/source history. Atomic commit makes exactly one successor eligible as current. The display status of the old immutable approval remains historical '승인됨'; run header says '정정안으로 대체됨' and links to successor. Existing schedule guard requires APPROVED; ALL existing and new reads/actions/materializers enforce the applicable no-outgoing-supersession fence. Existing create/drain natural-key retries currently call materialise_roster_for even when created=false; extend the payroll owner fence under the same prescribed guard/run-lock order so SUPERSEDED and other immutable approved history never refresh roster/source/verification/calculation fields. An identical historical create/drain retry returns historical receipt/status (and an optional currently authorized successor link) without materialization, state mutation or redirect-as-new-creation; different provenance still conflicts. Normal mutable draft restaging remains separately allowed under its own revision/state guards. Aggregate/list queries count only the active leaf, while history remains navigable with current permission.
Successor rejection does not reactivate predecessor. Correct that same successor through allowed reopen/reprepare and fresh submission; if a later APPROVED successor needs correction, the same action extends the chain. There is no 'restore old approval' shortcut or delete edge. One current chain leaf is structural via unique outgoing edge; duplicate independently created same-period payroll runs are a DIFFERENT existing product issue and cannot be assumed deduplicated by this relation. UI shows potential overlapping runs; submission admission must separately enforce the approved calculation-unit membership policy for overlapping runs.

AC1.6 Concurrency, receipts and recovery
Two supersession commands with the same expected predecessor: same-command replay returns the same pair; distinct command IDs race under the run lock, exactly one creates a successor, the other returns conflict plus currently authorized successor link. No orphan draft on rollback. A lost response after commit recovers through the original receipt.
Supersession vs schedule_disbursement: both lock predecessor run before effects. If scheduling wins, supersession is refused because status/disbursement changed; if supersession wins, schedule sees SUPERSEDED and is refused. Before enabling supersession, all release/attest/issue/payment entry paths and payable flips must be verified to use the same predecessor fence or be unavailable; independent tests must cover every reachable path, not only the scheduling example. No new payment capability is authorized by this contract.
An approved source that is later revoked makes old draft readiness stale; supersession remains available to an authorized manager to replace it without requiring the INVALIDATED old source to pass new-approval gates. The correction evidence/actor/current state are checked; successor preparation still needs new valid sources. Requiring the old bad tax row to verify before allowing correction would create an unrecoverable loop and is prohibited.

AC1.7 Migration and stop condition
Add SUPERSEDED to run status constraint/DTO mappings, retain calculation_enabled constraint by setting it false, create same-Company relation and indexes with append-only enforcement; update list/history/current-run projections and all payable/issue guards before any new supersession writes. Existing APPROVED history is not rewritten/backfilled as superseded. New schema accepts old rows.
Roll out expand schema/readers/guards first; enable action only when exact production binaries and gate checks cover all reachable paths. After first supersession, rollback may disable writes but must retain SUPERSEDED-aware readers and safety guards. Never deploy old readers that count the predecessor as current or remove relation/history to downgrade. Migration numbers/DDL/test files are selected only in the approved test/implementation sequence; these behavior/ownership constraints are fixed in the design.
Acceptance plan must include same-human dual-account rejection; S/D/Q/G matrix; target/digest substitution refusal; configured gate permit without payroll decision; approved old source revoked then valid correction; zero duplicated successors under concurrent calls; schedule-vs-correction two winners alternatives; postcommit response loss; predecessor immutable history and active-leaf aggregate; successor rejection remains correctable. Existing legal/payment/release HOLDs remain.

AC1.8 No occupational-role catalogue
S/D/Q/G name the participants of one action instance, not job titles, ranks or required seeded permission roles. Any actor meeting the current resource/action policy and assurance/independence constraints may fill that responsibility. AM1.9 applies; changing a title alone grants no decision or execution permission.

AC1.9 Required decision evidence and partial visibility
A human decision over the complete submission requires current access to all evidence/amounts that this decision contract requires. A preparer or reviewer with only a permitted subset cannot approve hidden amounts, infer them through totals, or produce whole-run approval from visible rows alone. Route remaining atomic work to a currently authorized actor, preserve permitted progress, and keep whole-run readiness incomplete. Partitioned review/coverage and confidential final aggregation need a separate exact B02/B07 contract; no existing full-run API is presumed safe for partial viewers. This is a remaining design gap, not an excuse to grant broader access or remove necessary information.
