PC1 — Payroll-owned exact review coverage with permission-scoped work
Status: proposed design; no implementation or legal/production claim. Extends candidate03 AM1/AI1/AC1. In UNIT_COVERAGE review, PC1 explicitly replaces AC1's single whole-run submission/decider assumption. AC1 exact intent, optional gate, different-human checks and supersession remain, now bound to each review proposal. Frozen prior files are not edited.

PC1.1 Source facts, ownership and choice
Current source lifecycle.rs:931 submit_run_in_tx sets one submitted_by on the run; :958 decide_run_in_tx compares that actor and updates the whole run. Migration0186 stores one submitted_by/decided_by and append-only per-line calculations. REST lifecycle.rs:1-53 uses org-wide PayrollRunManage, explicitly rejecting branch scopes. These paths are NOT proof of scoped approval. roster.rs materializes company-period imports with no selected-review partition. Candidate02 D02/D05 native input membership and candidate03 account/field policy are proposed prerequisite work, not existing implemented features.
Choose payroll-owned review proposals over existing immutable input/calculation units; retain ONE PayRun and its canonical salary calculation records. Do not create a new payroll engine, second money writer, workflow approval database or clone records per viewer. UI task grouping is separate from entity identity. The existing payroll domain/adapter lifecycle owns the new protocol; current payroll has no application crate, so do not claim such a crate exists. A coherent internal owner-use-case boundary may be added within that package before adapters converge; exact module placement belongs to the approved implementation design.
Alternatives rejected: (a) filtered whole-run decision permits blind approval; (b) split PayRuns according to today's viewers makes business identity depend on temporary permission/assignment and multiplies correction/overlap risks; (c) generic task-engine approval makes payroll depend on an alternate decision writer. If a company already has independently meaningful payroll runs, preserve them; PC1 does not force merging business units.

PC1.2 Calculation units, required population and work ownership
For the first supported kernel, a calculation/review coverage key is (Company, PayRun, period, employee, exact Employment), with one full-period Employment per employee as candidate02 D02/D04 already requires. A single person's net/gross/deductions and relevant evidence form one indivisible monetary decision obligation. Field-only contributors can prepare attendance or other source facts through their owning Actions without seeing salary, but that source work is not approval of the whole monetary obligation. Do not invent a 'partial sharing' responsibility type.
The payroll owner derives required population from the versioned, explicitly selected payroll scope and effective Company Employment facts, not the initiator's filtered employee list. Selection can be a scoped regular payroll domain (Company/site/department typed IDs and explicit descendants). Capture selection contract/version, membership query/effective-time evidence and nonempty exact resulting unit set R, plus typed excluded/unsupported cases. A protected person remains required even if the initiator cannot discover them. Unsupported/ambiguous eligibility blocks completion, never silently removes people or defaults liability to zero. A scoped operation must be labeled as that scope; it is not a claim that all Company payroll is complete.
Candidate02 already leaves independently created overlapping payroll runs unresolved. PC1 adds mandatory coverage reservation for this admitted REGULAR draft scope: one current reservation per (Company, employee, period) across active new-format regular runs. Reserve the full R atomically through payroll; reject conflicting overlapping ranges/current reservations, not just duplicate identical dates. Correction successors transfer their predecessor reservations atomically under AC1; history is retained. Legacy/independent runs must be inventoried for this period before enrollment; ambiguity is a blocker, not an assertion that no conflict exists. Off-cycle adjustment and multiple simultaneous Employment policies remain later supported-case work; no blanket dedup across those unknown semantics.
Each atomic preparation/decision task records at most one current accountable assignee and a version. Eligibility may include several people; assignment is not a permission grant. Explicit scoped assignments can be resolved automatically when a single eligible recipient exists. Ambiguous/no eligible recipient becomes a durable unassigned work item with an authorized manual assign/reassign Action, not a hidden default to a senior rank. General suitability/load-balancing and absence takeover remain B04; PC1 defines what must survive those transitions. UI may group many independently owned units into one worklist/batch without changing ownership or coverage.

PC1.3 Review evidence and durable records
A ReviewCycle is payroll-owned: Company/run, cycle_id/revision, immutable nonempty R and membership basis, exact required common-obligation set C (possibly empty), input/calculation snapshot refs, common required obligations, schema/rule/authorization-contract versions and state/history. A current head may advance under CAS; past cycles/membership remain immutable. Only one current cycle per run. Source values are referenced by immutable owner refs, never re-created in a review table.
A ReviewProposal has a typed target: Unit(coverage_key) or Common(obligation_definition_ref, scope_basis_ref). For Unit it identifies one coverage key and unit proposal revision within that same run; common-target identity and lifecycle are specified in PC1.11. Bind exact unit input/source versions, calculation line/version and result digest, required review-evidence projection contract, relied-on notes/evidence versions, shared dependency fingerprints, rule/schema versions, unit submission actor Account/human proof and explicit attestation. A current proposal has exactly one submitter S; attribution of earlier source contributors stays in its referenced evidence. Payload includes no caller-supplied S or arbitrary employee/Company substitution.
Review-evidence contracts specify which fields/facts are necessary for THIS decision: remuneration/basis, applicable deduction amounts and provenance, relevant attendance/Employment validity, deltas and unresolved issues. They do not demand unrelated personnel-card secrets such as raw resident ID, medical attachments or bank credentials merely because the entity contains them. When a reviewed source-owner attestation is explicitly sufficient, show that authorized attestation instead of leaking raw source material. The proposer/reviewer cannot self-select an easier evidence contract or treat redaction as equivalent proof. If required evidence is not available to an eligible actor, that obligation is blocked.
A Decision for either target binds Company/run/proposal ID and exact proposal digest, command ID, APPROVE|REJECT, reason when rejecting, D Account/current human proof and required optional gate consumption. S != D under current verified Group natural-person bindings. Customer policy can require additional contributor independence; PC1 does not infer it from titles. No active Employment/Group delegation prerequisite unless the exact authorization relies on it.
A CoverageCompletion receipt binds the exact ReviewCycle/R, one accepted current proposal+decision per key, common dependency/eligibility proof versions, membership scope, occurrence and owner derivation version. It is a deterministic payroll-owned evidence composition, NOT a new human decision by an automation account or last reviewer. Record the real causal command and all referenced human decisions; never fabricate submitted_by/decided_by to satisfy the legacy columns. Separate immutable historic completion from current readiness. All records are same-Company constrained and append-only except versioned current heads/assignment heads.

PC1.4 Inputs and transitions
Entry: my work / payroll scope -> permitted preparation tasks and known source facts -> correct permitted source data through owner Action -> calculate exact supported revision -> open review cycle. Preparing/closing/calculating a scope with hidden monetary inputs requires its own explicit operational Action permission; that permission does not reveal the inputs or attest them as human-reviewed. Existing whole-run employee/materialization/preflight endpoints cannot be handed to a scoped actor unchanged. Attendance close evidence stays attendance-owner/period-scoped, not a blind statement about hidden wages.
open_review_cycle(command_id,run_id,expected_input/calculation/membership versions) is a payroll owner operation explicitly authorized for the selected scope. It creates R/proposal drafts only after complete nonempty supported calculation and current close/source prerequisites; it does not submit or approve anyone's money. Run moves CALCULATED -> REVIEWING (new explicit status for new-format records). Outstanding data work remains before this point with durable typed blockers; no need to freeze every other employee's useful preparation.
submit_review_unit(command_id,proposal_id,expected_unit_revision,expected_basis_digest,attestation) requires current unit submission permission and all mandatory review evidence. It freezes S and the exact proposal, DRAFT -> SUBMITTED. A bulk submission references an explicit bounded set the actor can fully inspect. It never submits all hidden units merely because they belong to the same cycle.
decide_review_unit(command_id,proposal_id,expected_proposal_digest,decision,reason,optional_gate_ref) uses AC1 exact intent/owner-atomic governance consumption. If an extra execution gate exists, Q=D and Q!=G; permit of that gate is not a unit decision. D explicitly executes after gate permission in this slice. APPROVE yields one immutable accepted decision; REJECT yields one rejected proposal and a correction task with typed issue/evidence. Rejecting one unit does not reject, reset or resubmit unrelated units. Run stays REVIEWING until exact complete coverage.
Current unit view states: DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> APPROVED or REJECTED. STALE indicates the proposal no longer meets current basis/proof requirements, preserving its old decision. SUPERSEDED indicates an explicit replacement proposal. Task assignment/awaiting execution gate are separate work states, not payroll approval booleans. A rejected/stale unit gets a NEW proposal revision through correct_review_unit with exact predecessor CAS/reason and fresh basis; old submitter, decision and reason remain. A submitted proposal must first be conclusively cancelled or superseded under the same guard before changing actor/basis; competing old decide has one ordered winner.
When the last necessary decision commits, the payroll owner evaluates PC1.5 under the same transaction protocol and, if satisfied, appends one Completion receipt and sets new-format run APPROVED. No second human clicks 'approve what I cannot see'. The person's response is their unit/batch receipt; the global state or hidden count is returned only under separate current disclosure permission. If another unit is stale/incomplete, the valid local decision can commit while the run remains REVIEWING; no hidden error payload is returned to this reviewer. DB/transaction failure rolls the whole local command back.
Cancellation of the cycle requires a distinct scoped control Action, reason and expected cycle/head; cancels uncommitted work and returns the run to authorized preparation without deleting old units/receipts. Cancelling a whole run is not implied by editing one permitted unit. Once a Completion has made the run APPROVED, corrections use AC1 approved-draft successor, never partial mutation of the approved run. All successor units require fresh submission/decision under the new run identity; no transfer of old approval to a different run.

PC1.5 Exact coverage and common obligations
Completion predicate: current nonempty required set R is supported and reserved; each k in R has exactly one current proposal bound to the applicable current calculation/input facts, exactly one accepted decision with required independent S/D proof and configured gates; union of covered keys equals R and no key is outside R; no missing, duplicate, rejected, cancelled, stale or unresolved mandatory item; common source/rule/attendance/eligibility proofs and required Company-scoped obligations are valid. Equality of counts alone is insufficient. Reject malformed cover sets, not silently deduplicate them.
The common arithmetic validation is deterministic: sum the exact referenced unit results with checked integer arithmetic; verify required reconciliations, unit contribution membership and explicit source applicability. This does not grant a human permission to view the sum. If a business policy requires a human to review a whole-run total, budget or cross-unit constraint, add that explicit common decision obligation with its own required evidence and S/D/gate policy; an actor must be authorized to inspect that obligation. Where no such actor exists, the run cannot complete. Do not claim unit approvals prove a shared business judgment that nobody made. No mandatory new total approver is invented where policy does not require one.
For current supported kernel, unit arithmetic is per-person; eligibility/membership and source/rule policy still have common dependencies. Nonseparable group bonuses, cumulative shared caps, cross-Employment or other unsupported rules remain blocked under D04 until their dependency/decision contract is reviewed. No statistical privacy trick substitutes for a required business decision.
APPROVED means the exact immutable run received complete accepted coverage. Readiness for any NEW future effect rechecks current source/identity/policy validity; later revocation marks readiness stale/INTEGRITY_HOLD without rewriting the historical completion. Payment, issuance and legal clearance remain separately held; APPROVED never sets payable or dispatches funds.

PC1.6 Corrections without unnecessary repeated review
Before run approval, correct only the affected proposal and owner facts. A new cycle snapshot may reuse the SAME immutable approved proposal ID for an unchanged key in the SAME run only if payroll proves complete dependency equality: same employee/Employment/period and semantic unit, same local source and calculation value/version identity, same relevant shared dependencies, same required evidence/notes, rule/review contract, current composition eligibility under PC1.13; historical actors need not retain current work sessions or assignments. That proposal's original actor command remains bound to that proposal; reuse is a composition reference, not replaying it against a new proposal or minting another human approval.
Freeze a new cycle membership/revision and append an auditable reuse linkage after verification; never mutate old receipts. The parent cycle ID is not part of the unit business effect identity; run/Company/key/proposal identity IS. No reuse across runs, Companies, periods, changed required evidence or changed meaning. Identical displayed amount alone does not prove equivalence. If today's computation generates a new semantic calculation basis/version, the proposal is new even if the amount is coincidentally equal.
Use a source-derived dependency contract, not an LLM guess or manually asserted unchanged flag. Where an input/eligibility change can affect all units, invalidate all dependent proposals. If completeness of the dependency graph is unproven, conservatively re-review the affected cycle; do not promise isolated invalidation for that rule. Unrelated display labels/navigation edits are excluded from the signed effect basis. New required member gets its own proposal and prevents completion; removal requires the authorized scope/membership correction Action with reason/evidence and reservation reconciliation. Hiding an employee or editing a sidebar filter cannot remove an obligation.
This retention avoids unnecessary retyping/decisions when unchanged evidence is mechanically proven, while preserving an explicit fallback when proof is incomplete. It is not arbitrary portability of approvals between submissions. AC1 exact optional gates are never reusable for a different unit command.

PC1.7 Concurrency, current authority and command recovery
Use the existing same-Company PostgreSQL payroll owner transaction, strengthened by AI1.6 guard ordering. Each command is exact actor/action/payload bound and receipt-idempotent. Pre-plan the relevant current cycle/run revision and all actor/session/identity/relationship/policy/required source guard keys needed for the selected units AND possible completion. Take identity/Account/policy guards before payroll period reservation guard, then run head and unit heads sorted by IDs, source guards and governance consumption as prescribed. Validate the plan revision after locks; changed planned membership/proof set requires retry, never take an earlier-order lock after run lock. All writers that can change relied-on facts join their owner's guard protocol before activation.
For simplicity and proof at the 2000-person pilot, completion checks use a bounded whole-cycle proof scan, not incremental counters as sole authority. The command planner may load an upper bound of all current unit actor/proof keys plus the current actor, then verify no missing keys after acquiring run lock. Set-based reads/ordered row locks avoid per-employee network calls. This is O(number of cycle units and bounded referenced evidence) per batch, with no exponential permission combination or LLM call. Benchmark contention before admitting this path; do not claim a performance pass from the size bound alone.
User batch command maximum 100 explicit proposals in one Company/current cycle. All selected unit permissions and input versions are checked and its effects/gate consumptions commit all-or-none; never silently drop unauthorized items. Larger selections show deterministic chunks of at most 100 with stable per-chunk command IDs and receipts; no claim of all-chunk atomicity. Concurrent disjoint units preserve completed work even if a stale plan forces retry. Same-unit decisions have one current winner; duplicate same command returns the original receipt, different competing command conflicts.
At most 3 internal stale-plan/serialization retries with the SAME command identity and no committed effect; database owner execution deadline 5 seconds per attempt, bounded initial settings to validate under the pilot load, not measured SLOs. After exhaustion return retryable state preserving the user intent and server draft; never spin indefinitely, return success or mint another command. A committed response loss is resolved by current permitted receipt readback before new-effect guards as candidate02 D11. Unknown in-flight command stays unknown until serialized receipt/definitive rollback; absence alone is not proof of failure. No background worker impersonation is introduced.
Revocation racing a decision serializes on the same relied-on guards. After revocation commits, no new effect may rely on old proof. Final composition verifies current composition eligibility of all referenced completed decisions under PC1.13, distinct from current execution permission for NEW decisions. Explicitly invalidated ones require authorized re-review, but old receipts remain history. Account departure affects only its dependent assignments; lawful historical reads remain independently authorized. A deadlock/transaction error cannot leave half-consumed approval or a Completion without its exact cover.

PC1.8 Disclosure and useful work screens
A worklist is a current SSR projection of permitted tasks/objects/fields; no full roster is serialized and hidden client-side. The preparation worker sees editable source facts, blockers they may inspect, due date and next authorized Action. The decision worker sees their required evidence/deltas and actual S; they can approve/reject only the explicit inspected set. A Company coordinator may have permitted coverage status without individual amounts; summary/status existence itself requires policy. Neither coordinator nor policy administrator implicitly sees all salary.
A user without global progress permission sees only '내 검토 완료' for their completed set, never 'all 1,999 of 2,000', confidential-category names or the identity/timing of an outstanding hidden unit. Another actor may see a protected person's personnel card while salary task/evidence remains restricted. Counts, denominators, badges, error messages, timeline events, chat object cards, sum/difference filters and export obey AM1. Authorized counts alone do not authorize total amounts. Exact whole-run completion status is an explicitly released fact, not claimed to hide all possible inference from authorized facts.
Necessary restricted work routes to the currently eligible assignment through the same policy; the local worker need not discover who is confidential or obtain their pay. If assignment is unresolved, only an authorized routing operator sees enough metadata to resolve it, not automatic salary access. An unavailable necessary field is not zero or empty. Required human evidence that remains inaccessible blocks THAT monetary decision; source contributors still finish their independent authorized facts.
Detailed screen states and focus/receipt behavior are specified in screen-states.csv for this work only. Full application B07 and private draft storage B06 are still separate. Native forms reuse existing objects; 'open evidence' restores current authorized context, not a duplicate salary notebook.

Scalar legacy submitted_by/decided_by must not be filled with a service account or the last unit reviewer. Readers use the linked decision ledger for new-format attribution. No reinterpretation of existing historical approvals as if they had unit-level proof.
Before enabling UNIT_COVERAGE effects, all reachable submit/decide/withdraw/reopen/calculate/restage/cancel/supersede/payable/issue/list/exception/aggregate/readback paths must recognize the current cycle/membership fence or be unavailable for this format. Existing org-wide feature gates remain source reality but cannot be the completed target for scoped actors; add exact resource/action/property policy at the owner boundary, preserve Company RLS and remove title-based authority only through a separately reviewed compatibility transition. Shared UI/REST use cases must prevent direct legacy POST from approving a new-format run.
Add payroll-owned cycle/proposal/decision/coverage/reservation records with same-Company FK closure and append-only history. Review keys refer to existing immutable calculation/input facts; no second wage writer. Preserve every existing test; revised semantic tests require explicit independent test-SHA review. Account/actor-FK changes depend on candidate03; no new migration file or schema number is allocated here.
Expand schema/readers/guards, validate real FK and route closure, then enable new-format writes. After first new-format record, rollback disables writes while retaining coverage-aware reads/history and all safety fences. It cannot downgrade to a single fake decision or release overlapping reservations without a recorded withdrawal/correction. Current source has no demonstrated unit protocol; every positive acceptance must traverse owning Actions and real persistence after approved design/test gates.

Complete-source input collection, performance and FK/route closure still require implementation proof; B03 Group durable dispatch, B04 general automatic reassignment, B06 server drafts, remaining B07 screens and B08 statement publication/disputes remain open. PC1.11 Common obligations use the same proposal/decision protocol
The policy/source-contract owner defines immutable obligation definitions with stable IDs and versions, required review-evidence fields/attestations, scope/dependency selector and decision/gate/SoD rules. The payroll owner derives the exact required common set C while opening/revising the ReviewCycle; the initiator cannot remove requirements by submitting a smaller list. In this slice an optional common obligation may be review of the exact payroll total/reconciliation. References to a budget are illustrative only if an existing approved owner source supplies it; PC1 does not implement finance/budget management.
Common proposal key is (Company,run_id,obligation_definition_id,obligation_definition_version,governed_scope_id). Its immutable revision binds the exact selected unit calculation/source dependency map, derived total/reconciliation result if required, applicable common-source/rule/eligibility versions and review-evidence contract. Any change in those dependencies creates a new proposal revision; equal total alone cannot preserve it. A cycle includes exactly one current proposal per required common key, with no duplicate/missing/extra substitute accepted. Do not bind a common proposal to future human decision IDs and create a circular dependency: its review basis is the exact business evidence; composition separately checks individual decisions.
Expose the same owner submit_review_proposal and decide_review_proposal use cases for Unit and Common targets; the unit routes in PC1.4 are adapters restricted to the Unit target, not a second writer. A common-work task supplies its typed proposal ID, expected basis, attestation and optional exact gate just like a unit. Common S and D must be distinct verified humans; additional independence relative to unit actors exists only when explicitly required by current policy. Whoever approves a common monetary fact must have its required evidence permission; a unit-only reviewer cannot infer that capability. One actor can hold several separately authorized assignments; titles do not select target type.
A Common decision arriving LAST invokes the SAME owner-atomic completion evaluator as a Unit decision. Completion requires exact covers of BOTH R and C, not just all employee units approved. Common rejection/staleness opens that obligation's correction task and leaves the run REVIEWING; it does not erase valid unrelated unit decisions. A prior common proposal can be referenced in a new cycle only through the same full dependency-equality rule as PC1.6. UI labels name the specific authorized obligation ('급여 합계 검토' when disclosable), not a misleading second whole-run approval that silently consumes all unit authority.

PC1.12 Explicit correction/recalculation/cycle activation
Owning operation revise_review_cycle is payroll-owned, allowed only for new-format run REVIEWING with no CoverageCompletion or external/payable effect. Inputs: command_id, exact affected proposal refs/expected revisions, typed source-correction refs and reason; scope/membership changes additionally require their distinct authorized control Action. The server plans the current cycle/head and dependency set internally. A scoped actor never receives the hidden roster/global version just to supply an expected-head token. Require current permission to correct the selected unit/obligation and invoke the narrow owner recalculation; no permission to edit hidden unrelated inputs follows.
Source corrections happen through their existing source owners with their own command receipts; this operation references committed source revisions and NEVER writes wage/Employment/attendance through a review table. If a source correction has committed but cycle revision has not, old affected proposals are stale under live source guards and cannot finalize. Preserve the source result and retry cycle revision through its original command; there is no false cross-owner atomicity promise.
Within one Company payroll transaction and PC1.7 lock order: validate old current cycle/head and affected-proposal CAS; verify exact authoritative membership, current close/period validity and supported-case applicability; derive the conservative dependency-affected set; create the next immutable input/calculation snapshot map; recalculate affected supported units using the existing payroll domain build_line_calculation kernel and owner persistence primitive; leave unchanged line calculation IDs/versions physically unchanged; create new DRAFT proposals for changed Unit/Common bases; validate any reused proposal references; append the new immutable cycle containing complete R/C maps; CAS-advance the current cycle/run revision; invoke the same atomic completion evaluator over the newly activated R/C; if all PC1.5/PC1.13 predicates pass, append one Completion and set APPROVED with this revision command as causal command, otherwise remain REVIEWING; append one command receipt listing only caller-permitted changes. All payroll-side changes commit or all roll back. No second arithmetic implementation or invocation of existing whole-run calculate_run_in_tx unchanged.
The new snapshot map may reference unchanged existing line versions from the prior cycle plus new affected line versions; source/calculation unit semantic bindings remain exact. A changed whole-input-revision ID alone is not a claim that every unit's facts changed. New calculation or review basis for an affected key invalidates its old proposal even if its net amount matches. Any common obligation depending on changed inputs is recreated. If selective dependency completeness is unproven, conservatively calculate/review the complete dependent set using the same owner kernel. Changes that violate required attendance close, effective-period scope or supported kernel cannot activate a revised cycle; show the owner correction/close prerequisite, retain history and refuse stale completion.
Old-cycle decisions racing activation share the run/cycle fence. A decision that commits first remains an historical effect; activation carries it forward only if that exact proposal is still current and dependency-equal. If revision wins first, a request targeting a replaced proposal is stale; a still-current unchanged proposal may execute after server re-plan/current guard validation without asking the user to reapprove hidden global metadata. Finalization always references the locked CURRENT cycle and its complete R/C, so an old plan cannot finish a new cycle. If an old-cycle last decision makes the run APPROVED first, revision fails state/conflict and any correction must use AC1 successor instead.
Rejected/stale unit correction uses this operation, not legacy REJECTED->CALCULATED withdraw. Reassignment without business-basis change follows task assignment and pending-intent rules; it does not regenerate salary facts. Cancellation/reprepare remains the fallback when prerequisites cannot be preserved and requires explicit control authority; it never unlocks attendance or deletes approved history.

PC1.13 Completed decisions survive ordinary assignment/session endings
A committed unit/common approval is a completed business decision, not an unexecuted permit or a pending worker intention. Preserve its authorization-at-execution evidence and S/D attribution. A subsequent ordinary role expiry, transfer, logout or employment termination does not by itself undo it, force re-login by that former actor, or require that actor's old work permissions to remain active for another person's cycle completion. New submit/decide/gate execution ALWAYS requires the current actor's live authority. Pending unexecuted gates/intents remain subject to current revocation as candidate03 specifies.
Composition checks that the completed decision still covers the current business basis, has not been explicitly withdrawn/invalidated, and has no current identity-integrity defect. A later identity merge showing S and D were the same human, fraudulent identity finding, explicit source revocation or changed basis invalidates its contribution and may create INTEGRITY_HOLD. Mere change of proof version is not automatically a fraudulent completed decision; re-resolve through the identity owner to distinguish equivalent valid proof from contradiction/insufficient assurance. If necessary integrity cannot be established, do not fabricate coverage. No disclosure of former actor's credentials/profile is needed to verify the stored proof.
Before whole-run Completion, an authorized withdraw_review_decision Action may append a withdrawal/invalidation with exact decision/proposal CAS, reason/evidence and current policy/required gate; it never edits the old receipt. It supersedes that proposal with an unsubmitted revision under the current-cycle protocol, preserving basis references where valid and requiring fresh S/D. After whole-run APPROVED, corrections/integrity holds follow AC1 and release guards; unit withdrawal cannot silently reopen it. This explicit business revocation is distinct from removing an assignment.
The owner proof planner locks current actors' session/authorization guards for their new command and appropriate stored identity-integrity/revocation guards for earlier completed decisions. It must not mistake inactive historical actors' Account sessions for a missing current approval. This reconciles PC1's live checks with AM1/EC1 account continuity and preserves useful work after a colleague leaves.

PC1.14 Already-covered revisions and anti-omission guard
Cycle activation must evaluate completion even when no new human decision is needed. Example: R previously included approved U1 and rejected U2; an authorized and evidence-valid scope/membership correction removes U2; retained U1 still exactly covers new nonempty R and C is empty. If reservation/current basis/integrity and all other predicates hold, revise_review_cycle itself appends the Completion atomically. It records an owner-derived composition caused by the correction command, not a new human approval or a falsely pending task. The same rule applies whenever revised R/C already has complete valid evidence.
Removal is never a shortcut around a rejected confidential obligation. The owner must independently establish the new authoritative scope/membership facts and the caller's exact scope-amendment authority plus any required review/gate. Mere row filtering, no disclosure permission, absence of an assignee or a caller-supplied exclusion reason is insufficient to remove an eligible key. A genuinely narrowed payroll scope remains labeled as that narrower scope; it cannot produce Company-wide completion or release another required payroll scope's obligations. Empty revised R is not a completed payroll; use an explicit authorized cancellation outcome.
