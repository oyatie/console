B04 next design brief — OPEN; not an accepted assignment contract

Goal / finite boundary
A Company/Site lacks a local person; another Company's department supplies authorized staff. When the current person becomes unavailable, work-bound inputs and context reach one eligible successor per atomic task without the absent person manually finding coverage. The successor can continue without inheriting the old person's identity, sensitive fields, or approvals. Begin with payroll preparation/review tasks and candidate07 calculate attempts; no general optimization platform or complete HR absence implementation in this packet.

Proposed decisions to carry into the B04 candidate
1. Canonical task ownership stays with workflow. Common ontology revisions expose its governed projection and linked drafts, not a second task writer. Shared service coverage is a governed target-Company relation to Account or a composite provider Company/OrgUnit. Coverage is candidate eligibility evidence, not a grant. Define exact application ports and Group-owned capacity metadata only after tracing existing reusable owners; do not add a standalone scheduler service by default.
2. One atomic task has one accountable current assignee or an explicit unassigned exception. Queue supervision is a separate responsibility and cannot pretend the business task has been assigned. A task that has genuinely separable inputs becomes distinct linked tasks with exact completion coverage. 'Partial sharing' is not an assignment mode. Temporary/permanent transfer concerns responsibility duration; return must be a new guarded transition, never resurrection of a prior epoch.
3. Eligibility is conjunctive: current target scope/policy, required disclosed/reviewable fields, natural-person independence for the particular decision, typed qualification, permitted coverage, availability interval and capacity. Job title/seniority/local Employment does not supply privilege. Eligibility for future work does not require a continuously logged-in session; actual new human effects still require current session/authority under AI1. A background assignment workload principal has narrow routing authority only.
4. Start with deterministic rule filtering and stable ordering, with authorized preselection before capacity/qualification ordering; no LLM inference from notes. Evaluate the simplest bounded heuristic before min-cost matching/constraint solvers. Use a solver only if explicit constraints and observed failures show the heuristic cannot meet declared acceptance. Unknown qualification/capacity must have a typed handling rule; never quietly assume available or qualified. Define candidate enumeration completeness so page1 exhaustion is not 'no eligible candidate'.
5. Capacity across multiple Companies cannot use independently stale 'free slot' reads. Compare (a) Group-owned narrow Account capacity reservation with a fenced prepare/commit/release protocol and (b) preallocated per-Company quotas whose sum fits the Account budget. Option(a) supports dynamic shared services; option(b) simplifies coordination but may strand capacity. B04 must choose and specify one for the pilot, including reservation timeouts/unknown commits/reconciliation and nondisclosing counts. Account workload metadata may itself reveal confidential work; aggregate computation needs explicit workload authority and human views remain separately filtered.
6. Model absence as typed availability interval/change references. Routing needs availability, not medical narrative. Scheduled leave, extension, cancellation, unexpected absence and return should produce durable deduplicated reassessment. Coverage failure raises an operational exception; it is not leave denial. Do not infer unauthorized absence or discipline from a missing clock event. Those domain facts require their own owner workflow.
7. Assignment changes fence old-tab saves/executes using assignment and editing epochs. EDITABLE work draft follows only through the existing participating owner transaction. READY old-actor attempt becomes terminal no-effect under the same fence before a new actor explicitly seals a successor. SUCCEEDED is historical readback. UNKNOWN is reconciled before any successor effect; absence or session revocation cannot make it safe to repeat. Already completed decisions retain original attribution.
8. A Group-prepared child cannot be individually edited/cancelled/reassigned around GO1. Parent STOPPED and confirmed no-effect cleanup precede a successor operation over the affected explicit subset; successful children remain historical outcomes. Need exact ordering for parent cancel vs Company assignment fence vs in-flight receipt, without holding a Company lock while waiting for the reverse Group lock. Produce deadlock and lost-ACK traces, not just lock names.
9. Automatic handover must carry the task's linked work context, needed evidence and saved permitted fields. Mail/thread/document links keep their own policy; a preview or assignment never shares a restricted payload. Required hidden input blocks that actor's decision and routes the precise work requirement; no blind signature. Work-related versus Account-private custody is explicit before absence. Staff notes are surfaced at the relevant step; responsibility/qualification are never inferred solely from prose.
10. No eligible person: durable task exception visible to authorized supervisors, next permitted action to repair coverage/capacity or request a scoped policy change, and manual exact-person reassignment using the same validation. No emergency title/superuser bypass. If nobody is authorized to manage coverage, emit a restricted configuration failure to the established administrative recovery channel, not the business payload. Define bootstrap owner admission and how that channel is itself governed.
