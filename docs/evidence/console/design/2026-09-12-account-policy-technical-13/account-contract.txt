AS1 — Account security and Company acting context

AS1.1 Existing behavior and reuse
Current users are Company-scoped. signup_open_member inserts KNL+MEMBER. load_user_auth_context_tx needs Company users/is_active/nonempty roles. AccessClaims carries mandatory org/roles; ordinary request-context copies freshness without enforcing a global Account generation. WebAuthn and refresh lookup credential/token->Company then use Company RLS. identity deactivate_user deletes passkeys and revokes refresh families; employer admin OTP/reset can obtain sign-in power. Two passkey-delete handlers each count then delete without one Account lock. Registration terms acceptance is currently Company audit-backed. Company purge includes direct credential/user deletes and a catalog-driven FK sweep. All are material migration surfaces, not reasons to discard existing cryptography.
Reuse platform/auth ES256 verification/issuance, WebAuthn verifier/ceremonies, refresh families/rotation/reuse detection and absolute lifetime. Identity owns Account lifecycle and CompanyActor attribution; platform/auth retains credential protocol/SQL responsibilities. Provisioning retains enrollment orchestration and must invoke Account-aware owners. Consolidate duplicate key-list/delete route adapters onto one owner use case. Company relationship deactivation is separated from the explicit identity-security suspend/recovery use case. No new authentication service, fake Company user or employee identity at signup.

AS1.2 Account root and security custody
schema-candidate.json fixes proposed tables/keys and required access boundaries. accounts is Company-independent immutable identity(id,created_at); UUIDs from users map unchanged. account_security is its mandatory guard: account_id, security_state, security_generation positive BIGINT, revision and updated_at. States PENDING_ENROLLMENT, ACTIVE, SECURITY_SUSPENDED, RECOVERY_REQUIRED describe identity security only. A business relationship state or job title cannot mutate them. Existing legacy users remain Company profile/provenance, never the credential owner. No inferred merge of legacy accounts from email/name; high-risk human equivalence remains Group-scoped AI1 registry.
Retain auth table names and credential IDs/user handles, serialized passkey keys/counters and refresh hashes. Existing user_id fields can keep their physical name during transition but point to accounts. Remove BOTH users(id) and composite(user_id,org_id)->users actor FKs from account-owned auth tables after validated replacement; org_id becomes nullable legacy-origin provenance and ceases to authorize access. New accounts and credentials require no Company, role or Employment. Retained provenance cannot create a cascade from employer deletion. Token rows must reference a family with the SAME Account, not merely an existing family UUID.
Choose a dedicated restricted database role/pool console_auth_rt for credential and pre-auth custody in the same application/PostgreSQL deployment. Company business console_rt loses direct credential/refresh/ceremony/bootstrap DML and cannot SET ROLE to console_auth_rt or its owner. This is database least-privilege separation, not a different login audience for workers/applicants. Auth pool has only required owner functions/table verbs, NOSUPERUSER/NOBYPASSRLS and no arbitrary Company business data grants. Table owner is a non-login migration owner; function search_path is fixed, identifiers qualified, PUBLIC grants revoked. Existing permissive Company credential RLS policies must be removed, not OR-combined with Account checks. Account-scoped predicates are defense in depth for authenticated self-service; narrow pre-auth routines use opaque proof lookup and return only needed verifier material to auth owner, never public key-state blobs through a business helper.
Company transaction uses identity-owned read-and-lock helpers for bounded Account/security/session/CompanyActor proof; no passkey_json, refresh hash, reset token, email or other-Company grants are returned. These helpers are callable by the participating transaction role and take a server-verified Account/session context. Client-selected IDs/GUCs are scope selectors, not authentication. The application authentication boundary remains trusted; this contract does not claim protection against a compromised auth service or DBA. Ordinary Company administrators, endpoint callers and business SQL grants cannot become credential administrators.

AS1.3 One explicitly versioned Account session
Choose ACCOUNT_V1 with aud=console.account.v1 and token_kind=account_v1. Reuse configured issuer, configured PEM-backed ES256 signing/verifying keys and existing algorithm/issuer/audience/time validation; no existing JWKS implementation is claimed. Claims: iss,aud,sub(AccountId),sid(existing refresh-family UUID),jti,iat,nbf,exp,security_generation,auth_time,assurance. No org/roles/home Company/employee/persona. Existing Company-bearing JWT remains a distinct legacy parser/audience path for unmigrated subjects only; no sentinel org, nil UUID, default roles or treating missing org as platform authority. A token cannot select its parser by unverified fields: cryptographic validation and exact accepted audience/kind combination precede trusted interpretation.
Account cutover is global authentication enrollment, independent of Company policy/data enrollment. Before enabling a migrated Account, every successful legacy authentication path must check the authoritative Account/security root: if that subject has an account_security row, reject all legacy access and refresh tokens and require fresh Account primary authentication. Backfill may create this row in a non-ACTIVE state; its existence is an irreversible legacy-authentication fence, not a state-dependent exception. Refusal applies even to non-enrolled Company data, after recovery to ACTIVE, and during rollback. A legacy token has no provable sid/generation: never infer its family from jti, fill missing generation from the current row, or convert an employer-origin session into ACCOUNT_V1. Legacy refresh families/bootstrap credentials are revoked/refused at cutover; preserved key bytes may be used for a fresh verified primary login. Install this global resolver/refusal on HTTP, SSR/server functions and any alternate authenticated transports before backfill activation; an unclosed route prevents Account enrollment. Missing/unavailable migration lookup is denial, never permission to fall back to the old parser. Unmigrated compatibility may continue only through its separately guarded old authentication path.
Refresh family is the durable session; add protocol, Account generation at primary authentication, auth_time and assurance metadata. Do not add a duplicate sessions table. Resolver validates Account ACTIVE, exact generation equality, family Account/protocol, unrevoked family, token expiry, absolute family lifetime and any required action assurance. Higher token generation is invalid too; missing rows are denial, not0 baseline. Refresh never silently extends primary-authentication age or upgrades assurance; retain rotation, used-token detection and family revocation semantics. Global suspend/security recovery bumps Account generation and revokes required sessions under the Account guard. Company grant or Employment termination does not globally log the Account out; current Company policy makes new work fail independently.
Initial Account access TTL is at most15minutes, bounded by existing configured absolute session lifetime; refresh retains existing tighter expiry/rotation limits. This is a proposed session bound, not a claim current config equals15minutes. Short JWT TTL does not replace live Account/family checks. Assurance values are typed owner results (e.g. passkey primary / exact action step-up), never form booleans; high-risk actions require their own current assurance and AI1 human proofs. Primary authentication does not certify two different humans.
AccountPrincipal is constructed without a Company. resolve_actor_context(AccountPrincipal, typed resource selector, registered action) returns AccountScope, CompanyScope or GroupControlScope. This is a resource-context type, not different principals for applicants/former workers. A requested Company/header/path is an untrusted selector; resolver verifies actual object Company, current disclosed context and policy. It arms app.current_org only for CompanyScope. Group consolidated reads are independently authorized Company owner calls; Group context never arms Company RLS or grants salary read. New domain routes must use this resolver, not coerce AccountPrincipal into legacy role-bearing Principal.
No generic context-switch endpoint issues broader work tokens. Account navigation must expose only current authorized contexts; choosing one does not grant actions. Its proposed GET me/contexts discovery/continuation API is explicitly deferred and not among this packet's specified routes: W04b-04 must settle candidate discovery, current membership/revocation freshness and Account-owned continuation without Company custody. There is no implied usable no-pagination endpoint or truncation fallback; this remains a blocker before integrated design approval. Applicant own-case and former-worker own-record entitlements are current scoped owner proofs. Their actions may ensure a CompanyActor through the narrow AI1 owner helper, but need no present Employment or workforce grant. Historical receipt disclosure remains independent of current execution eligibility under TX1.

AS1.4 Enrollment, keys, terms and recovery
Account-only registration uses a server-created pending Account and single-use, short-lived WebAuthn ceremony bound to that Account, browser enrollment nonce and current Account terms version. A client cannot choose another Account to enroll or present an employer invite as authentication. Store/compare only protected nonce hashes; bind browser flow against CSRF, origin and RP-ID as the existing WebAuthn owner requires. Pending enrollment produces no business token. Finishing verified key registration and terms acceptance under Account lock activates the pending Account and creates its session; a used/expired/mismatched ceremony cannot replay into a different Account. Existing-account new-key enrollment still requires existing-key proof/current step-up, not bearer possession alone.
Migrate required terms/consent evidence from Company audit lookup into account_terms_acceptances and account_security_events. Preserve exact accepted version/content digest/evidence time where reconstructable; unknown historic version requires fresh acceptance, not fabricated consent. Do not put Account-wide credential/security audit into an employer-visible audit feed. Business audit retains the Account/CompanyActor attribution permitted in that Company.
Every registration, login key-counter update, refresh, key deletion, logout, global suspend/reset/recovery and device handoff that mutates security state acquires account_security first, then sorted involved session families/tokens, then key/ceremony/handoff rows in documented deterministic key order. Preliminary opaque lookup may identify the Account; recheck ownership under locks before writing. Claiming ceremony before Account lock must be reordered. Two last-key deletes serialize through account_security; floor validation uses current keys plus explicitly verified recovery capability, never a client claim. Preserve existing stricter last-key guard unless a separately approved recovery design supplies an alternative. No auth transaction acquires Company/group locks after these security locks; emit security events, then let future independently authorized work resolve Company context.
Employer admin OTP/reset and deactivate/delete aliases must lose credential authority before Account enrollment. Existing outstanding employer-minted bootstrap tokens are revoked or remain blocked for migrated Accounts; changing the label to invitation cannot leave redeem issuing normal token pairs. Invitations bind a proposed relationship to a currently authenticated recipient and issuer scope/expiry; they are neither account passwords nor proof of representing an external organization. Normal Account recovery needs existing user factors or supervised identity-security recovery with evidence and independent authority, never Company UserManage. Legacy-deleted keys cannot be restored from metadata; RECOVERY_REQUIRED preserves prior denial while providing Account recovery independent of employment.

AS1.5 Company attribution and high-risk human proof
company_actors PK(org_id,account_id) supplies immutable attribution with admission receipt and entitlement/source reference. Anchor existence grants no access. Identity admission or an explicit owner-verified own-case/engagement action can ensure only the exact authenticated actor's target anchor; no public ensure-any-UUID endpoint. Actor-only FKs point here, global audit/security actor FKs to accounts, while employee/Employment ownership FKs remain Company domain keys. Retain anchors after grant expiry/Company archive for history.
AI1 Group human proof uses group_human_subjects, append-only group_identity_binding_revisions and current binding heads with uniqueness/CAS, plus supervised root receipt. schema-candidate fixes key/check semantics. Selected pilot verification registry supplies opaque stable subject identifiers; different UUIDs/email/IdP account IDs alone are not proof of different humans. Propose and verify require different already-verified humans and the exact current case/proof/retention references, neither self-verification nor recursive bootstrap. Initial two operators come from the separately authorized out-of-band root receipt; it grants no payroll permission. Account aliases/canonical Company Person refs may resolve to one human; no Person/Employment row is rewritten by identity owner.
Group registry mutation acquires Group guard EXCLUSIVE, then sorted Account/security and target policy guards needed by the operation; new effects use Group SHARE. Current proof/version equality at decision detects later merges/splits/revocations; conflicting bindings suspend affected pending assurances. Security-only Account suspension takes only its earlier Account subset and never waits for Group. Scoped permission loss does not delete Account or immutable human/actor evidence. Raw identity artifact storage/custody and qualified operational review are separate required owner inputs; this packet defines references, not actual verified people or a legal identity provider.
