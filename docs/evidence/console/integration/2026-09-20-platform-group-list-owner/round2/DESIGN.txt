## Bounded outcome and rationale

Converge GET/HEAD /api/platform/groups on the existing PlatformProvisioner::list_groups owner. This is the next concrete workspace Group discovery dependency: its topology is cross-Company and its existing caller still authorizes with the unconditional PlatformPrincipal::authorize. Reuse the now-demonstrated Company/ops original-credential, retained-source and finite-Cedar read contract. Preserve native Group/Company identity and the exact existing bare-array wire shape. No new Group ObjectKey or second roadmap is needed.

Source facts: the mounted handler passes only optional actor and host time to an owner that immediately runs platform_list_groups(). Current platform middleware parses current roles but discards the set; MEMBER is a valid parsed role. Existing GroupManage policy instead requires current SUPER_ADMIN. The current owner cannot verify original credentials or selected-family revocation because those inputs are absent. A current-MEMBER disclosure and audit is therefore a source-supported hypothesis; only a real owner-bound probe may establish RED. There is no current release-qualified native Group UI claim in this assessment. Exact references and caller census are in CENSUS.md.

The sole HTTP caller supplies the exact raw Bearer header and existing server-composed dependencies. No caller actor, clock, feature, target, tenant identifier, detached policy permit or Principal grants authority. No alternate unauthenticated listing helper may remain public. Existing Company/ops owning signatures remain byte-compatible.

One explicit READ COMMITTED Business transaction uses live_legacy_platform_source_in_tx unchanged and retains its established Company KEY SHARE → User UPDATE → Account UPDATE → exact selected family SHARE guards through projection, mapping, audit and COMMIT. The fixed decision tuple is Direct / GroupManage / Groups / ReadProjection / DirectPlatformOwner. Existing Cedar/schema and source bridge already admit that tuple; their bytes, generation, fingerprints and truth-table contracts remain unchanged. Historical unbound credentials may read only while original signed source remains valid; an invalid present binding never falls back. Current roles are decided by Cedar, not a Rust role check. This transaction provides retained authorization; it does not promise immutable topology across requests.

Obtain fresh actual DB time and recheck the retained source/current policy (a) after source acquisition and before platform_list_groups SQL; (b) immediately after that SQL and before mapping; (c) after mapping and immediately before constructing/inserting audit; (d) immediately after audit INSERT and before COMMIT. Lost/ambiguous acknowledgement is unavailable with no projection and no automatic retry; explicit caller recovery is a separate read, which may produce a separate audit.
