This absence is an obligation for future enrollment, not concurrency proof.

Use a **deactivation-specific identity guard inside the existing Business transaction**. Do not add an Auth-pool precheck or hold a separate Auth connection's lock across Business commit. Separate-connection lock loss/crash can release the Account lock while the independent Business transaction still commits; application cancellation is not atomic rollback.
