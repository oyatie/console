# Human inputs

This record tracks facts and decisions that only the service operator can supply. It is not a product roadmap or release approval. [README.md](README.md) remains the entry point; the current [delivery authority](docs/current/DELIVERY.md) and release inventory determine acceptance.

These inputs are deferred to the final preparation of real enrollment documents. They do not block implementation, isolated testing, or workflow verification. Missing values must not be replaced with invented names or contacts, and fixture documents must not be published as client enrollment content.

| Input | What to provide | Needed by | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service operator | Registered legal name of the company or individual business operating Console; the product name alone is insufficient identification. | Finalize the service terms and privacy notice before real client enrollment. | Pending; deferred. |
| Privacy contact | A monitored privacy email address; actual responsible person or role and other contact details if designated. | Finalize the privacy notice and verify the contact works. | Pending; deferred. |
| Support contact | A monitored address for account access, recovery, and service assistance; it may be the same address as the privacy contact. | Finalize the service terms and verify the assistance path works. | Pending; deferred. |
| Final enrollment documents | Operator review of the completed, versioned terms and privacy notice with these factual details and the implemented service behavior. | Before publishing those versions for real consent. | Pending; defer until the product and document drafts are ready. |

The service operator is separate from customer Companies created inside Console for employment and payroll. Standard Korean forms supply document structure, not the operator's identity, contacts, or a legal conclusion about the assembled service.

When an input is supplied, record the actual value or the versioned document containing it, the confirmation date, and the remaining work. Keep credentials and other secrets out of this file. Completing this checklist does not establish product qualification or authorize production exposure, production database execution, or direct payments; those decisions remain separate under the current authorities.
